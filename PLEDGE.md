# The AEGIS pledge

**This work belongs to everyone.** It is our shared history and our shared universe, written as an equation. The code
is free under the GNU AGPL-3.0 and the science is free under CC BY 4.0. There are no fees, no NDAs and no exclusive
deals, and the copyright will never be sold.

## No weapons
I ask that nothing here ever be used for weapons, war or the military-industrial complex, for mass surveillance, or to
harm or exclude anyone. Open licenses cannot legally forbid this, so it is a pledge and a request, made openly. If you
use this work, I ask you to honour it.

## Every person matters
Every race, religion, colour and creed. We are all human, and we all deserve dignity.

## Supporting the work (optional)
Nothing is required. If you want to help, cite the work, check it, improve it, or teach it. Any future donations
will go to keeping this research open and to my family's basic needs, and I hope to place them in a public trust for
science and education.

— Danny Lee Eldridge
