## Corrections to v1

1. **"Every data block fits better than ΛCDM" does not apply to the baseline.** With ε fitted to n_s = 0.9736, the baseline (w = −1) fits as well as ΛCDM with n_s free, by construction (Δχ² ≈ 0). The quoted block gains (CMB −2.77, BAO −0.41, SNe −2.48) belong to a thawing background (w₀ = −0.956), which lowers χ² by a further 2.2 at the cost of one parameter (ΔAIC ≈ −0.15). That background would need the separate single-axion variant, not the baseline. ε was fitted after the crown test failed (D410, then D419).
2. **Not every number was pre-registered.** Leptogenesis (D394, D411) and the post-capture α estimate (D417) are unlocked estimates. The phrase "every computation was pre-registered" is corrected accordingly.
3. **Vacuum energy (D427).** After the usual one-off cancellation of the vacuum energy, the leftover x-dependent piece is the cliff value e^{−2n} at x_E. That cancellation must also absorb the χ-condensate energy, 10²⁶–10³² GeV⁴ (10⁷²–10⁷⁹ ρ_Λ). The depth n ≈ 124.1 is chosen to match ρ_Λ. Λ is insensitive to the exact capture point (Λ ∝ x_E^{−0.97}), and that is the only sense in which it "needs no tuning".
4. **Extra radiation and gravitino (D422, D430, D474).** ΔN_eff ≈ 0.02–0.06, from the gravitino only, not 0.05–0.10. The x decay products are heavy (5×10⁵–10⁹ GeV) and decay to leptons, so the hidden-pair 0.034 is withdrawn. The gravitino window is m₃/₂ ≈ 1.7–4.7 eV (√F ≈ 85–140 TeV), bounded below as well as above.
5. **Birefringence ratio (D428).** ρ_β = β_reio/β_rec ≲ 0.8 for m_a ≳ 30–40 H₀, and ≈ 0 for m_a ≥ 100 H₀. For m_a ≲ 20 H₀ it is indistinguishable from a calibration error. This replaces "< 1 if m_a ≳ 20 H₀".
6. **What the Route A tests test (D434, D464, D475).** Route A's band (n_s 0.9706–0.9766, r 0.0042–0.0050, ε 2.0–4.0×10⁻⁷) separates it from plain Starobinsky. It does not separate it from fibre inflation or α ≈ 2 attractors, so r, α_s and B1 do not single out Twist-2. What is specific to Twist-2 lies in its post-inflation predictions (ΔN_eff, gravitino window, ρ_β). "Tiny" deformation: ε/x* ≈ 2.5×10⁻³ at the CMB point.
7. **String control (D450–D453).** Read as a volume modulus, the field is at sub-string volume throughout inflation. Rescaling to restore 𝒱 ≈ 1–3 at the CMB point reduces the KK margin after inflation to m_KK/T ≈ 3–6. Control is marginal at best.

   - "Starobinsky-class inflation" holds only undeformed; Route A has α_eff ≈ 2.2.
   - The α-drift exclusion in §1 is conditional on the assumptions of 10.5281/zenodo.24232382 (v2) and is stated as a factor ≳ 10³–10⁵ in ζ, not "~10⁶".
   - "x ≈ 3 at matter freeze-out" refers to the dropped v5 matter mechanism and is removed.
   - Leptogenesis window: ~7×10⁸–10¹³ GeV (D454).
   - Dark matter must be stable by a gauged symmetry (D473).
   - "CMB-S4" is removed (cancelled).
   - Added prior art: fibre inflation (0808.0691, 1603.06789), D-term fixing and trapping (1110.3333, 1310.4751), exponentially small Λ (2107.09064, 2107.09065).
