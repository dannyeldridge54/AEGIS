## Corrections to the v5 text (not covered by the v5.3 errata)

1. **Late-time locked tests apply to an excluded variant.** The tail tests (WATCH-PURE-DR3, WA-CEILING, GROWTH, SHIELD, LAB-NULLS) apply only to the thawing-tail variant. At one loop that variant predicts d ln α_em/d ln x ≈ 7×10⁻³. Existing clock and MICROSCOPE bounds exclude this unless the Standard Model is decoupled by a mechanism not known in any string construction (D323–D334, D400). Of the locked tests, only those on r, n_s and β remain relevant. In the abstract, "the tail's w(z)" was locked after the TAIL-F failure (Δχ² = +29.2), for scoring on DESI DR3 and Euclid; it is not an a-priori prediction.
2. **n_s distances (§3.2).** Against CMB-SPA (0.9684 ± 0.0030, arXiv:2507.12459) the Twist (n_s = 0.9648, N* = 56.7, D387) is 1.2σ low. It is 3.0σ low against CMB-SPA + DESI DR2 (0.9728 ± 0.0027) and 2.8σ low against P-ACT-LB (0.9743 ± 0.0034). The main-text values −1.7σ and −3.7σ used the wrong reference values.
3. **Inflation numbers.** The N* = 57.6 run is withdrawn. On the full trajectory N* = 56.7, n_s = 0.9648, r = 0.0037 and α_s ≈ −6.2×10⁻⁴. Instant reheating gives N* = 55.7, n_s = 0.9642, r = 0.0038. Every 57.6-based number in the abstract, §2.4, §3.1 and §6 is replaced by these (D387, D391).
4. **NS-SPA margin.** The rule fixed n_s = 0.964, the low end of the band. That costs Δχ² = 11.7 against the locked threshold of 9 (INCONSISTENT, as scored). At the current n_s = 0.9648 the cost is about 10 (quadratic estimate, not a rescoring). The tension is driven by CMB + BAO jointly; against the CMB alone it is about 1σ. The rule was written with the stored ΛCDM fit in view, so it quantifies a known tension rather than being a blind test.
5. **Prior art.** Quintessential inflation in no-scale supergravity, with kination, instant preheating and the residual potential as dark energy, was constructed in arXiv:2008.09099. The present model differs in the cliff construction and the locked test record, not in the overall structure. Also relevant: Ellis–Nanopoulos–Olive (arXiv:1307.3537), Choi (hep-ph/9902292), Dimopoulos (hep-ph/0702018).
6. **"Derived" axial coupling.** c_A = √(3/8) follows once the Nieh–Yan current is taken to couple to the canonically normalised angle, which is a stated choice. The alternative reading changes the matter yield by a factor of 0.44 (v5.3 errata item 2).
7. **Smaller items.**
   - §4.4: the CMB-inferred H₀ is 66.8, not 65.7 (full fit; ΛCDM 68.0; D332).
   - §5.2: the heavy-gravitino discussion is superseded by an eV gravitino, later narrowed to 1.7–4.7 eV (D430, D474).
   - §6: "CMB-S4 class" is removed (CMB-S4 was cancelled). SO reaches r ≈ 0.0036 at about 3σ by ~2035.
   - "n_s cannot be raised inside the model" holds only without changing W or K (Route A, D419).
   - NS-PURE (Planck NPIPE + DESI + Pantheon+) gave −3.85 versus ΛCDM with its gate failed (no verdict); it is reported here beside NS-SPA's +1.45.
   - Birefringence: β_CMB = 0.277° ± 0.057° (arXiv:2608.06480) is the reference value used throughout.

What carries over to SERAPIS is the method (locked rules) and one lesson: an α = 1 plateau with N* ≈ 57 sits about 3σ below CMB + DESI.
