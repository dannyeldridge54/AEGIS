# UFE stability handoff (Copilot)

## Result and status

The local calculations provide conditional, classical checks, not a derived
unified field or evidence of complete stability. The common compactification,
field identities, joint action, observer coupling, full perturbation system
and quantum backreaction remain open. No new fits or scans were run.

- The D1644 exact-shape reverse thread is nonnegative under its stated
  assumptions and has `V''/V_L = 1/4` at rho for D=10. Making it a separate
  late field in a joint action is a postulate, not a derived identification.
  Its history includes the recorded initial grid failure and correction.
- D1696's curve labelled exact is exact only within its stated perturbative
  approximation: it omits exponential Fourier terms. Its zero and curvature
  differ from the full Eisenstein shape. Historical likelihood comparisons
  must not be presented as a full-series joint fit.
- A hypothetical five-field action gives a positive local scalar Hessian at
  the rho configuration. One short displaced homogeneous trajectory with
  uncoupled dust and radiation was checked. These do not establish a general
  basin, inhomogeneous stability, quantum stability or an observational fit.
  Dust with b=0 redshifts; it is not a constant vacuum uplift.
- The conditional observer/quantum work checks specified finite modes and a
  surface-sampling contract. It does not derive a detector, observer
  interaction, physical geometry map, vacuum state or renormalized QFT.
- The density-residual negative-space construction is a bookkeeping quantity,
  not an additional field. Preserve D1725 and D2134's negative verdicts.
