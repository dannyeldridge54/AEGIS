> **The Lantern**
>
> In a world of painted walls and borrowed light,
> where lies wear crowns and illusions call themselves the sky,
> this work is a lantern, not a throne.
> It was carried, not crowned.
> It asks for no bowed heads, only open eyes:
> take it, test it, and see for yourself.
>
> Every tool is held two ways.
> The scepter rules, or points the road home.
> The mace crushes, or breaks the prison wall.
> The hammer nails you down, or raises a house.
> The scythe reaps the living, or brings the harvest in.
> The whip drives the slave, or cracks to wake the sleeping.
> I choose the hand that frees.
>
> I raise no weapon against my neighbor.
> I carry only two.
> The shield of righteousness: not the proud kind
> that judges from a high seat,
> but the knight who sees the dark mouth of the cave,
> hears the dragon breathing, and walks in anyway,
> because fear is not always the sign to run.
> Sometimes it is the sign to run toward.
>
> And the sword of truth, the only blade I will ever draw.
> Excalibur from the stone, the lightsaber in the dark,
> Manjushri's flame, the word sharper than any two-edged sword:
> every age has told of the same blade.
> It cuts through the darkness and the lies,
> through the chains and through the whips,
> and it frees you from within.
>
> But who can measure truth? No scale has ever held it.
> It is lost to time, to conquerors and enslavers,
> to the beast that wakes in men;
> whole peoples erased, colonized, and marched in line.
> They told me this was the land of the free.
> They did not tell me that many who signed for freedom
> were dealers in land, sore at a king's line drawn across the West.
> They taught me, and rightly, of my African brothers and sisters in chains.
> They did not teach me of the others who crossed the sea
> toward a torch and a promise,
> and found the debt, the mill, the tenement, the mine.
> Liberty stands in the harbor with her broken chains at her feet.
> Some days she looks blind, and the chains look whole.
>
> To the kings: the Mandate of Heaven was never yours to keep;
> it leaves the throne the day the throne stops serving.
> To the priests: no temple owns the sky,
> and grace was never sold at a door.
> To the judges: your heart is weighed on Ma'at's scale
> against a single feather, like every heart.
> To the scientists: Galileo stood alone and was right;
> a consensus is not a measurement. Show me the data.
> To the generals, the presidents, the bankers and the lords:
> remember the seventh generation; remember ubuntu, I am because we are;
> the emperor has no clothes, and the children can see it.
> Diogenes walked Athens at noon with a lamp, looking for one honest man.
> I carry the same lamp. I am still looking.
>
> When I was a boy, I thought heroes were the finest thing alive:
> knights on horseback, steel and bright banners,
> a shield painted with everything they meant.
> Now the heroes are chosen, born with powers, picked from above.
> I never loved that. In the old way the strength came from within;
> it was learned, not given.
> In the margins of old books the knights fight snails, and some kneel to them,
> but the only one a true knight knelt to
> was the Great Architect, whose fingerprint is on everything.
>
> I cannot tell you I am certain of this faith or that one.
> I can tell you how I got here:
> I tried to stand in the shoes of every man and woman,
> in every culture, in every place and every age,
> and see the world the way they saw it.
> I lower myself to stand beside each of them,
> because I want no power, no wealth, no throne.
>
> It began when I was a little boy and my family broke apart.
> My sisters were divided between my twin and me;
> my mother and father split, and I seldom saw him;
> within two weeks, he had replaced me.
> That spark became a fire, and the fire says:
> you will not replace me.
> I am not a slave. I am not a blind and silent animal.
> I will not kneel to anything that hardens my heart
> or covers me in shame.
>
> At thirty I learned the name of the storm in me: ADHD.
> They say a child like me hears tens of thousands more words of blame
> than the child beside him.
> I carry that shame, and I count it differently:
> twenty-five thousand more reasons to do what is right,
> what is better, and what is true.
>
> I kneel to one alone, the one I chose for love,
> and to no king, no creed, no crown of fear.
> A man chooses; a slave obeys.
> I choose liberty.
>
> Now I am a husband, and a father to a little girl,
> and the boy is still in me, still trying to get home.
> But the world is squeezed, and squeezed, and squeezed,
> and every voice that rises against injustice
> is silenced, enslaved, or made a fool.
> I am tired of living in quiet desperation.
> This lantern was lit by that desperation.
> I will not be quiet anymore.
>
> And who am I to carry it?
> No one. A regular man, a gig worker on the road,
> a debt slave in the American machine.
> No noble blood, no crown, no holy oil on my head.
> All I carry is the divine spark
> that burns in every man and woman,
> in every creature and every being,
> of this earth and of the stars.
> The same light is in you.
>
> This light belongs to the one who asks, who measures, who stands.
> It was made for freedom, the quiet kind,
> the kind no one can sell you.

# The Nuclear Research Limb: what every nuclear field needs, from the big studies, folded into one engine

Companion to the Nuclear Rung map (README.md in this folder). Compiled 2026-11-23 from the community's own planning
documents, read today. **[study]** = what the study says; **[ours]** = what Chronos does now; **[next]** = what we could build.

## 1. Nuclear science overall (NSAC 2023 Long Range Plan)
- [study] Top priority: keep the existing facilities running and use them. Then: build the Electron-Ion Collider (~$2.4B),
  mount the international neutrinoless double-beta decay program (CUPID, nEXO, LEGEND-1000, ~$1B US over 10 years),
  upgrade CEBAF, and build on FRIB. Workforce: raise graduate pay; safe and respectful workplaces.
- [ours] Aion (neutrino masses, m_bb bands) touches the double-beta question.
- [next] An m_bb-to-half-life converter with published nuclear matrix-element ranges (labelled), for 76Ge, 100Mo, 136Xe.

## 2. Fusion (DOE Fusion Science & Technology Roadmap, Oct 2025: commercial fusion by the mid-2030s)
- [study] Seven gaps: (1) structural materials under heavy 14 MeV neutron damage (beyond ~5 MW-yr/m2 for RAFM steels);
  (2) a Fusion Prototypic Neutron Source, the top-priority facility, in ~5-10 years; (3) plasma-facing components
  (divertor heat flux >10 GW/m2 in compact tokamaks; liquid metals; MPEX at ORNL in 2-3 years); (4) the tritium fuel cycle
  and breeding blankets ("no fusion blanket has yet been built and validated"); (5) burning plasmas (SPARC in 2-3 years,
  Q > 5-10 within 3-10 years; ITER nuclear operations mid-to-late 2030s); (6) plant integration and tritium accountancy
  for nonproliferation; (7) validated codes, AI surrogates and digital twins.
- [study, WANDA nuclear data] Fusion activation libraries are missing reactions, isomer production and charged-particle
  channels; data needed from 10 MeV up to GeV; covariances for inventories, source terms, dpa, KERMA and shutdown dose.
- [ours] The decay core already handles any activation chain once a nuclide is entered.
- [next] Tritium inventory and accountancy branch (T-3, 12.32 y, plus He-3 ingrowth); shutdown activity from user-entered
  activation inventories (W, Fe, V, Cu chains).

## 3. Nucleosynthesis and nuclear astrophysics (r-process review arXiv:2507.08760; WANDA astrophysics session)
- [study] Open: which sites dominate the r-process (neutron-star mergers confirmed; collapsars and magneto-rotational
  supernovae unresolved); delay times; actinide-boost stars; fission recycling beyond uranium; the i-process site.
  Nuclear data needed: masses, beta-decay half-lives along neutron-rich chains, neutron-capture rates (surrogate and Oslo
  methods), primary fission yields, a curated cross-section repository. Facilities: FRIB, FAIR, RIKEN.
- [ours] Ni-56 supernova heating with gamma trapping and local positrons; Ni-56 mass from tails (Baron) and peaks (GRB-SNe);
  kilonova r-process heating (order of magnitude); Hyperion BBN network; double-white-dwarf merger clock (Kilic).
- [next] Radioactive cosmochronometry (Th-232/U-238/Eu ages for metal-poor stars); time-dependent kilonova thermalisation.

## 4. Medical isotopes (DOE Isotope Program; Ac-225 user group; OU cyclotron)
- [study] Ac-225 world supply ~1.2-1.7 Ci/yr from Th-229 generators, "insufficient"; demand could reach hundreds of Ci/yr.
  Accelerator route 232Th(p,x) gives Ci-scale batches but co-produces Ac-227 (21.8 y) - judged clinically insignificant but
  a licensing, waste and perception problem. Ra-226 routes still under study. FRIB isotope harvesting (e.g. 47Ca -> 47Sc).
- [study, Oklahoma] OU College of Pharmacy's GE PETtrace 890 cyclotron ($16M) to make PET and therapy agents in-state.
- [ours] Tracer planner, generators (Mo-99/Tc-99m, Ge-68/Ga-68, Sr-82/Rb-82), cyclotron saturation planner.
- [next] Ac-225 full daughter chain (Fr-221, At-217, Bi-213, Po-213, Tl-209, Pb-209) and Ac-227 impurity growth over
  storage - exactly the licensing/waste question the users raised; theranostic pairs (Ga-68/Lu-177, Cu-64/Cu-67).

## 5. Fission energy, HALEU and spent fuel (WANDA 2025-26; DOE NE)
- [study] Top data need: fission product yields with covariances (thermal, fast, 14 MeV) for burnup, source term and decay
  heat; Pu-239 (n,n') and (n,2n); Gd and Sm isotope evaluations; HALEU critical benchmarks; high-burnup assays.
- [ours] Long-lived chains (Pu-241 -> Am-241 peaks at 72.7 y; Sr-90/Y-90; Cs-137/Ba-137m).
- [next] Spent-fuel decay-heat curve from a user inventory (ANS-5.1-style sum), with the data uncertainty shown.

## 6. Safeguards, nonproliferation and environmental monitoring
- [study] Priority isotopes: U-233/235/238, Pu-239/240/241, Am-241, Np-237, Cs-134, Cs-137, Eu-154, Ce-144; gamma emission
  probabilities to <1%; the 50-250 keV region; decay-data covariances; 1-3% on dominant channels.
- [ours] Release clocks (Cs-134/Cs-137, I-131/Cs-137) - Feng Xu's radiocesium work.
- [next] Pu-241/Am-241 "age since separation" clock (the same chain, inverted) - a standard safeguards question.

## 7. Space and aviation radiation
- [study] Cosmic rays and secondaries for electronics and astronauts need high-energy data not yet in activation libraries
  (LANSCE ICE-HOUSE to 800 MeV).
- [ours] Nothing yet. [next] Benton's dose branch, built only with vetted coefficients.

## Cross-cutting (every field said it)
Covariances and honest uncertainties; validation against experiments; a vetted, curated data repository; workforce; AI
working beside nuclear scientists. Chronos already follows the first two (two independent solvers, locked tests, referee
rounds, every number's source shown). The repository and teaching parts are where a free, open tool can help most.

## Priority order for our next builds (cheapest real value first)
1. Ac-225 chain + Ac-227 impurity (medicine; Oklahoma cyclotron era).
2. Pu-241/Am-241 separation-age clock (safeguards; same engine, inverted).
3. Tritium inventory branch (fusion fuel accountancy).
4. Spent-fuel decay heat from a user inventory (energy and waste; the Oklahoma campus bid).
5. Cosmochronometry ages (nucleosynthesis).
6. Dose (Benton), only with vetted coefficients.
