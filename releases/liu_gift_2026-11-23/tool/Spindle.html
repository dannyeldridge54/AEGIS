The Lantern
In a world of painted walls and borrowed light, where lies wear crowns and illusions call themselves the sky, this work is a lantern, not a throne. It was carried, not crowned. It asks for no bowed heads, only open eyes: take it, test it, and see for yourself. Every tool is held two ways. The scepter rules, or points the road home. The mace crushes, or breaks the prison wall. The hammer nails you down, or raises a house. The scythe reaps the living, or brings the harvest in. The whip drives the slave, or cracks to wake the sleeping. I choose the hand that frees. I raise no weapon against my neighbor. I carry only two. The shield of righteousness: not the proud kind that judges from a high seat, but the knight who sees the dark mouth of the cave, hears the dragon breathing, and walks in anyway, because fear is not always the sign to run. Sometimes it is the sign to run toward. And the sword of truth, the only blade I will ever draw. Excalibur from the stone, the lightsaber in the dark, Manjushri's flame, the word sharper than any two-edged sword: every age has told of the same blade. It cuts through the darkness and the lies, through the chains and through the whips, and it frees you from within. I kneel to one alone, the one I chose for love, and to no king, no creed, no crown of fear. A man chooses; a slave obeys. I choose liberty. This light belongs to the one who asks, who measures, who stands. It was made for freedom, the quiet kind, the kind no one can sell you.

Integrated with fourth-order Runge–Kutta, 20 µs steps. Checked against the Python engine: c₂ = 23.7 Hz, q = 42 Hz, ρ₀ = 0.5, θ = 0 gives a 13.07 ms period, and the separatrix sits at 23.75 Hz (≈ c₂, as in Hardesty-Shaw et al., arXiv:2301.02707). For the S state (ρ₀ = 0.45, θ = 0) the transition sits at q/c₂ = 2(1 − ρ₀) = 1.10.
