Your decaying-WIMP mechanism, extended to a mini-split wino

A small calculation for Prof. Howard Baer · prepared by Danny Lee Eldridge (independent researcher, Oklahoma City) with AI assistance · 23 November 2026 · THEORY, order-of-magnitude

1. The question

Baer, Barger & Deal (arXiv:2609.04412) let the lightest neutralino decay through Kim–Nilles-suppressed R-parity violation, λ ~ fa/mP, so WIMPs from modulus decay disappear before BBN and axions are all the dark matter. Your benchmark is a natural-SUSY higgsino. What happens for a wino LSP with heavy scalars (mini-split, msoft ~ 100+ TeV)? That spectrum is outside the natural-SUSY setting you advocate; this extends your mechanism and does not test your framework. We hit exactly this problem in our own model: a 693 GeV wino with m3/2 ≈ 156–162 TeV (our current window; an earlier one was 125–132 TeV), where modulus decays overproduce winos.

2. Method

Your Eq. 8, Γ ≈ (3αλ²/128π²) mχ5/msoft4, λ = fa/mP (reduced mP), with the wino's coupling put in by hand, because a pure wino does not couple at tree level to right-handed squarks:

  • Case A, baryonic UDD/QQQ (the operator written in your paper): α = 1/128, width × (mt/msoft)² for the L–R mixing the wino needs (order of magnitude).
  • Case B, leptonic LQD/LLE: the wino couples through SU(2), α2 ≈ 1/30, no mixing suppression.

Requirement: τ ≤ 0.1 s (we treat only this as cleared for a large wino abundance; the full Jedamzik/Kawasaki abundance bound is not computed). Uncertainty: your formula is good to a factor of several, carried as fa ×/÷ √10.

4. Reading

  • With baryonic RPV, a wino LSP can use your all-axion window only for msoft ≲ 10–20 TeV. In mini-split it misses by a factor of about 300–500 in fa.
  • With leptonic RPV it works up to msoft ≈ 160 TeV, needing fa ≈ 1012 GeV, near the top of your window. Your low-msoft numbers (fa ~ 1010 GeV) are reproduced as a check.
  • A side check: Kim–Nilles μ ~ λμfa²/mP reaches the benchmark μ = 0.551 m3/2 ≈ 88 TeV for λμ ≈ 0.05 at fa = 2×1012 GeV.

Not included: exact tree-level widths with mixing (your 2505.09785 does this properly), the abundance-dependent BBN bound, the modulus → wino decay-chain lifetime sum with time dilation, proton-decay constraints on combining operators. We would value your correction on any of it.
