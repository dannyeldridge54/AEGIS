Mechanism: Poisson summation gives theta_L(alpha) = alpha^{-d/2} theta_{L*}(1/alpha); FCC and BCC are mutual duals, so they
must exchange order at alpha = 1, where they tie exactly. A self-dual (up to similarity) lattice such as the 2D hexagonal
has no distinct dual to swap with, so it cannot flip -> hexagonal wins at all scales (Montgomery). E8 and Leech, the
lattices proven optimal at every scale (Cohn-Kumar-Miller-Radchenko-Viazovska), are self-dual as well.
