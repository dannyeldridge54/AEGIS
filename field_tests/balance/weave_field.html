Where it has to close

Twists, bends and the parts of a particle

THE TWIST · geometry

Six turns that pin the shape

The space folds by the Z₆-II twist (1, 2, −3)/6: torus one turns 60°, torus two turns 120°, torus three turns 180°, all at once. A torus can only survive a 60° or 120° turn if it is hexagonal, which forces its shape to τ = ρ = e^{iπ/3}. That is the same point as the knot ρ, and in the Weave's reading the twist is what pins it at the centre. The third torus only flips, so its shape stays free, and that is where something can still move.

TWISTED STRINGS · geometry

Strings caught at the fixed points

The twist leaves fixed points, where a closed string can be caught half-turned. Those twisted strings are the small, hidden cycles in the brane cycles (the ½Σ(d δ + e δ̃) part). They carry the blow-up moduli, and they let a brane split into fractional pieces.

THE BEND · branes

Branes wrap at angles

Each force lives on a stack of D6-branes that wraps one loop on each torus, bent at an angle given by its wrapping numbers. All the wrapping has to cancel the orientifold's charge: ΣN(P+Q) = 8 and ΣN(U − V) = 24. That accounting is the tadpole, and its flux version is the wall that fails (46 against 2).

THE CROSSINGS · matter

Where branes cross, matter lives

At each point where two stacks cross at an angle, a string stretched between them has one massless spin-½ state: a chiral particle. Count the crossings and you count the families; the published Z₆-II model has exactly three. Strings between parallel stacks give the Higgs pair instead.

THE FLOWS · couplings

Mass flows across triangles

A Yukawa coupling links three crossings. The string sweeps the triangle between them, and the coupling falls off with that triangle's area. Small triangles give heavy particles, large ones light. In the Weave the same pattern is written as modular forms of τ, so how far τ sits from ρ sets the mass ratios.

THE MOVING PARTS · fields

What rolls, swings and decays

Rolls: the inflaton down its plateau, then the thread today toward the knot i. Swings: the heavy quark modulus τ_q oscillates after inflation. Decays: τ_q and the gravitinos decay late into winos, which is the N4 wall. Stays put: the shapes pinned by the twist.
