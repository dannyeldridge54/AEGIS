# V3: does dark matter feel a force stars do not? (Sagittarius and GD-1 streams) (DRAFT, not locked)

**Status: DRAFT** (8 Nov 2026). Needs the Gaia DR3 stream download to finish (PC collector, retries daily; ESA rate
limits on 8 Nov).

## What the Twist predicts (fixed now)
The fitted coupling β ≈ 0.04–0.05 gives dark matter an extra attraction of about 2β² ≈ 0.3–0.5% that stars do not
feel. Published Sagittarius limits are far weaker (β_KK < 0.2, arXiv:0807.4363). **Prediction: no detectable
difference** between leading and trailing tails beyond ordinary (ΛCDM) expectations. This is a null test the Twist
should pass; it cannot confirm the coupling.

## Method (fixed when locked)
- Members: Gaia DR3 stars in the stored query regions; membership by proper motion and colour–magnitude cuts written
  into the locked rule before any track is fitted, then frozen.
- Statistic: ratio of star counts and mean distance offset, leading vs trailing tail, in matched angular bins; compared
  with the published ΛCDM model expectation for Sagittarius (Law & Majewski 2010-type models) and a symmetric
  expectation for GD-1.
- Readings: **CONSISTENT** if the asymmetry is within 2σ of the ΛCDM expectation; **TENSION** if beyond 3σ in the
  direction an extra dark-matter force produces; otherwise **CANNOT TELL**. A limit on β from the data is reported.

## What may not happen
No change of cuts after looking at tails; no choice of stream after seeing results (both reported).
