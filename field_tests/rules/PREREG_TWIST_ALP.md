## Why it exists, stated plainly
The single-field Twist predicts β = 0 (PREREG_BIREF_NULL), against a pre-lock β = 0.277° ± 0.057°. This is the minimal extension (D250): a
spectator axion-like field a with V = m²a²/2 and −(g/4) a F F̃ (normalisation β = (g/2)Δa). Built after seeing the birefringence data, so only
future calibrated data may score it. It leaves every other Twist prediction unchanged (the coupling is topological: no fifth force, no α drift).
