# PREREG D476 — fifth forces from the pinned Twist: does the kink at the wall screen them? (cycle 61; PART 1/10, RIGHT; F1/fifth-force)
Locked before computing. Near x_E the potential is V = m_s²|ζ|M_s² on the soft side and a hard wall on the other (D437/D446), so V has a kink with one-sided
slope k = dV/dφ = m_s² r² M_P² ζ′(x_E) c x_E (φ in M_P units, r = M_s/M_P). A universal matter coupling adds β ρ φ (β ≤ 1/√6 for a volume modulus, take β = 1).
Pushes toward the wall do nothing (hard wall); pushes away move the field only if βρ > k. Compute k for m_s ∈ {1e-9, 1e4} GeV, r ∈ {1.43e-4, 3e-5};
compare with ρ of water (1 g/cm³), Earth's core (13 g/cm³), a neutron star (1e15 g/cm³), with 1 g/cm³ = 4.3e-18 GeV⁴. Verdict "SCREENED" if k > ρ_NS for
all cases; "SCREENED IN LAB/SOLAR SYSTEM" if k > 13 g/cm³ only; else "FIFTH FORCE". Note: a linear (not quadratic) response means no Yukawa force at all
below threshold. No reruns.
