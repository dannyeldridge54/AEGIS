# Deviations for PREREG_TWISTI

D1 (07:30 CT, 14 Nov, review; before any scoring): "stabilised after reheating" is too early. The v5 matter yield is computed while x rolls
from the end of inflation to x_end ≈ 29.8 (hidden_kick_decay_v2.py; D298), with the angle dynamics and the ×4.6 entropy dilution inside
that window. TWIST-I therefore means: x rolls exactly as in v5 until matter freeze-out (T ~ 2×10¹³ GeV, x ≈ 30), then settles at a minimum
x_min ≥ x_end, frozen before BBN. The scored rules (r, n_s, w = −1, β = 0) are unchanged.
D2 (same review, consequence, not a rule change): the eV-branch angle relic (D299–D300: 12–28% of DM, computed with x ≈ 73) scales as
φ_i² ∝ (y/x)², so it is ×(73/x_min)². At x_min ≈ 30 it would be ~0.7–1.7 of the observed DM, i.e. possible overclosure. TWIST-I therefore
needs x_min ≳ 50 in the eV-gravitino branch, or an angle stabilised together with x (the stabilising sector then also fixes Im T). Order-of-
magnitude estimate; to be checked.
D3 (07:40 CT, second review; before any scoring): "m_x ≥ 5 meV" is not enough. x arrives at x_min while rolling, so it oscillates about the
minimum with a Planckian amplitude (a cosmological moduli problem). A light (meV–keV) x would dominate and overclose. A heavy x must decay before
BBN. With Γ ≈ m³/(8π M_P²), T_decay ≈ 0.1 MeV at 10 TeV, 1.4 MeV at 50 TeV and 3.9 MeV at 100 TeV, so T_decay ≳ 4–5 MeV needs m_x ≳ 100 TeV
(O(1) coefficient; estimate). Revised assumption: the stabilising sector gives x, and the angle y with it, masses ≳ 100 TeV, or the settling
is adiabatic (minimum mass ≫ H throughout) so that no oscillation energy is left. With a heavy angle, D2's relic bound on x_min no longer
applies (the angle decays). The decay of x adds entropy after matter genesis, which dilutes n_B/s and shifts N* by a small amount; both still
need computing. The scored rules are unchanged.
D4 (07:45 CT; a consequence, not a rule change): a decaying ≳ 100 TeV x (D3) still dilutes n_B/s and DM by Δ ≈ (Δφ²/6M_P²) T_osc/T_decay
(10⁷–10¹³ for 10⁻³–1 M_P displacements, D359). TWIST-I therefore assumes ADIABATIC capture of x at x_min (residual displacement ≲ 10⁻⁷–10⁻³ M_P
depending on m_x), not merely a heavy decaying modulus. The scored rules are unchanged.
D5 (08:00 CT; correction to D4): the residual-displacement bound is ≲ 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁵ M_P for any m_x (D360a numerical check; D359 underestimated the heavy-x case).
D6 (08:12 CT; consequence, not a rule change): kinetic energy at capture is harmless if capture is late (Δ ∝ T_c³); the binding requirement is that x_min coincides with x's resting point to ≲ 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁵ M_P, a positional tuning (D362).
D7 (08:22 CT; consequence): D6's positional tuning is avoidable if x is trapped at an enhanced symmetry point (ESP) at x ≳ 30 crossed at T ≲ 10¹⁰–10¹² GeV with coupling g ≳ 0.1, where the stabiliser has its minimum by symmetry (D363–D364).
D8 (09:05 CT; consequence): with W ⊃ (T − T_E)(λ M_P χχ̄ + k Y), Y at weight 3, the ESP and the stabiliser minimum coincide exactly by construction; the positional tuning of D6 is removed; m_x ≳ 6×10⁴ GeV keeps the ESP a minimum against χ loops (D373–D375).
D9 (09:55 CT; text correction, not a rule change): the lock's sentence "Λ is a free tuning (the v5 tail's 'Λ from the cliff' is given up)" is
superseded. With the D373 stabiliser at V = 0, Λ = V_Twist(x_E) ∝ e^{−2n} (D383), and the capture point is x_E ≈ 27–29.5, crossed at
T ≈ 10¹¹–2×10¹² GeV (D367a, D384), not "x ≈ 30 at matter genesis". The scored rules (r, n_s, w = −1, β = 0) are unchanged.
