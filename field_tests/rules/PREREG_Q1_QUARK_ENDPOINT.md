with fitted |ε| = 0.028–0.038 ("≅ 0.03 needed for the down sector"). The Weave's inflaton ends at ρ = ω.
If the SAME modulus sets the quark Yukawas, its leftover offset after reheating must reach |ε| ≈ 0.03.
