# PREREG D2055: Q2, Kallosh–Linde survey. Locked before reading.
**Referee pre-check (formula):** the volume is stable during inflation only if the barrier height ≳ V_inf.
- In KKLT, V_barrier ~ m₃/₂² M_P², so the condition is H_inf ≲ m₃/₂ (hep-th/0411011).
- Ours: H = 5.69e12 GeV, m₃/₂ = 2.58e5 GeV, ratio 2.2e7.

**Rule:**
- **PASS(Q2)** if a published mechanism (post-2010) gives a volume barrier ≫ m₃/₂² M_P² while keeping m₃/₂ ≲ 1e6 GeV, at a separation of ≥ 1e6, without severe stated tuning.
- **FAIL** if the surveyed sources say it is unsolved, or that it needs tuning they call severe.
- **UNDETERMINED** otherwise.
