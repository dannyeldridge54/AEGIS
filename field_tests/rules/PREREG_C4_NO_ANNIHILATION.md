# PREREG C4 — no dark-matter annihilation signal (forecast lock, 21 Nov 2026)
Weave: dark matter = 12.8 GeV ASYMMETRIC dark baryon (the symmetric part annihilated away into dark photons, which decay to SM via ε) ⇒ today's annihilation rate ≈ 0. A minority wino component (0.67–0.71 TeV) does annihilate (C5, separate lock).
Predictions: (i) no γ-ray excess from stacked dwarf spheroidals for m_DM ≈ 5–50 GeV; (ii) the Galactic-centre GeV excess, if real, is astrophysical (bulge/millisecond pulsars), not DM; (iii) no antiproton/positron excess attributable to ~13 GeV DM.
Scored only on results after this lock (Fermi-LAT dwarf stacks with new dwarfs from Rubin/LSST, CTAO, AMS-02 final).
- FAIL: a stacked-dwarf detection ≥ 4σ (global) consistent with 5–50 GeV DM; OR the GCE shown to follow a DM (NFW-like, spherical) template over the stellar-bulge template at ≥ 5σ in two independent analyses.
- PASS otherwise. Point-source/pulsar resolution of the GCE (e.g. by radio MSP surveys) counts as confirming (ii).
