# PREREG D528 — modular-centre matter parity: weight-assignment search (locked 2026-11-14 before computing)
Question (from D527): is there a family-independent weight assignment k_i = n_i/M for (Q, U, D, L, E, Hu, Hd) such that the centre phase e^{iπk} of SL(2,Z) allows the MSSM couplings and forbids every proton-decay operator up to dimension 6?
Rules:
- Superpotential term allowed iff Σ k_i ≡ t (mod 2), where t is the weight W itself carries. Two conventions are reported: rigid t = 0 (2207.14014) and supergravity t = 1 (W has weight −1). Couplings are modular forms of even weight (any even weight is available).
- Kähler term Φa Φb Φc† Φd† allowed iff k_a + k_b − k_c − k_d ≡ 0 (mod 2).
- Must be ALLOWED: QHuU, QHdD, LHdE, HuHd, LHuLHu (Weinberg).
- Must be FORBIDDEN: LHu, UDD, LQD, LLE (dim ≤ 4); QQQL, UUDE, QQQHd (dim 5, W); QQU†E†, QLU†D† (dim 6, Kähler).
- Search M = 1…6, n_i ∈ {0,…,2M−1}. Report the smallest M with a solution, the number of solutions per M, and one example per convention. If none up to M = 6 → the candidate fails (D527 withdrawn to "assumed").
- Not tested here (stated in advance): discrete anomaly / Green–Schwarz condition; non-perturbative breaking; family-dependent weights.
