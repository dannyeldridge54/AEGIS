# PREREG D1901 — is U29 (tilted visible stacks, parallel Higgs branes on tori 2 and 3) compatible with three chiral generations? (locked 21 Nov 2026 before computing; repo at ba3aac58)
Toy model (stated): factorisable intersecting-brane picture T-dual to the IIB magnetised one, wrapping numbers (n, m) per torus with |n|, |m| ≤ 3, chirality I_xy = Π_i (n_x m_y − m_x n_y), orientifold image (n, −m). Orbifold projections, the hexagonal/square lattice shapes and tadpoles are NOT imposed (a later tick must). Stacks: a (U(3)), b (U(2)), c (U(1)).
Conditions:
 (1) generations: I_ab = ±3 with all three torus factors nonzero;
 (2) U29 tilt: a and b each have n ≠ 0 and m ≠ 0 on tori 2 and 3 (not parallel to their images there ⇒ no N=2 vectors);
 (3) Higgs from one N=2 pair: b ∥ c on tori 2 and 3 (factor 0) and |torus-1 factor of (b, c)| = 1;
 (4) leptons: I_bc′ = ±3.
Count the configurations (a, b, c) meeting all four, up to overall sign and torus-1 ↔ 1 relabelling only. PASS if ≥ 1 exists; FAIL if none. Report the count and the smallest example.
