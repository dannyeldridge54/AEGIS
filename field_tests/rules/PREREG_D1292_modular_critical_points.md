# PREREG D1292 — are i and ρ the only critical points of the 2D lattice energy? (locked before computing)
Energy: Gaussian θ_τ(α) − 1 at unit covolume, α ∈ {0.5, 1, 2}, and Epstein E(τ,2). Scan the fundamental domain
|x| ≤ ½, |τ| ≥ 1, y ≤ 3 on a fine grid; find all points where |∇E| (hyperbolic) has a local minimum below 1e-6 relative.
## Predictions
- P1: the only interior critical points (up to the domain's boundary identifications) are τ = i (saddle) and τ = ρ (minimum), at every α
  and for E(τ,2); no other critical point anywhere in y ≤ 3.
- P2: along the boundary arcs (x = ±½ and |τ| = 1) the energy is monotone between i and ρ (no hidden extremum).
