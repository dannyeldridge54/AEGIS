## Claim under test
D1788 (Λ) and D1760 (birefringence axion mass) both used the bare instanton term V ~ e^K a² e^{−2a Re T} (i.e. e^{−4π Re T}). With W0 ≠ 0 (KKLT, W0 = 5.9e-9 from the master relation) the leading instanton term in the F-term potential is the cross term. For K ⊃ −3 ln(T + T̄) the no-scale pieces cancel and
  V_cross = 4 a σ e^K |W0| A e^{−aσ} cos(aθ + φ),  σ = Re T, a = 2π, A = 1
(derived in the note; for the dark/lepton cycle as an extra modulus the same form is taken with e^K = 1/(2 Re S 𝒱²), 𝒱 = 4.2e4; prefactor uncertainty ×[0.1, 10] ⇒ ±0.37 in σ).

3. Compare with the visible cycle σ_vis = 21.1 (D1767) and the old values (20.57, 20.3–22.1).
