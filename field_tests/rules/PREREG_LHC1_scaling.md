# PREREG LHC1 — a physics route to the cross section without a PDF file: Drell–Yan scaling (locked before computing)
At leading order s·σ(m, √s) = Φ(τ₀), τ₀ = 4m²/s, IF the parton densities did not evolve with scale (exact scaling of the parton
luminosity). Test it on the Resummino tables (arXiv:2304.11915, Table 6): build Φ from the 13 TeV table, predict 13.6 TeV.
## Predictions (before computing)
- P1: pure scaling predicts the 13.6 TeV cross sections to within 5% for every process and mass 200–2000 GeV.
- P2: the residual (PDF evolution) is smooth in m and of one sign, so a one-parameter correction s·σ = Φ(τ₀)·(√s/13)^κ with a single
  κ per process fitted at 13.6 TeV reproduces the 13.6 table to ≤1%.
- P3: the scaling route and the log-linear √s extrapolation agree at 14 TeV to within 5% for m ≤ 1500 GeV (two independent routes;
  their spread is the extrapolation error we will quote).
