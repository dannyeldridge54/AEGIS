Data: Resummino wino tables, 13 and 13.6 TeV (arXiv:2304.11915). τ₀ = 4m²/s.
## Predictions (before computing)

  both energies (larger x ⇒ more u than d valence quarks).
- T2 (Drell–Yan scaling of a RATIO): at equal τ₀, A at 13.6 TeV equals A at 13 TeV to ≤ 1% (evolution cancels in ratios more than in
  absolute rates).

- T4: the tables pass all three as a single self-test the toolkit runs on load (fails loudly if a table is corrupted).
