# PREREG D615: frozen forward predictions for SERAPIS-ρ (scored ONLY on data published after 2026-11-15)

Model: I_ρ²/(I_ρ² + 1) + ε(L − L(ρ)), with ε = 6e-5 (post-hoc, declared; D610) and the vacuum at ρ.
- T_R = 1e3–1e5 GeV (sterile inflaton, D601/D614).
- AD leptogenesis (D599–D604).

Bands (results/rho_nstar_d613.txt, D588 offsets included):
- R1 n_s = 0.9691–0.9723.
- R2 r = 4.6–5.1e-4.
- R3 Σm_ν = 0.0588–0.0600 eV, normal ordering.
- R4 no superpartners below ~1e8 GeV; m₃/₂ ≳ 3.6e10 GeV.
- R5 negligible isocurvature; f_NL ≈ 0.01.

Scoring rules: identical in form to D605 (3σ disfavour rules; r band edges at 0.5× and 2×, i.e. [2.3e-4, 1.0e-3]; r < 2.3e-4 at 95% CL disfavours). No re-tuning of ε.

Relation to D605:
- The R and P bands overlap almost completely. n_s and r cannot separate the i and ρ vacua.
- A future n_s ≥ 0.974 favours i (D605) over ρ (D615) at a modest level only.
