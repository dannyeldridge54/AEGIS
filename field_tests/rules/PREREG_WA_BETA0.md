# PREREG_WA_BETA0 — the theory's own dark energy (beta = 0) is nearly a cosmological constant

## Hypothesis
With gravity-only dark matter the coupling is beta = 0 (Development 64). The tail's slope is fixed at sqrt(2/3) by
the 3 in K = -3 ln(T + Tbar), and the tail's bend only moves w toward -1 (Development 65). Hence the theory's own
prediction, read with ordinary dark matter and a CPL fit: **w0 in [-0.92, -0.88] and w_a in [-0.20, 0.00]** (the
pure exponential gives w0 -0.900, w_a -0.145 from both the CLASS fits and the background calculation; the band adds
0.02 in w0 and 0.05 in w_a for fit and estimator differences, and allows the bend toward w = -1 only through w_a).

## Data that will be scored
The first DESI DR3 BAO cosmology paper's headline w0waCDM result with CMB and a supernova set (baseline combination
as named by DESI; others reported), or a comparable Euclid result if it appears first.

## Readings (published central values and 1 sigma errors, the w0-w_a correlation ignored)
- **SUPPORTED**: both w0 and w_a are within 2 sigma of the predicted box.
- **NOT SUPPORTED**: w_a + 3 sigma_+ < -0.20, or w0 is more than 3 sigma outside [-0.92, -0.88].
- **TENSION**: otherwise.
A NOT SUPPORTED reading means the beta = 0 Twist fails; the coupled versions are scored only by PREREG_WA_CEILING.
