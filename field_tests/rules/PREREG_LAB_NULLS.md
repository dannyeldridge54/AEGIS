# PREREG_LAB_NULLS — the Twist's six laboratory-scale nulls

## Hypothesis
The Twist field couples to ordinary matter only through (i) the derivative Nieh–Yan coupling to the fermion axial
current, c_A (dphi/M_P) J5 with c_A = sqrt(3/8), and (ii) gravity. FLAG-1's coupling beta acts on dark matter only;
PURE-TWIST has beta = 0. There is no tree-level coupling to mass, charge or photons (phi F F and phi F F~ absent;
Development 14), and the field is minimally coupled to gravity. Hence six nulls.
