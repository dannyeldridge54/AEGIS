# PREREG D833 (K.1): normalisation of the anti-D3 interaction κ relative to the uplift V0
Locked before computing, 2026-11-16.
Derivation (to be recorded in D833): per closed-string sector, two parallel D3s at transverse separation r have |V| = 2κ₁₀² T₃² G₆(r), ∇²G₆ = −δ⁶ (Polchinski Vol. 2 §13.3). NS-NS attracts, RR repels; for D3–anti-D3 both attract: −2·2κ₁₀²T₃²G₆. T⁴ zero mode: G₆ ⊃ G(z,U)/(4π Vol₄), with G of D829 (∇²G = −4πδ). Matching V = −2κB (D831): κ = 2κ₁₀²T₃²/(4π Vol₄). With T₃ = 1/((2π)³α'²g_s), 2κ₁₀² = (2π)⁷α'⁴g_s²: 2κ₁₀²T₃² = 2π, so κ = 1/(2 Vol₄). Uplift V0 = 2T₃ (unwarped anti-D3 over BPS). Vol₄ = (2π)⁴α'² v₄ (v₄ in string units). Frame factors cancel in the ratio (both energies localised at the anti-D3).
Result to evaluate: κ/V0 = g_s/(8π v₄).
