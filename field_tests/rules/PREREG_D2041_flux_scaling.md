# PREREG D2041: does flux-scaling stabilisation (Blumenhagen et al., 1503.07634) realise mutation-cube corner 000100?
**Question:** in flux-scaling vacua, are the Kähler moduli stabilised by fluxes (non-geometric included) with:
- (a) masses above H_inf = 5.7e12 GeV, i.e. ≥ 3.7e3 × the KKLT m_T (target 3);
- (b) a visible-cycle F-term suppressed to F^T/(T+T̄) ≤ 1.2e-3 · m₃/₂/at, while anomaly mediation is kept (target 2);
- (c) m₃/₂ ≪ the moduli masses, at a level consistent with m₃/₂ ~ 2.6e5 GeV (no tuning beyond what the paper states)?

**Rule:** decided by what the paper states (read under this lock; quotes or summaries recorded).
- **PASS** if it states (a) and (c), and its SUSY-breaking structure does not give an O(m₃/₂/at) gaugino mass on the visible branes.
- **FAIL** if the paper says the scenario has m₃/₂ near the moduli masses (high-scale SUSY), or gives the gauginos ~m₃/₂.
- **UNDETERMINED** if the text does not say.
