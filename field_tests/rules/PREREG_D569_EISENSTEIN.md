# PREREG D569 — non-holomorphic Eisenstein series as a natural source of p = s plateaus (locked 2026-11-14 23:31 CT before computing)
Idea: E(τ, s) = Σ'_{(m,n)} y^s/|mτ + n|^{2s} (with the ζ(2s) normalisation that gives E → 2ζ(2s) y^s + …) is SL(2,Z)-invariant, ~ y^s at the cusp, with the next term ∝ y^{1−s}: no 1/y term for s > 1. These series multiply higher-derivative terms in string effective actions (s = 3/2, 5/2, 3 for R⁴, D⁴R⁴, D⁶R⁴-type; Green–Gutperle). A plateau V = V₀[1 − E(τ_min, s)/E(τ, s)] approaches V₀ like y^{−s}, i.e. p = s, naturally.
Compute (lattice sum, |m|, |n| ≤ 60): (a) E(τ, s) for s ∈ {3/2, 2, 5/2, 3} at τ = i and τ = ρ, to locate the minimum (where V = 0); (b) for s = 5/2 and s = 3, n_s, r, y* with K = −ln 2 Im τ (D545 method), V = V₀[1 − E_min/E(iy, s)] along the imaginary axis (if the minimum is at i) — else report that the V = 0 point is at ρ, not i.
