# PREREG D1297 — running it backwards in d=3: where does a 3-torus flow? (locked before computing)
Shape space of unit-covolume 3-tori: 5 moduli. Energy: Gaussian θ(α)−1. Minimise from 8 random starting shapes (random 3×3 bases,
det normalised to 1) at α = 0.6 and α = 1.6 (L-BFGS on the 6 upper-triangular basis entries, covolume fixed). Identify the end shape
by its first shell (FCC: 12 nearest neighbours; BCC: 8 nearest + 6 next at ratio 2/√3; cube Z³: 6).
## Predictions (before computing)
- P1: at α = 1.6 every start ends at FCC (12 nearest neighbours).
- P2: at α = 0.6 every start ends at BCC (8 + 6).
- P3: the cube Z³ is never an end point (it is not a minimum at either scale).
