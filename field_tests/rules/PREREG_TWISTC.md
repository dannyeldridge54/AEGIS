# PREREG_TWISTC — TWIST-C: a constant (Polonyi-like) helper term in the superpotential, and how it may be scored

## Why it exists, stated plainly
Gialamas–Katsoulas–Tamvakis (arXiv:2505.03608) keep K = −3 ln(T + T̄ − |S|²/3) and fix Starobinsky's n_s for ACT by a small INTEGER-power
correction to the superpotential. The Twist's analogue with the fewest new ingredients is W = √3 m S (T + c): a constant term linear in the
helper S (the most generic allowed term). Along real T with S = 0: V = (m²/4)(1 + 2c/u)²/(g E), u = T + T̄ — a correction growing toward the rim.
Built AFTER seeing the n_s data; only future data may score it.
