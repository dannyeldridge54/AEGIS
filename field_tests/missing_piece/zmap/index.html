Depth D_H (expansion rate): data and FLAG-1, relative to ΛCDM

D_H = c/H. Negative means faster expansion at that time. Both surveys see it a little fast at z ≈ 0.5–0.7. SDSS DR12 reports H; its offset is shown with the sign flipped to match D_H.

The coupling β across cosmic time

FLAG-1, constant ββ(z) bins to be fitted

FLAG-1 uses one β for all time. The β(z) model lets β take its own value at z < 0.5, 0.5–1 and 1–2. Its fit waits for its rule to be locked and for the M1 and M3 results; the bins fill in here when it runs.

What the fits are, and the limits

FLAG-1 is the coupled-dilaton Spiral Twist with κ = 1 (β ≈ 0.041), fitted to Planck + ACT DR6 + SPT-3G, DESI DR2 BAO, Pantheon+ and DES Y6 S8; ΛCDM is the matching fit. The DESI-versus-SDSS comparison here is an informal look against the ΛCDM fit that includes DESI; the locked comparison (MISSING-PIECE M3) uses a fit without any BAO. E_G assumes galaxies move with the dark matter. All numbers come from the attached files listed in Sources.
