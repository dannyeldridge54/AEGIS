Where the work stands

The reactor chain matches professional codes on 15 of 21 checks. Pushing it to a power plant, every standard tokamak design fails the same squeeze between protecting the divertor and staying in high-confinement mode. A flipped-shape (negative-triangularity) tokamak would avoid that, and the next wall is core radiation, which is now the single thing to verify. Every step was locked first, refereed, and kept on the record, including the mistakes.

Every known nucleus from the NUBASE2020 table, by protons and neutrons. Colour shows how long it lives. The curve on the right is why fusion of light nuclei and fission of heavy ones both release energy: iron and nickel are the most tightly bound.

Level 2 · Reactions

How often two nuclei fuse at a given temperature (the reactivity ⟨σv⟩), from the Bosch–Hale fits used in our engine. D-T is far easier than any other fuel, which is why every near-term reactor burns it.

1 keV ≈ 11.6 million °C. Computed in this window.

Level 4 · Neutrons and the blanket

Each fusion makes one 14 MeV neutron. The blanket must catch it and turn lithium into new tritium, more than one tritium per neutron. Watch neutrons wander through the wall and blanket, multiply, and get captured, live.

Teaching Monte Carlo (one dimension, energy groups) running in this window. The locked engine's full Monte Carlo gives TBR 1.095 ± 0.003 at 90% Li-6 and a 10 cm wall (paper 2); the design target is 1.05 with margin.

Level 5 · Exhaust and radiation

Impurities radiate heat away before it reaches the divertor. That protects the divertor, but radiation from the core also starves the plasma edge. These are the real atomic-physics curves CubeMind holds (OPEN-ADAS), the same ones our engine uses.

Celtic calendar · Aquarius · cosmic clock

Today in the Gaulish Coligny calendar, the fire festivals, the Age of Aquarius counted in Celtic ages, and where it all sits on the Weave's cosmic clock from inflation to now. Calendar and Aquarius dates are standard astronomy; the cosmic timeline uses our model's calibrated age and inflation parameters.

Fire festivals

Traditional dates, and the astronomical cross-quarter moment when the Sun is exactly halfway between solstice and equinox.

Tree month (Celtic tree astrology)

Tree months are a modern system (Robert Graves, 1948), not ancient Druid lore. The Coligny calendar is the genuine ancient one.

The Age of Aquarius in Celtic reckoning

Precession: IAU 2006. Celtic count from Samonios 2026 (14 Nov), 30-year ages of 5-year cycles. The starting year is our choice; the tablet's own epoch is unknown.

Cosmic clock (the Weave)

Log scale of time since the start of the hot universe. Inflation and reheating times are ESTIMATES from the Weave's adopted point (r = 4.8e-4, N* = 68, T_R = 8.9e8 GeV); the age 13.715 Gyr is the calibrated engine. The Weave does not move the Aquarius date by more than hours.
