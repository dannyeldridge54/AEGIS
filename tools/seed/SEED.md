# The Seed engine (Danny 22 Nov 06:46): forks, spinning lenses, pulsing outward until the goalposts are hit, with honesty built in
**The shape:**
- a few **forks** (discrete structural choices: geometry, brane placement, background);
- each fork spins integer **lenses** (wrappings, fluxes, stack sizes) and a radii grid;
- **hard consistency filters** come first: families, massless U(1)_Y, tadpole;
- then a **fit** of at most two measured numbers;
- then **predictions** of everything else, held out.
- Every configuration tried is counted, so every hit carries its look-elsewhere rate.
