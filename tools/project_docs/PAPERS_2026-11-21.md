# Papers on the working pattern (21 Nov 2026, 11:30; Danny 11:25: "gather as much data about this being the working pattern … get a whole bunch of papers on postulating this")
The pattern being tested (U22/U22′/U24):
- one source (the cusp) unfolds in stages;
- things stop at the modular fixed points (knots ρ and i);
- each stage is a lock-and-key selection rule;
- ladders are cusp-type with integer labels;
- golden/silver/Markov loops form the inside geometry.

Each paper is marked SUPPORTS / PARTLY / CONTRADICTS the pattern, with one line on why.

## A. Fixed points as the place things stop (knots)
- **2506.23343** Modular flavor symmetries and fermion mass hierarchies (UCI review). SUPPORTS U24's lock-and-key.
  - Near τ₀ ∈ {i, ω, i∞}, m₁ : m₂ : m₃ ≈ u^{n₁} : u^{n₂} : u^{n₃}, with u = (τ−τ₀)/(τ−τ̄₀) and integer n FIXED by the residual-stabiliser charges, a Froggatt–Nielsen-like labelling rule.
  - The exponent is bounded at ε² at i and ε³ at ω.
  - Charged leptons can reach ε² : ε : 1 near ω. Quarks "need i∞". This is in tension with the Weave's quarks at ρ, unless the slips are larger (see D1826).
- **2009.14242** A₄ model with hierarchical structures at nearby fixed points. SUPPORTS: hierarchies from small deviations off a fixed point.
- **2212.13336** A₄ quark hierarchies close to τ = ω. SUPPORTS the Weave's "quarks slip out of ρ".
- **2112.01680** Yukawa textures at the modular-symmetric vacuum (magnetised orbifolds, τ = ω). PARTLY: quark fit with tuned Higgs angles (D1791).
- **2209.07249** Quark and lepton flavour at residual modular points (magnetised). CONTRADICTS a naive Weave lepton placement: no viable models with an eigen-aligned Higgs (D1786, D1790).
- **2505.21405** Modular-symmetry-protected seesaw. SUPPORTS the neutrinos-at-i role: the knot protects the seesaw structure.
- **2111.05674** Dark-matter stability at fixed points (modular A₄). SUPPORTS U24: the residual symmetry at a knot acts as a lock that keeps DM stable.
- **2510.19927** Symmetry points of N = 1 modular geometry. SUPPORTS: ρ is SUSY-Minkowski only for weight k ≡ 4 mod 6 (D1789).

## B. One modulus doing several jobs (one source)
- **2411.18603** Modular invariant inflation and reheating. SUPPORTS "one source": the SAME modulus drives inflation (from near i along the arc |τ| = 1) and ends near ω, setting flavour and RH-neutrino masses. It differs from the Weave (the Weave's inflaton comes from the cusp and ends exactly at ρ); its r ~ 1e-7 vs the Weave's 5.7e-4 is a discriminator.
- **2405.06497** Modular invariant slow-roll inflation. SUPPORTS the one-source idea.
- **2405.08924** Modular invariant hilltop inflation. PARTLY: inflation from a fixed-point saddle.
- **2303.02947** Moduli inflation from modular flavour symmetries (JHEP 06 (2023) 187). SUPPORTS: the flavour modulus is the inflaton.
- **0804.0869** Triple unification of inflation, dark matter and dark energy with a single field. SUPPORTS U24's "one source" in spirit (not modular).
- **2509.22781** Rolling with modular symmetry: quintessence near τ ≈ i. SUPPORTS the thread ending at the i knot. Fifth forces are unsolved there too (as D1851–D1861).

## What the collection says about the pattern
1. **Strongest support:** "things stop at the fixed points and hierarchies are integer powers of the distance from them, with the integers set by the residual charges". This is a mainstream, published mechanism. It is exactly U24's lock-and-key and it gives the labelling rule D1825 lacked.
2. **One source:** published models do use one modulus for inflation plus flavour (2411.18603, 2303.02947), so the idea is not fringe. The Weave's version (cusp → ρ, dilaton as inflaton) is its own variant.
3. **Main contradiction:** the review says quark hierarchies need i∞, not ω. The Weave puts its quarks near ρ = ω, so this is tested next (D1826).
