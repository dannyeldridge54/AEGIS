Companion to WEAVE_NAMES.md (same charter: honour, never decorate; living traditions get review before public use; credit the source).

What these names label: the rungs of the ρ-side slip ladder (WEAVE_QUARK_LADDER.md), |ε_m| = 0.0251/(m + 1/16).
Rungs 1–9 keep plain numbers for now (rung 1 = down quarks, rung 8 = up quarks, 68% band 5–14).
Rungs 10 and beyond are EMPTY in the Standard Model. A name marks a place where a hidden family could sit (prediction L3); it does NOT claim a particle exists there.
Each name is PROPOSED, pending review by people of that tradition (charter rule 1); living traditions are marked ●.
