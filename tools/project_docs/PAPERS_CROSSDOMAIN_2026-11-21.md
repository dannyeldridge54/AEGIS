# The pattern across domains — power generation, fusion, accelerators, grids, superconductors, nuclear (21 Nov 2026, 11:40; Danny 11:33: "reach into other things like power generation and nuclear physics … get a whole bunch of paperwork together … see if you can't find the pattern")
The pattern under test (U25): **rational = lock** (resonance, island, step, locking: where energy transfers or structure forms or breaks), **most-irrational (golden/noble) = barrier** (stability, confinement, protection).

## 1. Fusion power: tokamak magnetic surfaces
- **physics/0206001** (the "tokamap", a Hamiltonian field-line map; Balescu et al.):
  - Magnetic island chains form on RATIONAL q = m/n surfaces, which are "generically topologically unstable". IRRATIONAL (KAM) surfaces resist chaos and form the transport barriers that hold the plasma.
  - In the standard map the LAST surface to break is the golden-mean one (K_c ≈ 0.9716). In the tokamap the most robust barriers are other "noble" numbers (N(1,11) ≈ 1.086, N(1,8)/N(1,7) ≈ 1.116/1.131 around the rational 9/8, edge N(4,2) ≈ 4.38). The authors note that the golden number itself plays no special role there.
  - **Pattern: CONFIRMED in form** (rational = break / transport, noble irrational = barrier). Caveat: it is "noble", not strictly "golden".
- farside.ph.utexas.edu (fusion notes), 1805.10394, 2608.05414 (symplectic field-line maps in tokamaks), 2610.23844 (MHD eigenmodes): tearing modes and islands live at rational surfaces. This is standard tokamak physics.

## 2. Particle accelerators
- CERN accelerator school, *L05 Resonances* (indico 1252671); USPAS notes (Cornell); arXiv 1608.02449 (beam losses, circular machines), 2008.12945 (ELENA frequency analysis), PRAB 2gfw-xckn:
  - The beam is destroyed or lost when the tunes satisfy n Q_x + m Q_y = integer (RATIONAL lines). A tune near a resonance can "lock on" to it.
  - Working points are chosen where the beam is least affected, i.e. away from low-order rational lines.
  - **Pattern: CONFIRMED** (rational = loss/lock). Choosing "most irrational" is common practice but not stated in this source.

## 3. Power grids
- arXiv 2601.11289 (decentralisation can hinder frequency synchronisation through multiple phase transitions), PMC9072397, NREL fy22osti/82489, PIK-Potsdam 28467, 1611.09825 (cycle flows and multistability), 1905.13664 (diluted topology):
  - Generators and inverters must PHASE-LOCK (Kuramoto-type) to deliver power; loss of lock is a blackout mode. Synchronisation is a 1:1 rational lock.
  - **Pattern: CONFIRMED** (power flows through the lowest-order lock, 1:1).

## 4. Superconducting electronics (Josephson junctions)
- arXiv 1309.1277 (Devil's staircases and continued fractions in Josephson junctions), 2505.20502 (all fractional Shapiro steps), 1802.09212, 1905.09672, PRE 96 022210, PRB 88 214515:
  - Under microwave drive the junction LOCKS onto Shapiro steps at voltages V = [N ± 1/(n ± 1/(m ± …))]ω, a continued-fraction (Farey-tree) Devil's staircase. This is the basis of the voltage standard: the volt is DEFINED by an integer lock.
  - **Pattern: CONFIRMED** (rational locks give exact, usable output). The step-width ordering is not quantified in 1309.1277.

## 5. Wireless / resonant power transfer
- arXiv 1908.02541 (strongly coupled magnetic resonance), 1401.3324 (frequency- and impedance-tuned non-radiative transfer), 2011.14215, Lancaster IECON2024, Essex repository, USPTO 8299652 / 9099885 / 9923417 / 8446045 / 9461478:
  - Maximum power transfer happens when transmitter and receiver are tuned to the SAME resonance (1:1). Strong coupling splits it into two modes.
  - **Pattern: CONFIRMED** (amplification = lowest-order rational lock).

## 6. Nuclear physics (elements)
- Nuclear shell model (Wikipedia; UCSD 130A; HyperPhysics; MIT 8.701), arXiv physics/0301074 (particles and shells), 2504.09148 (from spin to pseudospin symmetry: the origin of magic numbers), Sorlin (ESC 2009):
  - Nuclei are most bound at shell closures (magic numbers 2, 8, 20, 28, 50, 82, 126), which the spin–orbit term creates. Elements build through resonant captures, i.e. lock-and-key resonances (the Hoyle state in carbon).
  - **Pattern: PARTLY.** It is clearly lock-and-key (closed shells and resonances), but the magic numbers are not a Farey or modular sequence. The analogy is to the selection-rule part of U24, not the Farey part.

## What the stack says (the pattern found)
1. **Across every engineered system checked, the same split holds.** Locked rational ratios are where energy is TRANSFERRED or LOST: grid synchronisation, wireless power, Shapiro steps, accelerator resonances, tokamak islands. Irrational ("noble") ratios are where things are CONFINED or PROTECTED: tokamak transport barriers, accelerator working points.
2. **The amplification route is the lowest-order lock (1:1 first).** Every power-delivery technology above works by holding a 1:1 (or low-order) lock. The protection route is noble/irrational. In fusion it is "noble numbers" (golden in the simplest standard map), not always golden.
3. **Nuclear physics shows the lock-and-key half (shells, resonances) but not the Farey half.** The universality is strongest for periodic, driven, coupled systems, as U25 said.
4. **Honest limit (unchanged):** this is universality of the circle map / KAM mathematics. It shows the Weave's pattern is the pattern nature uses for locking and protection. It does NOT, by itself, show the Weave's specific physics is right.
