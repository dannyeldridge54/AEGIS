- The visible cycle T is fixed by a hidden SU(N) condensate (KKLT). Each rank N carries the coupling across a band of width λ = ln(1/W₀)/2π ≈ 3 before handing it to the next rank (the band ladder).

## 3. How it fits together (the joints that close)
1. **The knot ρ** sets g_s, Koide, three families (|M| = 9) and the flux part of every visible force. One point carries leptons, families and forces.
2. **The flux 9** appears twice: as the family count (3 in each of the 3 Z₃ sectors) and as the inflaton's coupling to our forces (f = T + 9S). So three generations, the strength of the forces and the reheating temperature are one knot.
3. **Cycle size ≈ 21** appears three times: the visible forces (from unification), the dark force (α_D), and the photon-axion (birefringence). If the three cycles are equal, β ≈ 0.28° and α_D = 0.035 both follow from gauge unification.
4. **The knot i** carries the neutrinos, the dark thread's endpoint, and the branch point of the down quarks' half class.
5. **One chain links inflation to the LHC:** the tilt (χ) and the coupling (N, λ) set W₀ and the gravitino, which set the wino mass, which sets the dark-matter component and the collider target. The master relation is the tilt–coupling–wino relation.
6. **When τ_q dilutes, reheating drops out of r** (exact cancellation), so r measures the quark modulus directly.

## 4. Honest limits of "one step"

That has no evidential weight: one-operation expressions in 2 and 3 reach 43 of the integers 1–60. Only a rule fixed in advance would count; the clean ones so far are families = 3 = |Z₃| ⇒ |M| = 9, and the half class = 1/|Z₂|.
