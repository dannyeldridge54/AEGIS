- The inflaton (the string coupling S) rolls down the species plateau and stops at ρ. Because ρ is self-dual, the coupling it leaves behind is fixed: g_s = 2/√3.

- The quarks slip out of ρ on the SUGRA ladder |ε_m| = 0.0251/(m + 1/16):

  - down quarks on the half-integer class m = ½. A half class only exists on a double cover branched at i, so the down quarks carry i's Z₂. The computed minimum is 0.0457 at 49°.
- The forces live on a magnetised D7 whose gauge function is f = T + 9S. The 9 is the same |M| = 9 flux that makes three families. The dark force lives on an unfluxed D7 (f = T), so it stays cold.
- The visible cycle T is fixed by a hidden SU(N) condensate (KKLT). Each rank N carries the coupling across a band of width λ = ln(1/W₀)/2π ≈ 3 before handing it to the next rank (the band ladder).
