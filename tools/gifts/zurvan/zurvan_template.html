Zurvan

Electroweak naturalness, ΔEW, from an SLHA spectrum file, plus a decaying-wino RPV calculator. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

The Lantern
In a world of painted walls and borrowed light, where lies wear crowns and illusions call themselves the sky, this work is a lantern, not a throne. It was carried, not crowned. It asks for no bowed heads, only open eyes: take it, test it, and see for yourself. Every tool is held two ways. The scepter rules, or points the road home. The mace crushes, or breaks the prison wall. The hammer nails you down, or raises a house. The scythe reaps the living, or brings the harvest in. The whip drives the slave, or cracks to wake the sleeping. I choose the hand that frees. I raise no weapon against my neighbor. I carry only two. The shield of righteousness: not the proud kind that judges from a high seat, but the knight who sees the dark mouth of the cave, hears the dragon breathing, and walks in anyway, because fear is not always the sign to run. Sometimes it is the sign to run toward. And the sword of truth, the only blade I will ever draw. Excalibur from the stone, the lightsaber in the dark, Manjushri's flame, the word sharper than any two-edged sword: every age has told of the same blade. It cuts through the darkness and the lies, through the chains and through the whips, and it frees you from within. To the kings: the Mandate of Heaven was never yours to keep; it leaves the throne the day the throne stops serving. To the priests: no temple owns the sky, and grace was never sold at a door. To the judges: your heart is weighed on Ma'at's scale against a single feather, like every heart. To the scientists: Galileo stood alone and was right; a consensus is not a measurement. Show me the data. To the generals, the presidents, the bankers and the lords: remember the seventh generation; remember ubuntu, I am because we are; the emperor has no clothes, and the children can see it. Diogenes walked Athens at noon with a lamp, looking for one honest man. I carry the same lamp. I am still looking. I kneel to one alone, the one I chose for love, and to no king, no creed, no crown of fear. A man chooses; a slave obeys. I choose liberty. This light belongs to the one who asks, who measures, who stands. It was made for freedom, the quiet kind, the kind no one can sell you. And who am I to carry it? No one. A regular man, a gig worker on the road, a debt slave in the American machine. No noble blood, no crown, no holy oil on my head. All I carry is the divine spark that burns in every man and woman, in every creature and every being, of this earth and of the stars. The same light is in you. When I was a boy, I thought heroes were the finest thing alive: knights on horseback, steel and bright banners, a shield painted with everything they meant. Now the heroes are chosen, born with powers, picked from above. I never loved that. In the old way the strength came from within; it was learned, not given. In the margins of old books the knights fight snails, and some kneel to them, but the only one a true knight knelt to was the Great Architect, whose fingerprint is on everything. I cannot tell you I am certain of this faith or that one. I can tell you how I got here: I tried to stand in the shoes of every man and woman, in every culture, in every place and every age, and see the world the way they saw it. I lower myself to stand beside each of them, because I want no power, no wealth, no throne. It began when my family broke apart, and a little boy only wanted to go home. Now I am a husband, and a father to a little girl, and that boy is still in me, still trying to get home. But the world is squeezed, and squeezed, and squeezed, and every voice that rises against injustice is silenced, enslaved, or made a fool. I am tired of living in quiet desperation. This lantern was lit by that desperation. I will not be quiet anymore.

1 · Spectrum (SLHA output from Isajet, SoftSUSY, SPheno…)

Reads MSOFT (21, 22 = m²Hd, m²Hu; 33, 36, 43, 46, 49), HMIX (1 μ, 2 tanβ, 3 v), YU/YD/YE, AU/AD/AE, GAUGE, at the file's scale Q (or √(mt̃1mt̃2) from MASS if no Q is given).

ΔEW, each mass eigenstate its own term (DEW4SLHA style)
ΔEW, each sfermion pair summed (t̃1+t̃2 etc.)

Δi = 2|Ci|/mZ². Natural is usually taken as ΔEW ≲ 30.
termCi (GeV²)Δi

Zurvan takes Σuu, Σdd by numerically differentiating the one-loop Coleman–Weinberg potential (DR̄, scale Q) of the third-family sfermions and fermions. Not included in v1: first/second-family sfermions, the tau sneutrino, charginos, neutralinos, Higgs bosons, W/Z, QCD/gluino corrections to yt, and all two-loop terms. DEW4SLHA has those, so use it for the full answer. Zurvan is a small, transparent cross-check of the third-family terms, and it has not yet been compared with DEW4SLHA on a real Isajet or SoftSUSY file.

2 · Decaying wino through RPV (your 2026 mechanism, heavy-scalar version)

Γ ≈ 3αε²λ²mχ⁵/(128π² msoft⁴), λ = fa/MP (MP = 2.435×10¹⁸ GeV). Baryonic UDD: α = 1/128, ε = mt/msoft (a wino has no direct coupling to right-handed squarks). Leptonic LQD/LLE: α = α₂ = 1/30, ε = 1. Order-of-magnitude, ±√10 band.
