Zurvan

Electroweak naturalness, ΔEW, from an SLHA spectrum file, plus a decaying-wino RPV calculator. Runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded.

This work is a lantern, not a throne. It belongs to the one who asks, who measures, who will not kneel: take it, test it, and see for yourself.

1 · Spectrum (SLHA output from Isajet, SoftSUSY, SPheno…)

Reads MSOFT (21, 22 = m²Hd, m²Hu; 33, 36, 43, 46, 49), HMIX (1 μ, 2 tanβ, 3 v), YU/YD/YE, AU/AD/AE, GAUGE, at the file's scale Q (or √(mt̃1mt̃2) from MASS if no Q is given).

ΔEW, each mass eigenstate its own term (DEW4SLHA style)
ΔEW, each sfermion pair summed (t̃1+t̃2 etc.)
Δi = 2|Ci|/mZ². Natural is usually taken as ΔEW ≲ 30.
termCi (GeV²)Δi

Zurvan takes Σuu, Σdd by numerically differentiating the one-loop Coleman–Weinberg potential (DR̄, scale Q) of the third-family sfermions and fermions. Not included in v1: first/second-family sfermions, charginos, neutralinos, Higgs bosons, W/Z, the two-loop gluino terms. DEW4SLHA has all of those, so use it for the full answer; Zurvan is a quick, transparent cross-check.

2 · Decaying wino through RPV (your 2026 mechanism, heavy-scalar version)

Γ ≈ 3αε²λ²mχ⁵/(128π² msoft⁴), λ = fa/MP (MP = 2.435×10¹⁸ GeV). Baryonic UDD: α = 1/128, ε = mt/msoft (a wino has no direct coupling to right-handed squarks). Leptonic LQD/LLE: α = α₂ = 1/30, ε = 1. Order-of-magnitude, ±√10 band.
