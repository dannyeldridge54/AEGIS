Mass sum and μ→eγ

mh (TeV)max |fμτ| (MEG II 1.5×10⁻¹³)

BR(μ→eγ)h = α|feτ*fμτ|²/(48πGF²mh⁴), with feτ/fμτ from the dials. This is the h⁺ contribution only; the k⁺⁺ loop has the opposite chirality and adds incoherently, so the ceiling is conservative and depends on mh alone.

The minimal Zee–Babu model: A. Zee, Nucl. Phys. B264 (1986) 99; K. S. Babu, Phys. Lett. B203 (1988) 132; see also Babu & Macesanu, hep-ph/0212058, and Nebot et al., arXiv:0711.0483. Formulas used here are from Herrero-García, Nebot, Rius & Santamaria, arXiv:1402.4491 (convention Mν = U diag(m) Uᵀ, checked). Aion predicts flavour ratios only, not the overall mass scale or the loop function I(mk/mh); perturbativity and the μ bound push mh, mk to the few-TeV scale. Not covered: Babu & Saad's SU(5) completion (arXiv:2412.14562), where coloured scalars contribute comparably, and the k⁺⁺ sector (its g couplings are free). The Python version gives 3σ scan ranges over δ and both θ₂₃ octants.
