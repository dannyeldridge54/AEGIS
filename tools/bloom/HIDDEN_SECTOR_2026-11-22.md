## 2. What string theory says about hidden sectors
- **The dark-glueball problem** (1609.02151, quoted):
  - any confining hidden Yang–Mills sector with **3 eV ≲ Λ ≲ 2.4e8 GeV** overproduces dark matter or breaks BBN, unless reheating is extremely asymmetric (ξ ≳ 2.3 Λ/eV);
  - Λ ≥ 2.4e8 GeV is rescued by decays to moduli;
  - "seventeen orders of magnitude" of Λ are dangerous.
- **Intersecting-brane hidden sectors:** filler USp stacks on the O-planes (He–Li–Sun 2112.09632; CLL hep-th/0403061; hidden-sector variations 0704.1855).
  - They confine when β = −6 + 2|I_a| + |I_b| + |I_c| < 0.
  - Exotics become composites.
  - ≥ 2 confining USp groups are usually needed for moduli stabilisation.
