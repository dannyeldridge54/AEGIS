We study inflation on the modular plane with Kähler potential K = -ln(2 Im tau) and alpha = 1/3, driven by a plateau built from the modular invariant |j - 1728|. The inflaton settles at the square torus, tau = i, which we show is both a critical point required by S-duality and a supersymmetric Minkowski vacuum (E6(i) = 0). The minimal plateau leaves this vacuum massless; adding a single term linear in |j - 1728| (the holomorphic E6 route), of relative strength epsilon ~ 1.5e-4, makes it massive and tilts the spectrum. The model then has two faces sharing one closed form, which we call a Janus pair: the untilted limit gives n_s = 0.961-0.963 and r = 3.0-3.3e-4, while the tilted model gives n_s = 0.970-0.975, r = (4.8-5.7)e-4 and running alpha_s = -(8.6-9.9)e-4. Eliminating epsilon yields a parameter-free relation fixing alpha_s from n_s and r to about 12 percent. A variant at tau = rho gives n_s = 0.969-0.972 and r = (4.6-5.1)e-4. We check reheating through a stiff phase (w ~ 0.55, T_R = 1e3-1e5 GeV), gravitino closure (m_3/2 > 1e11 GeV), no-scale soft masses, proton stability from the modular centre, and Affleck-Dine leptogenesis, which implies a nearly massless lightest neutrino and sum m_nu ~ 0.059 eV with normal ordering. The size of epsilon is fitted, not derived. All predictions were frozen with public hashes before submission and will be decided by LiteBIRD, CMB-S4-class surveys, DESI/Euclid and JUNO/DUNE.
