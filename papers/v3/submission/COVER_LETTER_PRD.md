Please consider the enclosed manuscript, "SERAPIS: a modular plateau with a massive vacuum at the square torus, its Janus pair of predictions, and a three-observable consistency relation", for publication in Physical Review D.

The paper builds an inflationary plateau on the modular plane from the invariant |j − 1728| and shows that the endpoint τ = i is fixed by the modular symmetry. A single additional term gives this vacuum a mass and tilts the plateau, producing two closed-form faces of the model and a parameter-free relation between n_s, r and the running α_s that future CMB data can test. We work through the consistency chain such a model must pass — reheating, gravitinos, soft masses, proton stability and baryogenesis — and state openly what is assumed: the tilt strength ε is fitted rather than derived, loop-size soft masses require visible matter of modular weight one, and the frozen predictions assume inefficient preheating.

All predictions were frozen with SHA-256 hashes in a public repository before submission (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24250733); an appendix maps every cited computation to its pre-registration and script. The manuscript was revised after two independent internal referee passes.
