Title: SERAPIS: a modular plateau with a massive vacuum at the square torus, its Janus pair of predictions, and a three-observable consistency relation

Abstract (1831 characters; limit 1920):
We study inflation on the modular plane with Kähler potential K = -ln(2 Im tau) and alpha = 1/3, driven by a plateau built from the modular invariant |j - 1728|. The inflaton settles at the square torus, tau = i, a critical point required by the S generator of the modular group, where j - 1728 has a double zero. The minimal plateau leaves this vacuum massless; adding a single term linear in |j - 1728|, of relative strength epsilon = (1.3-1.6)e-4, makes it massive and tilts the spectrum. The model then has two faces sharing one closed form, which we call a Janus pair: the untilted limit gives n_s = 0.959-0.961 and r = (3.3-3.7)e-4, while the tilted model gives n_s = 0.970-0.975, r = (4.8-5.7)e-4 and running alpha_s = -(8.8-10.2)e-4 (frozen band -(8.6-9.9)e-4). Eliminating epsilon yields a parameter-free relation fixing alpha_s from n_s and r, accurate to about 2 percent once the constant offset of the modular invariant is included. A variant at tau = rho gives n_s = 0.969-0.972 and r = (4.6-5.1)e-4. We check reheating through a stiff phase (w ~ 0.55, T_R = 1e3-1e5 GeV), the closure of preheating, proton stability, and Affleck-Dine baryogenesis, whose tree-level A-terms vanish at tau = i and which requires an essentially massless lightest neutrino. In the modular-covariant completion of the supergravity embedding the inflaton carries no supersymmetry breaking and the plateau bounds the gravitino mass; with anomaly-mediated gauginos this points to m_3/2 ~ 1e5-1e6 GeV and a TeV-scale wino, and the modular centre still yields ordinary matter parity at rho, though not at tau = i without flavour multiplets. The size of epsilon is fitted, not derived. The inflationary predictions were frozen with public hashes before submission and will be tested by future CMB polarisation and large-scale-structure surveys.
