Abstract (1506 characters; limit 1920):
We study inflation on the modular plane with Kähler potential K = -ln(2 Im tau) and alpha = 1/3, driven by a plateau built from the modular invariant |j - 1728|. The inflaton settles at the square torus, tau = i, a critical point required by the S generator of the modular group, where j - 1728 has a double zero. The minimal plateau leaves this vacuum massless; adding a single term linear in |j - 1728|, of relative strength epsilon = (1.3-1.6)e-4, makes it massive and tilts the spectrum. The model then has two faces sharing one closed form, which we call a Janus pair: the untilted limit gives n_s = 0.959-0.961 and r = (3.3-3.7)e-4, while the tilted model gives n_s = 0.970-0.975, r = (4.8-5.7)e-4 and running alpha_s = -(8.6-9.9)e-4. Eliminating epsilon yields a parameter-free relation fixing alpha_s from n_s and r to about 12 percent. A variant at tau = rho gives n_s = 0.969-0.972 and r = (4.6-5.1)e-4. We check reheating through a stiff phase (w ~ 0.55, T_R = 1e3-1e5 GeV), gravitino closure (m_3/2 > 1e11 GeV), proton stability from the modular centre, and baryogenesis; an order-of-magnitude Affleck-Dine estimate points to a nearly massless lightest neutrino (m_nu1 < 1e-8 eV). The Kahler potential is not of no-scale type, but tree-level soft masses vanish for visible matter of modular weight one. The size of epsilon is fitted, not derived. All predictions were frozen with public hashes before submission and will be tested by future CMB polarisation and large-scale-structure surveys.
