## What we learned
1. **The plateau's origin (P3).** Its E(τ,2)-ratio shape has no identified string origin. One-loop anti-D3 terms add a rising wall, not a plateau (D828–D832); warped throats and any modular-invariant holomorphic factor approach a plateau exponentially, not as the needed power law (D837–D839). P3 stays a postulate, now stated as "the plateau sits in the uplift sector" (D870).
2. **The anti-D3.** An anti-D3 on the inflaton torus is incompatible with SERAPIS-E's tilt: at the moduli that give the tilt, its pull would shift n_s by +0.005 to +0.06 (D833–D836, D867). An anti-D3 in a distant warped throat is shielded enough (D869); a T-brane uplift is the other option (D868). Both need more than the toroidal orbifold.
3. **τ = i.** Kept as a conditional variant under a decision rule written in advance (D841): no frozen prediction fails, but its ε has no derived source, it needs a 2⊕1 heavy-neutrino structure, and its leptogenesis needs CP-odd axions on two cycle classes (D854).
4. **CP violation (P1 → P1′).** In string theory CP is a symmetry, so "free phases" had to go (D843). Our torus alone has no CP source (D844). A two-term racetrack for a Kähler axion breaks CP spontaneously in 90% of cases, and in about 40% that vacuum is the deepest (D845, D851). Only τ = i needs it; ρ does not (D855).
5. **Moduli consistency.** After correcting a misread of the BHK formula (D866: the tilt's modulus is the 4-cycle transverse to the inflaton torus), the racetrack works on the other two 4-cycles with half- or quarter-strength instantons, but only at string-size tori and g_s ≈ 0.4–0.5 (D867). Loop and α′ corrections are small (D862, D863); the D7 piece of the tilt is negligible unless the inflaton torus is string-size (D873).
6. **Paper.** The v3.2 draft has all of this, passed a full independent referee (10 major and 15 minor items handled, D858, D871, D872), builds cleanly at 16 pages, and is held for Danny's decision.
