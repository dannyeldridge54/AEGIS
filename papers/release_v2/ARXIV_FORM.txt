Title:
Torsion-Born Early Dark Energy with an Onset Locked to Matter-Radiation Equality: Derivation and Pre-Registered Likelihood Tests

Authors:
Danny Lee Eldridge

Abstract (1752 of 1920 characters):
We construct early dark energy (EDE) from spacetime torsion and test it with full Boltzmann codes and decision rules hashed before each fit. Minimal Einstein-Cartan torsion cannot shorten the sound horizon: its spin-torsion energy is negative and $\sim10^{-57}$ of the radiation density near equality. Promoting the Barbero-Immirzi parameter to a field gives a canonical, ghost-free pseudoscalar whose kinetic energy is the torsion energy; with one assumed potential, $V=\Lambda^4[1-\cos(\phi/F)]^3$, the model is axion-like EDE. We lock the field's onset to matter-radiation equality, leaving two extra parameters. On Planck 2018 + DESI DR2 + Pantheon+ the locked model gives $\Delta\chi^2=-4.70$ ($\Delta{\rm AIC}=-0.70$, $H_0=70.5$); on the pre-registered Planck + ACT DR6 test it gives $\Delta\chi^2=-7.23$ ($\Delta{\rm AIC}=-3.23$, $\approx2.2\sigma$, $H_0=70.4$), robust to three supernova compilations. Two independent field solvers agree to $0.4$ in $\chi^2$, and a Floquet analysis with expansion finds no fragmentation. The pre-registered overall verdict is a tie, because the model raises $S_8$. The pure torsion width $F=\sqrt{3/2}\,M_{\rm Pl}$ is excluded ($\Delta\chi^2=+14.6$); a profile in $F=\sqrt{3/2}\,M_{\rm Pl}/N$ prefers $N=8$ on Planck + ACT and $N=9$ on Planck. On the first fully independent CMB data, SPT-3G D1 with DESI DR2 and Pantheon+, the pre-registered one-parameter model ($N=9$) fails ($\Delta\chi^2=+0.52$), as does a zero-parameter variant with a fixed initial angle ($\Delta\chi^2=+0.43$). These results agree with the ACT collaboration's finding of no significant preference for EDE. The minimal model predicts no cosmic birefringence. We state which parts are derived, which are assumed, and which results failed.
