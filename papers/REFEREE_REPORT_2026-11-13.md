# Independent referee report on Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.24202422 (v4 PDF)

**Referee report: \"The Twist: inflation, matter and dark energy from one supergravity field\" (Eldridge, Zenodo 10.5281/zenodo.24202422)**\n\n**1. Summary of claims**\n\nThe paper identifies a dilaton plus Barbero–Immirzi angle (field-space curvature −2/3) with the no-scale field T of K = −3 ln(T + T̄). It takes the axial coupling to fermions from the Nieh–Yan term, c_A = √(3/8).\n\nA plateau → cliff → tail potential is claimed to give:\n- α = 1 inflation, with n_s ≈ 0.963–0.965 and r ≈ 0.004;\n- lepto-axiogenesis driven by the rotating angle, giving n_B/s = 8.7×10⁻¹¹;\n- a thawing dark-energy tail of slope √(2/3) with β = 0.\n\nThat tail (PURE-TWIST) is claimed to beat ΛCDM in three pre-registered late-time tests. The paper also lists frozen predictions (r, n_s, null birefringence, w(z)) and negative results. About a third of the text consists of dated addenda that revise or withdraw earlier material.\n\n**2. Overall assessment and recommendation**\n\n**Recommendation: reject in current form; a consolidated resubmission would be welcome.**\n\nThe pre-registration discipline and the openly reported negative results are real strengths, and much better than is typical for independent work. As a paper, though, this is not reviewable. It is a lab notebook with addenda added after the references, and later sections contradict earlier ones on:\n- where the theory fits in string theory;\n- where the cliff comes from;\n- the parameter values (μ, n, m_σ, the dark-matter mass);\n- the observable predictions (n_s, r, E_G, w_a).\n\nThe abstract still states claims the paper itself later withdraws or reports as failed. The headline inflation prediction failed its own locked test (NS-SPA: INCONSISTENT). On the full dataset the late-time \"preference\" turns into Δχ² = +1.45. All late-time fits assume a fifth-force shield that the paper finally concedes cannot be placed in any string setting it examined. A mid-tier journal could not accept a manuscript whose abstract disagrees with its own results section.\n\n**3. Major issues**\n\n1. **The abstract does not match the results.**\n   - The abstract lists n_s ≈ 0.963–0.965 as a \"frozen prediction\" but omits that PREREG_NS_SPA scored it INCONSISTENT (Δχ² = 11.7 against a threshold of 9).\n   - It says the δ-term \"kicks the angle\", but Sec. 4 replaces that with a hidden field σ (\"What works is a separate hidden field σ\").\n   - It says the model \"produces the observed baryon asymmetry\", but m_σ is fixed by that same asymmetry (\"its one free input… is fixed by the observed amount of matter\"), so this is a fit, not a prediction.\n   - **Fix:** rewrite the abstract from the final state of the work only.\n\n2. **String embedding: TWIST-K contradicts the later sections.**\n   - TWIST-K says worldsheet instantons are \"available in the heterotic and type IIA settings where matter is neutral, which removes the conflict found at the string level\". The next sections say \"No string setting examined keeps ordinary matter neutral\", that today's point has g_s ≈ 26 (or ≥ 3), and that the shield \"stays an effective-theory assumption\".\n   - TWIST-K says n \"corresponds to the resummed instanton sum… a precise route to deriving it\". Item (iii) of the evening section says \"Instanton sums cannot supply the cliff depth n\".\n   - **Fix:** keep one position. On the paper's own evidence, that position is \"no known UV completion; n is tuned\".\n\n3. **Hidden SU(8) versus TWIST-K.**\n   - Sec. 2 says e^{−πT/4} \"is the exponent of an SU(8) condensate\". Sec. 3 has \"The hidden SU(8) confines near 10¹⁴ GeV\". Sec. 4 uses SU(8) gaugino condensation for the kick. Addendum assumption 4 needs a Z₂ racetrack coupled to SU(8).\n   - Correction 2 then says the SU(8) coupling is strong throughout inflation, so the construction is outside calculational control. TWIST-K says \"No SU(8)… needed\", yet keeps the hidden-gauge construction \"as an alternative\".\n   - It is unclear whether the kick sector still needs SU(8) under TWIST-K, and if so, whether it has the same strong-coupling problem.\n   - **Fix:** choose one construction and state which sectors survive.\n\n4. **Parameter values are inconsistent.**\n   - **μ:** μ = 10⁴ in the Sec. 2 table; Sec. 5 later adopts μ ≲ 1 \"noting that the expansion data already seen favour it\". That is a post-data choice.\n   - **n:** given as 124 (Sec. 2, 9), 125 (Sec. 2 table, 9, addendum), 127 (Sec. 5) and 10 for the numerics.\n   - **Hidden mass m_σ:** 1.3×10¹¹, ~8×10⁹, 3×10¹⁰, 1.5×10¹⁰, ~10¹⁰ and 6×10¹⁰ GeV. The addendum's \"pre-registered check… at m_σ = 6×10⁹\" uses a value Sec. 4 says was withdrawn.\n   - **Heat share:** 0.13%, 0.8% and 0.01%.\n   - **Dark-matter mass:** 1.4–6.6×10⁶ GeV (Sec. 6), 1–6×10⁸ GeV (Sec. 5 iii), 100–500 TeV (Sec. 5 iv and addendum).\n   - **Field excursion:** Δφ ≈ 20 M_P (Sec. 9) versus 16 M_P (addendum).\n   - **Fix:** one parameter table, with every number in the text taken from it. The text currently points to an external \"model card\" as the \"single source of truth\", which is not acceptable for a paper.\n\n5. **n_s and r are quoted inconsistently.**\n   - At N* = 55.6–55.7 with instant reheating, the paper gives n_s 0.9653 / r 0.0034 (Sec. 3 table) and n_s 0.9642 / r 0.0038 (Sec. 2 and the consistency pass).\n   - It also gives 0.9648 / 0.0036 \"in every case tested\", and 0.9654 / 0.0035 at N* = 57.6.\n   - The loop shift is +0.002, then \"0.965–0.968 with loops\", then \"0.964–0.965 once the loop shift is recomputed\".\n   - The frozen r band (0.0037–0.0040) is leading-order 12/N², and the \"full calculation\" falls below it.\n   - **Fix:** one definitive N*(reheating) calculation, with the loop correction either computed or excluded.\n\n6. **The late-time \"PREFERRED\" result rests on post-data choices.**\n   - The original cliff failed its locked test (TAIL-F, Δχ² = +29.2). The cliff was then changed (\"a choice, not a derivation\").\n   - With the original μ = 10⁴, the theory's tail is \"almost a cosmological constant\". μ ≲ 1 was then chosen on data already seen.\n   - The PURE-TWIST rule was written after SPIRAL0 and KAPPA-ALPHA1 were known. At least seven variants appear in the table, plus TAIL-F and NS-PURE.\n   - The held-out improvements (−0.89, −0.41) are noise-level, and on the full dataset PURE-TWIST is worse than ΛCDM (Δχ² = +1.45).\n   - \"Passes all three pre-registered prediction tests\" in the abstract is therefore misleading.\n   - The paper gives a look-elsewhere correction for the flagship (0.2–1.0σ) but none for PURE-TWIST.\n   - **Fix:** apply a trials correction to PURE-TWIST, and describe μ and the cliff form as post-data.\n\n7. **The fifth force is unresolved, so the late-time fits are conditional.**\n   - Sec. 9 proposes a minimally coupled, non-supersymmetric Standard Model that \"passes Cassini\". Addendum assumption 1 says this \"removes the fifth force\". Correction 1 withdraws that. A symmetry shield is then introduced, and later declared to have no string realisation.\n   - The paper itself says unshielded couplings are β ≈ 0.41 (about 120× the Cassini bound). Every late-time fit therefore tests \"the tail's shape given a working shield\".\n   - The predicted dark-matter coupling β = 0 also relies on the same T-independence of masses that the shield must supply.\n   - **Fix:** state the shield as an explicit assumption in the abstract and in the predictions.\n\n8. **Gravitino and supersymmetry-breaking scale.**\n   - Sec. 6 escapes gravitino overproduction because \"a gravitino heavier than T_RH is simply not produced\".\n   - Sec. 9 has m_3/2 ≈ 10¹¹–10¹³ GeV with T_RH ≈ 3–5×10¹⁴ GeV, so the gravitino is lighter than T_RH and the escape fails.\n   - The addendum says W = 0 at S = 0 \"so the gravitino stays massless\".\n   - Separately, the argument relies on arXiv:1710.07341, which the authors say they read \"in abstract only\".\n   - **Fix:** settle what breaks supersymmetry and what m_3/2 is, then redo the gravitino argument.\n\n9. **Growth predictions contradict each other.**\n   - Sec. 5 says PURE-TWIST predicts \"E_G about 5% higher\". Sec. 7 says \"If PURE-TWIST (β = 0) is preferred, E_G would instead match ΛCDM\".\n   - For w_a, WA-CEILING gives PURE-TWIST \"−0.10\", while the CPL fits give −0.143 and −0.142.\n   - The BAO offsets in Sec. 7 (D_M +0.86% at z = 0.3; H 0.45–0.66% faster) differ from the addendum (+1.3%; H up to +1.9%). The two use different conditioning, but nothing in the text reconciles them.\n   - **Fix:** one set of observables at one stated conditioning.\n\n10. **Coupling definition is ambiguous.**\n    - The Immirzi parameter is mapped as 1/γ = y/x, but the chemical potential is written μ_A = c_A θ̇/M_P with θ = y.\n    - During kination x changes rapidly, so d(y/x)/dt and ẏ/x can differ a lot, and the \"curved-space boost\" depends on which is used.\n    - θ is not canonically normalised, so \"c_A is not a free parameter\" holds only after a normalisation choice the paper does not state.\n    - The yield also changes \"400× in the other frame\".\n    - **Fix:** write the Lagrangian explicitly in canonical variables.\n\n11. **Isocurvature: three estimates with different margins, then labelled unverified.**\n    - δσ/σ ≈ 10⁻⁵ \"safe\"; β_iso ≈ 3×10⁻⁴ \"far below 0.04\"; S_B ≈ 6×10⁻⁶ \"8× below\" the bound.\n    - Correction 4 then says this is unverified, and the angle's end point \"swings widely\".\n    - **Fix:** report one computed bound or none.\n\n12. **Sec. 9 is out of date with the rest of the paper.**\n    - \"Instant preheating is put in by hand… not modelled\" contradicts Sec. 4: \"Reheating need not be put in by hand\".\n    - \"Post-inflation history was not [checked at n = 125]\" contradicts Sec. 4's n = 125 decay analysis and the consistency pass.\n\n**4. Minor issues**\n\n- **Overloaded symbols:**\n  - r: tensor ratio and θ̇/(HM_P).\n  - β: dark-matter coupling, birefringence angle, isocurvature fraction, the TWIST-P Kähler coefficient, the graviton-rate prefactor and the fifth-force coupling.\n  - γ: kick phase, Immirzi parameter, superpotential exponent and growth index.\n  - α: attractor parameter, fine-structure constant, gauge coupling and running.\n  - a/A, κ, n and μ (μ_A versus μ).\n- Typos: \"w₀ − 0.90, w_a − 0.145\" (Sec. 2) is missing \"≈\"; \"m2\" should be \"m₂\".\n- Birefringence significance is 4.8σ in Sec. 6 and 4.9σ in Sec. 7; 0.277/0.057 = 4.86σ.\n- FLAG-1's DESI prediction is \"+12.5\" in the text but +10.88 in the table. Reconcile the test definitions.\n- Sec. 7 is titled frozen \"on 12 November\", but contains 13-November locks and a rule that is still \"drafted and awaits locking\".\n- Reference 5 has no authors. Reference 3 reads \"(link)\". Kaplunovsky–Louis, Carroll (1998), Zheng (2022), Garny et al., Kolda–Lyth, Frodden et al., the TCC paper and arXiv:2512.05108 are cited in the text but missing from the reference list.\n- Mir et al. is read \"abstract only\" yet used to claim novelty. Read it in full.\n- Code is \"available on request\". For a paper built on pre-registration, the repository, rule files and hashes should be public and archived.\n- Addenda come after the references. Merge them into the main text.\n- The definition of \"flagship\" moves between FLAG-1 and PURE-TWIST (Sec. 1 versus the abstract).\n- Sec. 3 uses \"N*\" without defining it; the pivot scale is never given.\n\n**5.
