Serapis

The space of all shapes one torus can take, drawn as a disk. Every tile is the same shape seen through the duality; the triangles fan out from the walls, and the field rests at the centre.

  • Square torus (centre). The resting point. Its leftover energy is Λ; the field here is heavy (4×10¹³ GeV), so it exerts no fifth force.
  • Triangle points. Where the torus is hexagonal. In Serapis each one is a wall the shape cannot sit on; every tile's sharp corner touches one.
  • Inflation path. The torus starts almost flat near the rim (stretched), rolls inward, inflation ends, and it settles at the centre.
  • Rim. Infinitely stretched shapes. Near it the energy is an almost constant plateau, which is what drives inflation.

The order of things

  1. Void: stretched torus near the rim, inflation. Im τ ≈ 126 → 2.1
  2. Plasma: the shape falls toward the square and its energy becomes heat.
  3. Hydrogen and helium form; sound rings through the plasma.
  4. Light is released (the CMB); stars and galaxies grow.
  5. Rest: square torus at the centre, Λ. n_s 0.9667 · r 0.0011

Drawn exactly: each pixel is a torus shape τ, mapped to the disk by (τ − i)/(τ + i) and reduced to the basic tile by the modular group; tile colour alternates with the number of reflections. Numbers from AEGIS D517 (SERAPIS-1). The other two tori of the six extra dimensions are heavy and not drawn.
