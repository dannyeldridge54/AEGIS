Subject: One-loop mass of a charged brane scalar in the T⁴/Z₂ anti-D5 (BSB) orientifold — sign and complex-structure dependence?

Dear Dr. [Name],

I'm an independent researcher working on a modular-invariant inflation model (SERAPIS; preprint 10.5281/zenodo.24289118). One candidate origin for its plateau needs a specific one-loop fact, and your work on brane supersymmetry breaking is the closest I have found.

The question: in the six-dimensional T⁴/Z₂ orientifold with anti-D5 branes (Antoniadis–Dudas–Sagnotti, hep-th/9908023), further compactified on a T² of complex structure U and area A, does a scalar localised on the anti-D5 stack and charged under its gauge group get a one-loop mass² that is (a) positive and (b) proportional to the weight-2 lattice sum Σ′ U₂²/|m + nU|⁴ divided by A?

Our field-theory estimate says yes: with a bosonic excess in the loop (the anti-D5 sector has no light gauginos), the brane scalar's mass from positive quartic couplings is +(N_B − N_F)·E(U,2)/A, the opposite sign to the one-loop vacuum energy (as in Kitazawa, 0805.0824), while Wilson-line scalars come out negative (hep-ph/0004091; 1909.03007). We have since computed the D̄5 Möbius amplitude with the full open-string tower for a D̄5 position scalar (annulus supersymmetric, Möbius with bosons and fermions of equal sign, Ω = (−1)^N on oscillators): the mass² comes out positive with E(U,2)/A² shape to 1% for A ≳ (10√α′)², by two independent numerical routes. We neglected T⁴ windings and did not fix the coupling normalisation, and we may be missing a string effect such as a traceless mass matrix (1105.0591) or a D9–D̄5 contribution.

If you know of a computation of this, or can see quickly whether our treatment misses something, I would be very grateful for a pointer. A one-line answer would help a great deal.
