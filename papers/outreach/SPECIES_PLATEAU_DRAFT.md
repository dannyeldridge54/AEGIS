Subject: Species-scale inflaton potential from the IIB R⁴ coefficient E(τ,3/2): can the dilaton carry it with a constant prefactor?

Dear Prof. [Name],

I'm an independent researcher working on a modular-invariant inflation model (SERAPIS; preprint 10.5281/zenodo.24289118). Your species-scale potential V ∝ G^{ττ̄}|∂_τ ln Λ_sp|² gives our best candidate for the plateau's origin, and I would value a pointer on one step.

What we did: we took the 10D IIB species scale Λ_QG = E(τ,3/2)^{−1/6} (van de Heisteeg–Vafa–Wiesner–Wu, 2310.07708) and built your potential along the axio-dilaton. Because E(τ,3/2) = 2ζ(3)y^{3/2} + 4ζ(2)y^{−1/2} + …, the plateau approaches its height as 1 − (16ζ(2)/3ζ(3))·(Im τ)^{−2} with no free shape parameter, and it vanishes at the desert point ρ. With a small rising correction proportional to Λ_QG^{−6} (= C·E(τ,3/2), C ≈ 8×10⁻⁷), an exact Mukhanov–Sasaki computation gives n_s = 0.9706, r = 5.4×10⁻⁴ at Im τ* ≈ 56 (g_s ≈ 0.018). We have not found this E(τ,3/2) version in the literature.

The step we cannot close: your one-loop result in 2507.05345 gives the potential with prefactor m_3/2². For the IIB dilaton in a modular-invariant supergravity, e^{G} goes like 1/Im τ at large Im τ (or exponentially), and in our checks any m_3/2² ∝ (Im τ)^{−q} with q ≳ 0.03 turns the plateau into a bump with too few e-folds. A gauged-shift D-term gives V ∝ (∂_y ln E)², which falls like (Im τ)^{−2}.

Questions:
1. Is there a known way for the IIB axio-dilaton to carry the species-gradient potential with an Im τ-independent prefactor (a different SUSY-breaking sector, a D-term, or a non-supersymmetric origin)?
2. Is there a natural source for a small correction proportional to Λ_sp^{−6} (or Λ_sp^{−4}) on the inflating background?

Even a one-line pointer would help a great deal.
