\begin{abstract}
This addendum brings the Twist-2 preprint~\cite{T2,T21} up to date. It lists every correction found after v1.1, the caveats on control of the string construction, results added since then, and prior art that should have been credited. None of the corrections reverses a scored test. Several narrow or qualify claims in the original text, and where this addendum and v1/v1.1 disagree, this addendum governs. The original PDFs are kept unchanged in the record.
\end{abstract}

\section{Corrections}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Dark radiation.} The gravitino and KK contribution is $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=0.02$--$0.06$, not 0.05--0.10. With the hidden Fayet--Iliopoulos (FI) sector the total is $\approx0.06$--$0.08$ (gravitino 0.047, KK 0.005--0.021, hidden 0.013).
\item \textbf{$\Lambda$ ``from the cliff''.} The cliff fixes the \emph{magnitude}, $e^{-n}$ with $n\approx124$. The O(1) factor is selected by the D7 flux integers (unit step 0.08\%); it is not predicted. The cliff value holds only after the usual one-off cancellation of the cosmological constant, and that cancellation must also absorb the soft vacuum energy of the $\chi$ condensate ($10^{26}$--$10^{32}$~GeV$^4$).
\item \textbf{Birefringence table.} ``$\beta_{\rm reio}/\beta_{\rm rec}<1$ if $m_a\gtrsim20H_0$'' is replaced by $\rho_\beta\lesssim0.8$ for $m_a\gtrsim30$--$40H_0$, and $\approx0$ for $m_a\ge100H_0$.
\item \textbf{Geometric control.} The inflationary point lies at sub-string volume ($\mathcal V\approx4\times10^{-6}$) in the original normalisation. With $\mathcal V=1$--10 at the CMB point, control is marginal rather than lost. The rescaling band is $a\approx3.8$--$7.9\times10^3$, since $\mathcal V=10$ at the CMB point is excluded by the KK tower. The string scale is $M_s\sim10^{14}$~GeV.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Consequences of a low string scale}
With $M_s\sim10^{14}$~GeV, baryon number must be protected against proton decay, and there is no GUT unification below $M_s$. Dark matter must be stable by a gauged discrete symmetry. An axion mass needs a cycle with $\tau\approx40$, so at least three K\"ahler moduli are required.

\section{Additions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Gravitino window.} $1.7\lesssim m_{3/2}\le4.7$~eV ($\sqrt F\approx65$--140~TeV), from $m_h=125$~GeV gauge mediation and cosmology, with at most 5--6 messengers if $\alpha_3$ is to stay perturbative up to $M_s$. An eV gravitino requires a strongly coupled messenger sector.
\item \textbf{Hidden decay channel.} The field $x$ decays to heavy hidden $\psi$ ($5\times10^5$--$10^9$~GeV), which must decay through an $L$-conserving, U(1)$_X$-neutral lepton portal ($y'\gtrsim5\times10^{-3}$ keeps the hidden sector thermal at capture). The sgoldstino is heavy through strong coupling.
\item \textbf{Route A band.} $r=0.0042$--0.0050, rising with $n_s$ (the opposite trend to Starobinsky), with $\alpha_s=-(7.1$--$8.4)\times10^{-4}$ and $\epsilon$ in $(2.0$--$4.0)\times10^{-7}$.
\item \textbf{Capture origin (conditional).} A candidate string origin for the capture coupling is a moduli-dependent FI term on magnetised D7-branes. A toy model captures $x$ and pins it at $x_E$; the small-$x$ obstacle is stated.
\item \textbf{Birefringence forecast.} LiteBIRD's $\sigma(\Delta\beta)\approx0.05^\circ$ gives a 2--5.5$\sigma$ signal for axion masses $m\gtrsim50$--$100H_0$ and nothing for $m\lesssim10H_0$~\cite{LB}.
\end{itemize}

\section{Prior art that should have been credited}
Route A's $(n_s,r)$ lies on the $\alpha\approx2$ line of fibre inflation~\cite{fibre}. The Twist differs through its $\Lambda$--capture link and its $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ and $\beta$ predictions. D-term fixing of K\"ahler-moduli combinations by magnetised D7 FI terms~\cite{dterm} and moduli trapping at enhanced symmetry points~\cite{trap} underlie the capture mechanism. Exponentially small $\Lambda$ from flux vacua~\cite{smallL} precedes the cliff idea. Planckian interacting dark matter~\cite{PIDM} precedes the gravity-only DM. The 4.7~eV gravitino bound is from Ref.~\cite{grav}, the strongly coupled messenger requirement is discussed in Ref.~\cite{msg}, and a 21-cm test is proposed in Ref.~\cite{21}.

\section{Status}
Later work in this project moved the main inflation model to SERAPIS-$E$~\cite{SE}. Twist-2 is kept as a record of the capture route and of its pre-registered tests. The corrections above are what a reader should apply to v1/v1.1.
