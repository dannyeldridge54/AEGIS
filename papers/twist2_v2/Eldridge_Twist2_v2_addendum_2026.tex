\begin{abstract}
This addendum brings the Twist-2 preprint~\cite{T2,T21} up to date. It lists every correction found after v1.1, the caveats on control of the string construction, results added since then, and prior art that should have been credited. None of the corrections reverses a scored test. Several narrow or qualify claims in the original text, and where this addendum and v1/v1.1 disagree, this addendum governs. The original PDFs are kept unchanged in the record.
\end{abstract}

\section{Corrections}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Dark radiation.} The printed total $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=0.05$--$0.10$ is replaced. The light gravitino gives $\Delta N_{\rm eff}=0.047$ ($0.02$--$0.06$ over the range of $g_*$ at its decoupling). Gravitationally decaying KK modes add at most $0.005$--$0.021$ per light species (worst case, all into dark radiation), giving $0.05$--$0.07$. The hidden Fayet--Iliopoulos (FI) sector adds at most $0.013$ at its energy-share ceiling, for a total of at most $\approx0.065$--$0.081$. In the rescaled geometry the hidden share is computed to lie far below that ceiling.
\item \textbf{$\Lambda$ ``from the cliff''.} The cliff fixes the \emph{magnitude}, $e^{-n}$ with $n\approx124$. The O(1) factor is selected by the D7 flux integers (one flux unit moves $\Lambda$ by as little as 0.08\% in a toy model); it is not predicted. The cliff value holds only after the usual one-off cancellation of the cosmological constant, and that cancellation must also absorb the soft vacuum energy of the $\chi$ condensate ($10^{26}$--$10^{32}$~GeV$^4$).
\item \textbf{Birefringence table.} ``$\beta_{\rm reio}/\beta_{\rm rec}<1$ if $m_a\gtrsim20H_0$'' is replaced by $\rho_\beta\lesssim0.8$ for $m_a\gtrsim30$--$40H_0$, and $\approx0$ for $m_a\ge100H_0$.
\item \textbf{Geometric control.} The inflationary point lies at sub-string volume ($\mathcal V\approx4\times10^{-6}$) in the original normalisation. Rescaling by $a\approx3.8$--$7.9\times10^3$ gives $\mathcal V\approx1$--$2.9$ at the CMB point, where control is marginal rather than lost. The safer $\mathcal V=10$ is excluded, because the KK tower would then be thermally produced ($m_{\rm KK}/T\approx1.3$). The string scale is $M_s\sim10^{14}$~GeV.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Consequences of a low string scale}
With $M_s\sim10^{14}$~GeV, baryon number must be protected against proton decay, and there is no GUT unification below $M_s$. Dark matter must be stable by a gauged discrete symmetry. An axion mass needs a cycle with $\tau\approx40$, so at least three K\"ahler moduli are required.

\section{Additions}
\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Gravitino window.} $1.7\lesssim m_{3/2}\le4.7$~eV ($\sqrt F\approx85$--140~TeV). The upper end is the cosmological bound~\cite{grav}. The lower end follows because a gluino above 2.3~TeV in minimal gauge mediation needs more messengers at lower $\sqrt F$, and $\alpha_3$ stays perturbative up to $M_s$ only for at most 5--6 messengers. Independently, $m_h=125$~GeV in low-scale gauge mediation needs $m_{3/2}\gtrsim1$~eV even with a strongly coupled messenger sector~\cite{msg}.
\item \textbf{Hidden decay channel.} The field $x$ decays to heavy hidden $\psi$ ($5\times10^5$--$10^9$~GeV), which must decay through an $L$-conserving, U(1)$_X$-neutral lepton portal ($y'\gtrsim5\times10^{-3}$ keeps the hidden sector thermal at capture). The sgoldstino is heavy through strong coupling.
\item \textbf{Route A band.} Over $n_s=0.9706$--0.9766, $r=0.0042$--0.0050, rising with $n_s$ (the opposite trend to Starobinsky), with $\alpha_s=-(7.1$--$8.4)\times10^{-4}$ and $\epsilon$ in $(2.0$--$4.0)\times10^{-7}$. The running does not separate Route A from fibre inflation (0.2$\sigma$ for $\sigma(\alpha_s)\approx2\times10^{-3}$).
\item \textbf{Capture origin (conditional).} A candidate string origin for the capture coupling is a moduli-dependent FI term on magnetised D7-branes. A toy model captures $x$ and pins it at $x_E$; the small-$x$ obstacle is stated.
\item \textbf{Birefringence forecast.} With LiteBIRD's headline $\sigma(\Delta\beta)\approx0.05^\circ$~\cite{LB} and $\beta_{\rm rec}=0.28^\circ$, the reionisation--recombination difference is a 2--5.5$\sigma$ signal for axion masses $m\gtrsim50$--$100H_0$ (1--3$\sigma$ with foreground residuals), and nothing for $m\lesssim10H_0$. The Twist does not fix $m$, so this is a conditional test.
\end{itemize}

\section{Prior art that should have been credited}
Route A's $(n_s,r)$ lies near the $\alpha\approx2$ line of fibre inflation, $r\approx3\alpha(1-n_s)^2$~\cite{fibre}; across the band the effective $\alpha$ runs from 1.6 to 3.0. The Twist differs through its $\Lambda$--capture link and its $\Delta N_{\rm eff}$ and $\beta$ predictions. D-term fixing of K\"ahler-moduli combinations by magnetised D7 FI terms~\cite{dterm} and moduli trapping at enhanced symmetry points~\cite{trap} underlie the capture mechanism. Exponentially small $\Lambda$ from flux vacua~\cite{smallL} precedes the cliff idea. Planckian interacting massive particles~\cite{PIDM} precede the gravity-only DM. The 4.7~eV gravitino bound is from Ref.~\cite{grav}, the $m_h=125$~GeV lower limit and the strongly coupled messenger requirement are from Ref.~\cite{msg}, and a 21-cm test is proposed in Ref.~\cite{21}.

\section{Status}
Later work in this project moved the main inflation model to SERAPIS-$E$~\cite{SE}. Twist-2 is kept as a record of the capture route and of its pre-registered tests. The corrections above are what a reader should apply to v1/v1.1.
