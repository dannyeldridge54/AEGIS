# Twist-2 v2 addendum — referee response

## 18 Nov pass

Referee and editor pass on `Eldridge_Twist2_v2_addendum_2026.tex`. No new results. Numbers were checked against D422, D430, D446, D453, D456, D458, D463, D467, D469–D475, D484, D485 and D511 in `theory/pending_docs/D72_D75_weak_spots.md`. References were checked against arXiv.

**Verdict:** minor revision. There were two internal inconsistencies (one correction item and one parameter conversion), several underspecified forecasts, and incomplete references.

### Numbers verified (unchanged)
- Λ cliff n ≈ 124, χ condensate 10²⁶–10³² GeV⁴ (D427/D456). ρ_β ≲ 0.8 for m ≳ 30–40 H0, ≈ 0 for m ≥ 100 H0 (D414 correction).
- 𝒱 ≈ 4e-6 original, a ≈ 3.8–7.9e3, M_s ~ 1e14 GeV (D452/D453). Hidden ψ 5e5–1e9 GeV, y′ ≳ 5e-3 (D422/D425).
- Route A r 0.0042–0.0050, α_s −(7.1–8.4)e-4, ε (2.0–4.0)e-7 (D434). Birefringence 2–5.5σ (D484). τ ≈ 40 with three Kähler moduli (D471/D472).

### Changes
1. **Dark radiation (correction item 1; wording of a headline number).** The item said "gravitino and KK contribution is 0.02–0.06". That range is the gravitino alone (0.047, 0.017–0.059 over g*; D422/D430). Gravitino plus KK is 0.052–0.068 (D458/D511), so the old sentence was internally inconsistent with its own breakdown. The item was rewritten: gravitino 0.047 (0.02–0.06), plus KK ≤ 0.005–0.021 per light species, gives 0.05–0.07. The hidden FI sector adds *at most* 0.013 (a ceiling, D463), for a total of at most ≈ 0.065–0.081. It also notes that D467 finds the hidden share far below the ceiling.
2. **Gravitino window.** "√F ≈ 65–140 TeV" did not match 1.7–4.7 eV. 65 TeV is 1.0 eV (the D430 window), and 1.7 eV is 85 TeV (D474). Corrected to **√F ≈ 85–140 TeV**. The two sources of the lower bound were also separated: D474's messenger count and gluino bound give 1.7 eV, and Ibe–Yanagida's m_h = 125 GeV gives ≳ 1 eV.
3. Geometric control: "𝒱 = 1–10 … 𝒱 = 10 excluded" contradicted itself. It now says the band gives 𝒱 ≈ 1–2.9 and 𝒱 = 10 is excluded by the KK tower (m_KK/T ≈ 1.3) (D453).
4. Flux step: "unit step 0.08%" became "as little as 0.08% in a toy model" (D456; other flux units move Λ by 12–14%).
5. Birefringence forecast: added the β_rec = 0.28° assumption, the 1–3σ figure with foreground residuals, and that the test is conditional because m is not fixed (D484).
6. Route A: added its n_s span. The "α ≈ 2 line" was qualified: across the band the effective α in r = 3α(1−n_s)² runs 1.6–3.0 (arithmetic on D434's numbers). Added D475's result that α_s does not separate Route A from fibre inflation.
7. References completed and verified. 1603.06789 is Burgess–Cicoli–de Alwis–Quevedo. 1110.3333 is Cicoli–Mayrhofer–Valandro, JHEP 02 (2012) 062. 1310.4751 is Enomoto–Iida–Maekawa–Matsuda. 2107.09064/5 are Demirtas et al. 1601.07386 is Osato et al. 1608.01610 is Ibe–Yanagida. 1605.09191 is Oyama–Kawasaki. 2108.09287 is Sherwin–Namikawa.
8. **PIDM credit fixed.** 1709.09688 is Garny–*Palessandro*–Sandora–Sloth (the old entry omitted an author). The original PIMP paper, Garny–Sandora–Sloth PRL 116 (2016) 101302 (1511.03278), was added. "Planckian interacting dark matter" became the literature's name, "Planckian interacting massive particles".
9. Method note range: D422–D484 became D422–D485 (D485 is the source of the 0.065–0.081 total).
