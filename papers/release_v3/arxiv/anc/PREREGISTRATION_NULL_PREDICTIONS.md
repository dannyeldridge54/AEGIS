# Pre-registration: the minimal Locked Twist's two zero predictions

**Written:** 2026-10-31 21:16 CT (time from the clock).
**Locking:** SHA-256 hashed and committed when written; never edited afterwards.
**Model:** the minimal Locked Twist, the Barbero-Immirzi field from the Holst action with no photon coupling and no
non-minimal coupling to curvature (paper, sections on predictions; HELPFUL_FINDS.md).

## Prediction 1: no cosmic birefringence from the early field

The minimal model has no tree-level phi F Ftilde coupling; a loop-induced one is suppressed by about (omega/m_e)^2 ~ 3e-13
(paper). Predicted isotropic rotation of CMB polarisation from the field: |beta| < 1e-9 degrees, effectively 0.

**Already known when written:** ACT DR6 reports a mean rotation of 0.20 +/- 0.08 degrees, and Planck/WMAP analyses 0.34 +/-
0.09 degrees. These are dominated by the uncertainty in the instruments' absolute polarisation angle.

**Rule (the first analysis that meets the test standard decides):**
- **Test standard:** a measurement of the isotropic rotation that the collaboration itself states is calibrated
  *independently of the CMB EB signal* (for example with a ground or celestial calibrator, or a known-angle source),
  from the Simons Observatory, ACT, SPT, BICEP/Keck or LiteBIRD.
- **FAIL** (the minimal model is ruled out as the early field): that measurement excludes beta = 0 at 3 sigma or more,
  *and* the collaboration attributes it to a cosmological (not instrumental) origin.
- **PASS:** a measurement at that standard with an uncertainty of 0.05 degrees or better is consistent with beta = 0
  within 2 sigma.
- **Otherwise:** open.

**Scope:** a non-zero beta from a *late-time* source (for example a separate dark-energy axion) does not by itself
falsify the early field. That would be stated plainly, and not used as an escape: the collaboration's attribution
decides.

## Prediction 2: no gravitational-wave friction

The minimal dynamical Holst field leaves gravitational-wave propagation as in GR (Gao et al., arXiv:2608.19392). So
the standard-siren friction parameter is Xi_0 = 1 exactly.

**Rule:**
- **FAIL:** a standard-siren analysis from LIGO-Virgo-KAGRA, Einstein Telescope, Cosmic Explorer or LISA excludes
  Xi_0 = 1 at 3 sigma or more.
- **PASS:** a result with sigma(Xi_0) <= 0.05 consistent with 1 within 2 sigma.
- **Otherwise:** open.

**Known when written:** GWTC-3 dark sirens give Xi_0 = 1.2 +/- 0.7, which is open.
