Title:
Torsion-Motivated Early Dark Energy with an Onset Locked to Matter-Radiation Equality: Pre-Registered Tests on Planck, ACT, SPT-3G and DESI

Authors:
Danny Lee Eldridge

Abstract (1736 of 1920 characters):
Promoting the Barbero-Immirzi parameter of Einstein-Cartan-Holst gravity to a field gives a canonical, ghost-free pseudoscalar. With an assumed potential $V=\Lambda^4[1-\cos(\phi/F)]^3$ it is axion-like early dark energy (EDE); we fix the peak of its energy fraction at matter-radiation equality, leaving two extra parameters, and test it with full Boltzmann codes and decision rules hashed before each fit. On Planck + ACT DR6 + DESI DR2 + Pantheon+ it improves on $\Lambda$CDM by $\Delta\chi^2=-7.23$; on SPT-3G D1 alone no version improves meaningfully (best $\Delta\chi^2=-1.27$). On all three CMB data sets combined with DESI DR2 and Pantheon+ it passes its pre-registered rule ($\Delta\chi^2=-5.63$, $f_{\rm EDE}=0.070$, $H_0=70.15$), but late evolving dark energy ($w_0$-$w_a$) fits better ($\Delta\chi^2=-10.89$; $\Delta$AIC lower by 5.3). A profile likelihood prefers $f_{\rm EDE}\approx0.05$-$0.10$ (68\%), with $f=0$ disfavoured by $\Delta\chi^2=5.98$; because the initial angle is undefined at $f_{\rm EDE}=0$, this is an upper estimate of the significance, and a look-elsewhere correction and a joint early-plus-late fit are pre-registered and running. The data favour the flat-bottomed $n=3$ shape over $n=2$, which we show is not produced by the Nieh-Yan term or by natural instanton or anomaly potentials, and the equality lock cannot be derived from the minimal action. Two codes agree to 0.4 in $\chi^2$ and the field does not fragment. Fixed-number variants of the width and initial angle all fail. We outline a late-time alternative in which the same field, with the natural $n=1$ potential and a Planck-scale range, acts as thawing dark energy. We state which parts are derived, which are assumed, and which failed.
