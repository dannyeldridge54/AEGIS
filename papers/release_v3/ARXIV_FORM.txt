Title:
Torsion-Motivated Early Dark Energy with an Onset Locked to Matter-Radiation Equality: Pre-Registered Tests on Planck, ACT, SPT-3G and DESI

Authors:
Danny Lee Eldridge

Abstract (1787 of 1920 characters):
Promoting the Barbero-Immirzi parameter of Einstein-Cartan-Holst gravity to a field gives a canonical, ghost-free pseudoscalar. With an assumed potential $V=\Lambda^4[1-\cos(\phi/F)]^3$ it is axion-like early dark energy (EDE); we fix the peak of its energy fraction at matter-radiation equality, leaving two extra parameters, and test it with full Boltzmann codes and decision rules hashed before each fit. On Planck + ACT DR6 + DESI DR2 + Pantheon+ it improves on $\Lambda$CDM by $\Delta\chi^2=-7.23$; on SPT-3G D1 alone no version improves meaningfully (best $\Delta\chi^2=-1.27$). On all three CMB data sets combined with DESI DR2 and Pantheon+ it passes its pre-registered rule ($\Delta\chi^2=-5.63$, $f_{\rm EDE}=0.070$, $H_0=70.15$), but late evolving dark energy ($w_0$-$w_a$) fits better ($\Delta\chi^2=-10.89$). A profile likelihood prefers $f_{\rm EDE}\approx0.05$-$0.10$ (68\%), but after a look-elsewhere correction for the initial angle the global significance is only $1.2$-$1.4\sigma$, a weak hint. Fitted together with late dark energy, early energy improves $\chi^2$ by 3.65 but does not pay for its two parameters. The favoured flat-bottomed $n=3$ shape is not produced by the Nieh-Yan term or by natural instanton or anomaly potentials, and the equality lock cannot be derived from the minimal action. Two codes agree to 0.4 in $\chi^2$. Acting late with its natural $n=1$ potential, on code corrected for two AxiCLASS bugs we found, the field ties $\Lambda$CDM with Pantheon+, gains a weak, sample-dependent $\Delta\chi^2=-6.2$ with DES Y5, and never beats $w_0$-$w_a$. The torsion-natural dark-matter coupling is excluded, and the minimal field cannot produce the measured cosmic birefringence. We state which parts are derived, which are assumed, and which failed.
