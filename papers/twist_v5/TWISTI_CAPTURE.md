# TWIST-I: how the Twist field is captured after matter genesis (consolidated from D355–D377, 14 Nov 2026)

Exploratory proposal; not a locked prediction. AI-assisted. Rule: field_tests/rules/PREREG_TWISTI.md + DEVIATIONS D1–D8.

## Why capture is needed
A rolling field with K ⊃ −3 ln(T + T̄) cannot keep the Standard Model constants fixed today (one-loop no-go, D323–D345). In TWIST-I the Twist field x
rolls exactly as in v5 through inflation, reheating and matter genesis (to x ≈ 30, T ≈ 2×10¹³ GeV), and is then frozen before nucleosynthesis.
Dark energy is a cosmological constant.

## What capture must achieve
- x must decay or be held so that no long-lived oscillation is left. A coherent residual displacement Δφ dilutes n_B/s and DM by
  ≈ (Δφ²/6M_P²)(m/H)²T_osc/T_decay, which requires Δφ ≲ 10⁻⁷–10⁻⁵ M_P (D359–D360a, numerically checked).
- x's kinetic energy is harmless if capture is late (dilution ∝ T_c³; x carries Ω_kin ≈ 0.02–0.17 at 2×10¹³ GeV, D362; D367 will compute it).
- The mass at the minimum must be ≳ 10⁵ GeV so that the remnants decay before BBN (D357).

## The construction
W ⊃ (T − T_E) (λ M_P χχ̄ + k Y),  with χ, χ̄ at modular weight 3/2 and Y at modular weight 3.
1. Trapping (D363–D364): χ is massless at T = T_E. As x rolls through, χ particles are produced and stop x within d ≈ φ̇²/(2g n_χ) of T_E
   (10⁻⁸–10⁻⁵ M_P for crossing at T ≲ 10¹⁰–10¹² GeV, canonical g = λ x_E √(2/3) ≳ 0.1). The kinetic energy goes into χ, which decays to radiation.
2. Stabilisation (D373, exact, sympy): at Y = 0, V = k²|T − T_E|², with no e^K tilt because of the weight-3 cancellation. Its minimum is exactly
   at the trapping point because both come from the same factor (D375). m_x² ≈ (4/3)k²x_E².
3. Thermal hold (D371): the χ thermal mass keeps x at T_E until T ~ m̃_χ. Below that, k must beat the χ-loop negative mass:
   m_x ≳ 6×10⁴ GeV for λ = 1, m̃_χ = 10⁵ GeV (D366, D375).
4. Loop tilts on the minimum are ≲ 10⁻³¹ M_P (D374).

## Bounds
- 0.004 ≲ λ ≲ 0.03 for x_E ≈ 30: trapping efficiency, and m_χ ≤ M_P during inflation; the effect on n_s and r is negligible (D377).
- m_x ≳ 10⁵ GeV; T_E inside x's remaining roll range (x_E ≳ 30).

## Consequences
- Entropy release at capture: Δ ~ 1.5–3. This is absorbed by refitting m_σ (≈ 3.4×10¹⁰ GeV at Δ = 2), and it raises m_DM by Δ (200–400 TeV), which
  favours a suppressed or zero gravity-portal coupling (D376).
- The CMB predictions are Starobinsky-class at N* 55.7–57.6 (D368–D370). w = −1. β = 0.
