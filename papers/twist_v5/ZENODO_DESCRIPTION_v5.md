- One construction is kept: TWIST-K.
- The gravitino argument is corrected.
- The tail-slope and reheating-temperature numbers are corrected.
- The axial coupling is written explicitly in canonical variables.
- Growth, w_a and BAO numbers are each given at a stated conditioning.
- Missing references are added.
