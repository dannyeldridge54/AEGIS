Title: The Twist: inflation, matter and dark energy from one supergravity field — a pre-registered test programme

Description:
We study whether one field — the Barbero–Immirzi parameter promoted to an N = 1 supergravity superfield with the no-scale
Kähler potential K = −3 ln(T + T̄) (Gates, Ketov & Yunes 2009) — can run inflation, make the matter–antimatter asymmetry,
and act as today's dark energy. Its size half gives α = 1 plateau inflation (locked prediction n_s = 0.963–0.965,
r = 0.0034–0.0040; with the kination phase after the cliff the best estimate is n_s ≈ 0.965–0.968, r ≈ 0.0035). A cliff ends
inflation; instant preheating gives T_RH ≈ 2–4×10¹⁴ GeV; a hidden SU(8) axion kicks the twist angle and lepto-axiogenesis
makes the observed baryon asymmetry for a hidden mass ≈ 5–6×10⁹ GeV; dark matter is a gravity-only fermion. The field's
tail is thawing dark energy with slope √(2/3) and no dark-matter coupling (PURE-TWIST): w₀ ≈ −0.90, w_a ≈ −0.15. Every data
test was fixed and SHA-256 hashed before its fit. PURE-TWIST fits DESI DR2 BAO + supernovae + CMB better than ΛCDM with no
extra parameters (Δχ² −3.4 compressed; −3.85 with full Planck PR4) and beats ΛCDM on DES Y5, Union3 and DES-Dovekie
supernovae. Results against the model are reported at full size: a locked test of the full model with its original cliff
shape failed (Δχ² +29); the cliff was reshaped afterwards (stated as a post-hoc change); look-elsewhere correction leaves the
late-time preference at 0.7–1.7σ globally; and the main open problem — a fifth force on ordinary matter from the light
tail, with no known shielding — is stated in full. Frozen predictions for DESI DR3, Euclid, LiteBIRD and Simons Observatory
are listed. Scripts, locked rules and every number: https://github.com/dannyeldridge54/AEGIS (REVIEW_GUIDE.md).

We study whether one field — the Barbero–Immirzi parameter promoted to an N = 1 supergravity superfield with the no-scale
Kähler potential K = −3 ln(T + T̄) (Gates, Ketov & Yunes 2009) — can run inflation, make the matter–antimatter asymmetry,
and act as today's dark energy. Plateau inflation gives n_s ≈ 0.965 (0.965–0.968 with loops) and r ≈ 0.0035. A cliff
exp(−n(1 − e^{−πT/4})) ends inflation; its 4/π is assumed to come from a hidden SU(8) (strongly coupled during inflation, so
not under calculational control), and the superpotential can be obtained from a Z₂ racetrack of two hidden gauge groups that
keeps the gravitino massless; the cliff depth n ≈ 125 is chosen — a tuning of today's dark energy. Instant
preheating heats ~0.8% of the energy; a hidden SU(8) axion kicks the twist angle and lepto-axiogenesis makes the observed
baryon asymmetry for a hidden mass ≈ 6×10¹⁰ GeV (refit after a missed prediction; confirmation running); dark matter is a gravity-only fermion (mass estimate not
yet stable).
The tail is thawing dark energy: w₀ ≈ −0.90, w_a ≈ −0.14, insensitive to the heat share. Every data test was fixed and
SHA-256 hashed before its fit. PURE-TWIST fits DESI DR2 BAO + supernovae + CMB better than ΛCDM with no extra parameters
(Δχ² −3.4 compressed; −3.85 with full Planck PR4) and beats ΛCDM on DES Y5, Union3 and DES-Dovekie supernovae; the
look-elsewhere correction leaves 0.7–1.7σ globally. Results against the model are reported at full size: the full model with
its original cliff failed a locked test (Δχ² +29) and the cliff was reshaped afterwards (stated as post hoc); a pre-registered
matter check missed its prediction (7.5× against 2–3×); two hypotheses (γ = 1/4, a derivation of n) failed. Seven added
assumptions are listed in a model card. The main open problem is a fifth force on ordinary matter: removing it requires an
unexplained symmetry forbidding couplings between the tail field and the Standard Model. The late-time test is generic thawing
quintessence with slope √(2/3). An independent referee-style review is summarised in the repository. The model's n_s ≈ 0.965 sits ~1σ from CMB-SPA alone but 2–3σ from CMB + DESI BAO — INCONSISTENT on the locked NS-SPA test: fixing n_s at 0.964 costs Δχ² = 11.7 against CMB-SPA + DESI (ΛCDM at the same n_s: 8.5); on that full data set PURE-TWIST with n_s free also fits slightly worse than ΛCDM (Δχ² +1.45); a separate model,
TWIST-P (a different Kähler potential, built after seeing this data), was locked before any test and predicts n_s 0.965–0.971
with r 0.0036–0.011, to be scored on future CMB data only. Frozen predictions for
DESI DR3, Euclid, LiteBIRD and Simons Observatory are listed. Scripts, locked rules and every number:
https://github.com/dannyeldridge54/AEGIS (REVIEW_GUIDE.md, papers/MODEL_CARD_v5.md).
