Title: The Twist: inflation, matter and dark energy from one supergravity field — a pre-registered test programme

Description:
We study whether one field — the Barbero–Immirzi parameter promoted to an N = 1 supergravity superfield with the no-scale
Kähler potential K = −3 ln(T + T̄) (Gates, Ketov & Yunes 2009) — can run inflation, make the matter–antimatter asymmetry,
and act as today's dark energy. Its size half gives α = 1 plateau inflation (locked prediction n_s = 0.963–0.965,
r = 0.0034–0.0040; with the kination phase after the cliff the best estimate is n_s ≈ 0.965–0.968, r ≈ 0.0035). A cliff ends
inflation; instant preheating gives T_RH ≈ 2–4×10¹⁴ GeV; a hidden SU(8) axion kicks the twist angle and lepto-axiogenesis
makes the observed baryon asymmetry for a hidden mass ≈ 5–6×10⁹ GeV; dark matter is a gravity-only fermion. The field's
tail is thawing dark energy with slope √(2/3) and no dark-matter coupling (PURE-TWIST): w₀ ≈ −0.90, w_a ≈ −0.15. Every data
test was fixed and SHA-256 hashed before its fit. PURE-TWIST fits DESI DR2 BAO + supernovae + CMB better than ΛCDM with no
extra parameters (Δχ² −3.4 compressed; −3.85 with full Planck PR4) and beats ΛCDM on DES Y5, Union3 and DES-Dovekie
supernovae. Results against the model are reported at full size: a locked test of the full model with its original cliff
shape failed (Δχ² +29); the cliff was reshaped afterwards (stated as a post-hoc change); look-elsewhere correction leaves the
late-time preference at 0.7–1.7σ globally; and the main open problem — a fifth force on ordinary matter from the light
tail, with no known shielding — is stated in full. Frozen predictions for DESI DR3, Euclid, LiteBIRD and Simons Observatory
are listed. Scripts, locked rules and every number: https://github.com/dannyeldridge54/AEGIS (REVIEW_GUIDE.md).
