## How it relates to the current Weave/SERAPIS line (plainly)
- **Different physics, no shared equations.**
  - This paper promotes the Barbero–Immirzi parameter of Einstein–Cartan–Holst gravity to an axion-like field with an assumed [1 − cos]³ potential, acting around matter–radiation equality to raise H₀.
  - The current line has no torsion field, no early dark energy and no H₀ mechanism. Its components are an SL(2,Z)/species-scale inflationary plateau (10.5281/zenodo.24378141, SERAPIS v3.4), a postulated thawing "reverse thread" for late dark energy (10.5281/zenodo.24380387), and flavour and matter sectors built on modular symmetry.
- **Lineage only.** The project went from torsion EDE (Oct–Nov 3) to the Spiral Twist/FLAG-1 dilaton (10.5281/zenodo.24140195), then the Twist and Twist-2 (no-scale supergravity), then SERAPIS (modular τ), and finally the Weave. What carries over is the method: rules locked and hashed before fits, and failures reported. No result carries over.
- **TorsionΛ toolkit.** This is the related software of the same era. It is not the subject of this paper. A locked audit found that its background parameter β is exactly a relabelled Ω_m (E² ∝ Ω_m(1+β)). The audit also found that its H₀ = 73 configuration fails the Planck distance priors: ℓ_A is −75σ by D715 and about −190σ with first-principles recombination (D1025). TorsionΛ is therefore a teaching and consistency tool, not a cosmological model.
- **Echoes, not support.**
  - This paper found that its late n = 1 variant gains Δχ² ≈ −6 only with DES-Y5 and never beats w0wa. The current reverse thread shows the same pattern: meaningful only on DES-Y5 (CLASS −8.1), and trailing w0wa (D1725).
  - This paper found the torsion-natural dark-matter coupling excluded. The current thread's dark-matter coupling was also not detected (D1725).
  - These are separate tests, not confirmations of each other.
