The species-plateau realisation lies within 1.8σ of every current n_s (Planck 2018 +1.4σ, ACT DR6 +0.5, P-ACT −0.1, P-ACT-LB −1.1, LB2 −1.5, SPT-3G D1 +1.8, CMB-SPA +0.8, CMB-SPA+DESI −0.8; SERAPIS v3.4). r passes BK18, P-ACT+BK18 and PR4+BK18. This is the one strict pass in the Weave count.
- **One-loop sign (open step behind "only plateau direction"):** now computed. The thread is positive at D = 8 and D = 4, and at D = 6 and 5 for non-stringy tori. It is scheme-dependent only at string-size tori (D1672, D1680–D1683; rectangular B = 0 slice).
- **Endpoint ρ:** confirmed for the inflaton (D1606). It cannot also serve as the quark modulus: the leftover offset is ~1600× too small (D1727).
- **Reheating:** T_R is now fixed at 1.34×10⁹ GeV (κ = 0.12, D1665), against 8.94×10⁸ GeV used for N_* here. Estimated shift ΔN_* ≈ +0.1 and Δn_s ~ 10⁻⁴ [estimate, not computed]; v3.4 quotes N_* ≈ 50.4–50.6. This T_R over-produces winos via gravitinos, so the wino window is conditional on T_R ≲ 1.07×10⁹ GeV or later dilution (D1687, D1720).
- **The same plateau reused for dark energy** ("reverse thread") is supported over ΛCDM only with DES-Y5 and trails w₀w_a. A dark-matter coupling was not detected on full Planck (D1712, D1725).
- The species-gradient principle (step 1) remains an assumption.

## Addendum text (ready to typeset)
**Status update, 20 November 2026.** This note adds to, and does not replace, the original text.
*Errata.* (1) The dateline "(v1.1, draft)" should read "v1.1". (2) Reference [P2] is now published as doi:10.5281/zenodo.24379357; its TM2 neutrino prediction has since been excluded by JUNO (see that record). No equation or number in this paper is affected. We re-checked the plateau coefficient 7.2983, the slow-roll formulas, the untilted r values and g_s = 2/√3 at ρ.
*What changed.* We state plainly that n_s is fitted: the tilt C was chosen to place n_s at 0.97065, so n_s is not evidence. The predictions are r = 5.41×10⁻⁴ (D = 10) or 1.07×10⁻³ (D = 6), and α_s ≈ −9.7×10⁻⁴. The fitted n_s lies within 1.8σ of every current CMB determination. r is below all current bounds; in our strict count this r bound is the single pass (1 pass, 2 tension, 1 fail, 1 open). The one-loop sign behind the "only plateau direction" argument has been computed on the rectangular torus slice. It is positive, giving a plateau, at D = 8 and 4, and at D = 6 and 5 for tori not near string size. The endpoint ρ is confirmed for the inflaton, but it cannot double as the quark modulus. Reheating is now fixed at T_R ≈ 1.3×10⁹ GeV instead of 8.9×10⁸ GeV; we estimate that this moves N_* by about 0.1. The same plateau reused as dark energy is supported over ΛCDM only with DES-Y5 supernovae. The species-gradient principle itself remains an assumption, and the model, formulated after CMB-SPA and DESI, is still scored only on future data.
