A postulated thawing field (the "reverse thread") beats ΛCDM meaningfully only with DES-Y5 supernovae, trails the w0wa fit, and shows no detected coupling to dark matter. Under strict counting the current model scores 1 pass (the r bound), 2 tensions, 1 fail and 1 open. No test has yet used data released after its prediction.
