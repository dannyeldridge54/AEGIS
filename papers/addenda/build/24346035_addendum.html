Status update, 20 November 2026. No errata were found. The counts (0 of 10 promoted in the analysed window, 0 of 16 by the end of the morning) and the binomial bounds (26% and 17%) were rechecked. Since publication no further grading passes have been logged. The upgrades described in the post-cutoff note (fact sheet, mandatory runnable check, self-check, and feedback of rejection reasons) therefore remain unevaluated, and the next report must evaluate them under a lock. The archive also confirms a limitation stated in the paper: on 18 November a separate evaluation job occupied the local model server while the learner requested a different model, and learner cycles stalled. A later report must log the model used for each proposal. The pre-registration workflow cited here has a newer version, Lock Before You Compute v2.1 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24346127). The project's strict, locked rule for what counts as evidence is described in "Weave Control and the Evidence Atlas" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24399447).
