Status update, 20 November 2026. This note adds to, and does not replace, the original text. Errata. No number in the paper is wrong for the model and score it states. One wording scope note applies. On the exact SERAPIS potential the window ends where the field reaches the floor at ρ, not where V would turn negative. On that potential the window is slightly narrower: 0.038 instead of 0.045 in ln amp. What changed. The full-likelihood test called for in Sec. II has been done. On compressed CMB + DESI DR2 + DES-SN5YR the thread improves on ΛCDM by Δχ² = 4.8, against 10.8 for CPL. With full Planck 2018 + DESI DR2 + supernovae, in two independent Boltzmann codes, the gain is 1.9–3.4 for Pantheon+ and Union3 and 8.1–9.0 for DES-Y5. w₀w_a gains 9–20. The Gaussian result (i) is therefore superseded: the thread is preferred over ΛCDM only with DES-Y5, and it trails w₀w_a on every supernova set. We count both comparisons as tensions, not passes. A thread–dark-matter coupling that closed the gap on compressed data was not detected on full Planck (D1725). The starting height is not set by the framework; it is a roughly 1-in-300 initial condition (D1642). A forecast shows that DESI DR3 BAO alone will not separate the thread from CPL (D1707). The predictions w ≥ −1 and (w₀,w_a) ≈ (−0.79,−0.36) are unchanged and remain to be scored on DR3 + Euclid together with supernovae and the CMB. Current strict count across the Weave: 1 pass, 2 tension, 2 fail, after the quark row was added and open rows stopped counting (atlas deviation D3; the 12.8 GeV row stays open). No test has yet used data released after its prediction. Newest records.
