Status update, 20 November 2026

Addendum to: Lattice Champions and the Hexagon (Zenodo record 24346701) · Danny Eldridge, independent researcher, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA · dannyeldridge54@gmail.com

Status update, 20 November 2026. No errors have been found in this note since publication. The lattice counts, the duality proposition and the numerical champion table stand as written. Three later results bear on the final, speculative section. (1) A grid scan of the fundamental domain (Im τ ≤ 3) finds that E(τ,2) itself has no critical points other than τ = i and τ = ρ. This check was not pre-registered, and its script is in the archive. (2) Shifted Epstein sums, the lattice sums of spontaneously broken N=4 → N=2 planes, have no interior minimum. Under that modification the hexagonal vacuum is lost, not moved (D1380). (3) The 10D species-scale function E(τ,3/2)^(−1/6) also has its vacuum at the hexagonal point. It gives the same untilted inflation as the E(τ,2) form used in SERAPIS-E (D1523, D1552). The physics programme this note served is now reported in "Inflation from the Running of the Species Scale" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24378141) and the SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665). Under strict counting that programme stands at 1 pass, 2 tensions, 1 fail and 1 open test. None of its tests has yet used data released after the prediction.
