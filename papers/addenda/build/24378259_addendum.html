Status update, 20 November 2026. This note adds to, and does not replace, the original text. Errata. (1) The dateline "(draft)" should read "v1". (2) Reference [P1] should point to the current versions: Inflation from the Running of the Species Scale, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24378141, and the flavour companion, doi:10.5281/zenodo.24379357. No equation or number in the paper is affected. We re-checked the eigenvalues, the r ladder and the D = 6/D = 10 ratio. What changed. The paper left open the sign of the regularised one-loop R⁴ coefficient at D = 6, 5 and 4. We have since computed it on the rectangular, B = 0 slice, with code validated against the known d = 2 threshold. The one-loop thread is positive, giving a plateau rather than a hilltop, at D = 8 everywhere and at D = 4 on the whole slice. At D = 6 and 5 it is positive for any torus not close to string size: at D = 6 this means a total T⁴ volume above about 1.6 string units, and a B-field changes this only slightly. At the self-dual point itself the sign depends on the subtraction scheme. The full SO(d,d) moduli space is not yet checked. We also state plainly that the n_s ≃ 0.9706 at which r is quoted is fixed by a one-parameter tilt. n_s is therefore fitted and is not evidence; r is the prediction. The r values lie well below current bounds, and this r bound is the one strict pass in our evidence count (1 pass, 2 tension, 2 fail, after the quark row was added and open rows stopped counting (atlas deviation D3; the 12.8 GeV row stays open)). The heterotic entries for d ≥ 3 remain arguments by analogy, and a six-dimensional phase at horizon exit has not yet been realised. Newest records.
