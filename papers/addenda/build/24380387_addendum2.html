Status update 2, 20 November 2026: what the thread can be

Addendum to: The Reverse Thread (concept record 24380387; follows the first status update in doi:10.5281/zenodo.24399558) · Danny Eldridge, independent researcher, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA · dannyeldridge54@gmail.com

This paper postulates a slow field with the species-plateau shape, thawing today, but does not say what the field is. A check against the author's earlier result on rolling moduli and the fine-structure constant (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24232382, v2 doi:10.5281/zenodo.24281962) restricts it.

If the thread were a Kähler modulus (K = −3 ln(T + T̄)) whose one-loop threshold enters the visible gauge coupling, its coupling to α would be ζ ≈ 7×10⁻³ (k/3) √(2/k) ≈ 5.7×10⁻³ per Planck unit of field. Over the roll the model needs since recombination, Δφ ≈ 0.64 M_P, α would change by Δ ln α ≈ 3.7×10⁻³. That is about 4×10³ times larger than quasar and ESPRESSO limits (~10⁻⁶). The induced equivalence-principle violation, η ≈ 10⁻³ ζ² ≈ 3×10⁻⁸, is about 10⁷ times the MICROSCOPE limit (2.75×10⁻¹⁵). To be allowed, ζ must be suppressed below 1.7×10⁻⁶, a factor ≳ 3×10³.

Consequence. The thread cannot be a modulus of the visible sector or the dilaton. It must either be sequestered from the visible gauge fields by ≳ 3×10³ beyond one loop, for example a modulus that lives only on the dark side of the compactification (a brane-separated dark sector), which has not yet been shown, or be axion-like, which does not match the plateau as written. These numbers are an estimate under the stated assumptions, not a pre-registered test. The requirement is recorded as open (project log D1748) and will be tested next.

Nothing else in this paper or its first status update changes. Current records: SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665); Weave Control and the Evidence Atlas (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24399447).
