Addendum to: SERAPIS-E Audited: Corrected Reheating, a Point Prediction, and How the CMB Would Measure the Compactification (Zenodo record 24330316) · Danny Eldridge, independent researcher, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA · dannyeldridge54@gmail.com

Status update, 20 November 2026. Three errors are corrected. (1) The CMB comparison pair used here (0.9684±0.0030; 0.9728±0.0027) could not be traced to a publication. With published values (SPT-3G D1 0.9679±0.0033; 2512.10613 0.9682±0.0032, +DESI 0.9728±0.0029; 2512.05108 0.9693±0.0029, +DESI 0.9737±0.0025), the point is +1.75 to +1.97σ from CMB-SPA. Over its frozen range the model is +0.85 to +2.1σ from CMB-SPA and within ±0.8σ of CMB-SPA+DESI (D1273a). (2) The meter slope was taken at C = 1.15e-6, not at the benchmark. At the benchmark ∂n_s/∂ln C = 0.0133, so SO+SPHEREx would fix Im S·Im ρ to ±12%, not ±14% (D1265a/b). (3) The point's T_R is fixed by choosing M_H = 1e12 GeV. It is not fixed by leptogenesis: the benchmark's own instanton mass gives no thermal leptogenesis (D1270). Also, the range 560–975 for Im S·Im ρ is the n_s band mapped through the same formula, so the model currently measures, not predicts, that product (D1275b). These corrections, with sourced data, are collected in SERAPIS-E v2.1 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24345807). The full-data late-universe results and the strict evidence count (1 pass, 2 tension, 2 fail, after the quark row was added and open rows stopped counting (atlas deviation D3; the 12.8 GeV row stays open); no test yet on data released after its prediction) are in the SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665). The current inflation realisation, with n_s fitted, is doi:10.5281/zenodo.24378141.
