JUNO's first measurement, sin²θ₁₂ = 0.3092 ± 0.0087, excludes exact TM2 (0.3408) at 3.6σ (4.6σ with NuFIT 6.0). Result (iii) and its δ_CP predictions are withdrawn. This is the first falsified flavour prediction of the framework. After JUNO, we found that modular S₄ at the same two vacua gives TM1: the neutrino point −½ + i/2 is an SL(2,ℤ) image of i. TM1 gives sin²θ₁₂ = 0.3184 (+1.1σ). Three of the six images of i give TM1, three give an excluded pattern, and none gives TM2. The surviving sum-rule branch gives δ_CP ≈ 262°–286°; with T2K+NOvA's θ₂₃ it gives about 286°. Because TM1 and level 4 were chosen after JUNO, this is a post-hoc rescue, not a confirmed prediction; it will be scored by DUNE and Hyper-K. In the quark sector, θ̄ ∈ {0, π} stands. Later locked tests show that the framework hosts the quark masses and mixing but does not predict them. The quark modulus is not the inflaton's endpoint, Koide does not extend to quarks, and no slip-ladder variant has yet passed the CKM test (D1727–D1739). "τ_q ≈ 1/8 + i" is one published model's fit. Current strict count across the Weave: 1 pass, 2 tension, 2 fail, after the quark row was added and open rows stopped counting (atlas deviation D3; the 12.8 GeV row stays open).
