> Status update, 20 November 2026. The fits and tests in this record were re-checked against the stored results. No numerical errors were found. FLAG-1 is no longer a working model. Its frozen DESI DR3 and weak-lensing predictions (§Frozen predictions) still stand and will be scored once, unchanged, when the data appear. > > Clarifications. (1) The nulls (no α drift, no fifth force) assume the field couples to dark matter only. If the field is a supergravity modulus that the visible sector also feels, a one-loop α drift appears (10.5281/zenodo.24281962). (2) The JWST-based absolute magnitude is an approximate H₀ rescaling. Using the SH0ES H₀ = 73.04 instead gives a gap of about 1.7σ rather than 1.4σ. > > Since publication. In a pre-registered hold-out test, ΛCDM predicted DESI DR2 better than FLAG-1 did. The author's current work models evolving dark energy with a separate thawing field (the "reverse thread"). On full Planck + DESI DR2 it is meaningfully better than ΛCDM only with DES-Y5 supernovae, and it trails the w0wa fit. A dark-energy–dark-matter coupling, the mechanism used here, was tested in that model on full Planck and was not detected. Under strict counting the current model scores 1 pass, 2 tensions, 1 fail and 1 open. No test has yet used data released after its prediction. > > Current records: SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665); The Reverse Thread (10.5281/zenodo.24380387); "Weave Control and the Evidence Atlas" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24399447).
