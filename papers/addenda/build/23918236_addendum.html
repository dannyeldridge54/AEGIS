Addendum to: Torsion-Motivated Early Dark Energy with an Onset Locked to Matter–Radiation Equality (Zenodo record 23918236) · Danny Eldridge, independent researcher, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA · dannyeldridge54@gmail.com

> Status update, 20 November 2026. This record belongs to an earlier, separate line of the author's work. That line used a Barbero–Immirzi (torsion) pseudoscalar as early dark energy. It is no longer pursued, and its fits are kept as reported. The current work (the "Weave" and SERAPIS records) shares no equations with it. That work has no torsion field and no early dark energy, and it does not try to raise H₀. Inflation there comes from a species-scale plateau with a fitted tilt, and late dark energy from a separately postulated thawing field. What carries over is only the method of locking test rules before fits. > > The companion TorsionΛ toolkit was audited later. Its background parameter β is exactly a rescaling of Ω_m, and its H₀ = 73 configuration fails the Planck acoustic-scale prior by a wide margin. It should be used only as a consistency checker. > > Two findings here recur in the current work, as separate tests: late dark energy that helps only with DES-Y5 supernovae and does not beat w0wa, and no detectable dark-energy–dark-matter coupling. Under strict counting the current model scores 1 pass, 2 tensions, 1 fail and 1 open. No test has yet used data released after its prediction. > > Current records: SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665); 10.5281/zenodo.24378141; 10.5281/zenodo.24380387; "Weave Control and the Evidence Atlas" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24399447).
