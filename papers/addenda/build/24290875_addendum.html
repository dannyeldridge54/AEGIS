Status update, 20 November 2026

Addendum to: SERAPIS-E: Modular Plateau Inflation at the Hexagonal Torus with a One-Loop String Tilt (Zenodo record 24290875) · Danny Eldridge, independent researcher, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA · dannyeldridge54@gmail.com

Status update, 20 November 2026. One numerical error in the v2.1 update is corrected. The Hessians in "Why the vacuum is hexagonal" were evaluated with E(τ,2) divided by 2ζ(4). In the model's convention (E(ρ,2) = 5.78334) they are [+7.711, +7.711] V0 at ρ and [−2.216, +10.012] V0 at i, where V = 0.1958 V0. The signs, the minimum at ρ and the axion-unstable saddle at i are unchanged (D1280a). The v1 inflaton mass and N* window had already been corrected in v2. The statement that E(τ,2) was not scanned for further critical points is now answered: a grid scan of the fundamental domain (Im τ ≤ 3), made in this update and not pre-registered, finds only i and ρ. The frozen predictions of 15 November stand and remain what will be scored. The plateau's form is still a postulate here. A sourced alternative, the 10D species-scale function E(τ,3/2)^(−1/6), gives the same untilted inflation and is now the main realisation: "Inflation from the Running of the Species Scale" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24378141). There the spectral index is fitted, so it is not evidence. The model's overall status is in the SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665). Under strict counting it has 1 pass (the r bound), 2 tensions, 1 fail and 1 open test, and no test has yet used data released after its prediction. Flavour results are in doi:10.5281/zenodo.24379357, where exact TM2 is now excluded by JUNO and TM1 was adopted after the fact.
