> Status update, 20 November 2026. A re-check of v2 against its source notes and the cited bounds found no errors. Under the stated assumptions, a rolling Kähler modulus has d ln α_em/d ln x = 7.0×10⁻³ (k/3) at one loop. That coupling must be suppressed by ≳ 2×10³ (MICROSCOPE) and by ~10⁵ (the combined bound of Martins et al.). Any measurable thawing of such a modulus (1 + w₀ ≳ 10⁻¹²) is excluded. > > Relation to later work by the author. The Reverse Thread (10.5281/zenodo.24380387) models evolving dark energy with a slowly thawing plateau field (w₀ ≈ −0.79) but does not specify that field. If it is a Kähler modulus or the dilaton with the Standard Model in the same supergravity, the present result excludes it. A viable thread must therefore be axionic, or decoupled from the visible sector more strongly than any known string mechanism achieves. That condition is stated here as an open requirement on the newer model. Under strict counting that model currently scores 1 pass, 2 tensions, 1 fail and 1 open test, and no test has yet used data released after its prediction. > > Current records: SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665); The Reverse Thread (10.5281/zenodo.24380387); "Weave Control and the Evidence Atlas" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24399447).
