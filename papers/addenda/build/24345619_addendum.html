Addendum to: Shape, Scale and Duality: Why the SERAPIS-E Vacuum Is Hexagonal (Zenodo record 24345619) · Danny Eldridge, independent researcher, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA · dannyeldridge54@gmail.com

Status update, 20 November 2026. No new errors have been found. Version 2 already corrected the Hessian values of v1. The statement that a potential monotone in E(τ,2) has its vacuum only at ρ assumed that E(τ,2) has no critical points other than i and ρ. A grid scan of the fundamental domain (Im τ ≤ 3), made in this update and not pre-registered, confirms this. Two later results qualify the paper's message. First, shifted Epstein sums, as in spontaneously broken N=4 → N=2 planes, have no interior minimum, so the hexagonal preference is specific to the unshifted lattice sum (D1380). Second, the 10D species-scale function E(τ,3/2)^(−1/6) also has its vacuum at the hexagonal point and gives the same untilted inflation as E(τ,2) (D1523, D1552). The model's current form and status are in "Inflation from the Running of the Species Scale" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24378141) and the SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665). There the spectral index is fitted and the strict evidence count is 1 pass, 2 tension, 2 fail, after the quark row was added and open rows stopped counting (atlas deviation D3; the 12.8 GeV row stays open). The audited point quoted in Sec. V uses a heavy-neutrino mass that is chosen, not derived (D1270).
