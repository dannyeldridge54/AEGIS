We re-checked the plateau coefficient 7.2983, the slow-roll formulas, the untilted r values and g_s = 2/√3 at ρ. What changed. We state plainly that n_s is fitted: the tilt C was chosen to place n_s at 0.97065, so n_s is not evidence. The predictions are r = 5.41×10⁻⁴ (D = 10) or 1.07×10⁻³ (D = 6), and α_s ≈ −9.7×10⁻⁴. The fitted n_s lies within 1.8σ of every current CMB determination. r is below all current bounds; in our strict count this r bound is the single pass (1 pass, 2 tension, 2 fail, after the quark row was added and open rows stopped counting (atlas deviation D3; the 12.8 GeV row stays open)). The one-loop sign behind the "only plateau direction" argument has been computed on the rectangular torus slice. It is positive, giving a plateau, at D = 8 and 4, and at D = 6 and 5 for tori not near string size. The endpoint ρ is confirmed for the inflaton, but it cannot double as the quark modulus. Reheating is now fixed at T_R ≈ 1.3×10⁹ GeV instead of 8.9×10⁸ GeV; we estimate that this moves N_* by about 0.1. The same plateau reused as dark energy is supported over ΛCDM only with DES-Y5 supernovae. The species-gradient principle itself remains an assumption, and the model, formulated after CMB-SPA and DESI, is still scored only on future data. Newest records.
