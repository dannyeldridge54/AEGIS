Status update, 20 November 2026. This record (Twist-2: v1, the v1.1 and v2 addenda) describes a model that is no longer pursued. It is kept for its test record. Two results still stand: the pre-registered joint-fit failure of the undeformed α = 1 plateau (Δχ² = 7.95), and the observation that a thawing single field does not lower n_s.

Erratum. In the v2 addendum, Corrections item 2, "e^{−n} with n ≈ 124" should read "e^{−2n} with n ≈ 124.1". The vacuum energy scales as e^{−2n}, as the v1.1 addendum (item 3) and the source notes (D383, D413) state. No number changes.

Since publication. The author's inflation work moved from SERAPIS-E to a species-scale plateau along the string coupling. In that realisation the spectral index is fitted (a one-parameter tilt), and r ≈ 5×10⁻⁴ is predicted. The late universe is treated in a separate postulate, the "reverse thread". None of the Twist-2 capture physics (FI-term trap, cliff-sized Λ, eV gravitino, low string scale) is used there. Under strict counting the current model scores 1 pass (the r bound), 2 tensions, 1 fail and 1 open test. No test has yet used data released after its prediction.
