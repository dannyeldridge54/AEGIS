Status update, 20 November 2026. This note adds to, and does not replace, the original text. Errata. (1) The dark-photon ceiling in item 2 is now sourced from a digitised LZ 2024 curve (1.3×10⁻⁴⁷ cm² at 12.8 GeV): ε_max = 3.5×10⁻⁹, 3.9×10⁻⁸ and 9.7×10⁻⁷ at 0.3, 1 and 5 GeV. The digitisation is secondary, not an official LZ table. (2) The abstract and table should read ε ≲ 10⁻⁶, not 10⁻⁷, at the top of the band. (3) The cassion mass band is not capped at 5 GeV: any mass below m_X = 12.8 GeV is allowed, and 5–12.8 GeV is unconstrained over a wide range of ε. (4) r = 5.4×10⁻⁴ lies 67 times, not 65 times, below r < 0.036. What changed. Our full-Planck test of the Arthur-field coupling, run in a patched Boltzmann code, is complete (D1725). The coupling is not detected: the best-fit b changes sign between supernova sets and is consistent with zero, and the gain over the uncoupled thread is below our locked threshold. Item 3 is therefore no longer listed as a hint; in our strict count it is a failed test. The sign-switching effective Λ derived from the coupling is likewise not supported by data. The uncoupled thread is confirmed in an independent code (Δχ² = −2.6 to −8.1 against ΛCDM) and still trails w₀w_a. The ravon is no longer predicted: the natural cassion mass is of Stückelberg type (D1721). If family gauge bosons exist, they are expected at tens to hundreds of TeV (D1721). The TM1 pattern behind item 4 was adopted after JUNO excluded exact TM2. With T2K+NOvA's θ₂₃ it gives δ_CP ≈ 286° (D1690). Current strict count. 1 pass, 2 tension, 2 fail, after the quark row was added and open rows stopped counting (atlas deviation D3; the 12.8 GeV row stays open). The 12.8 GeV sarion is the open item. No test has yet used data released after its prediction. Newest records. SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665); "The Quark Thread" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24399408).
