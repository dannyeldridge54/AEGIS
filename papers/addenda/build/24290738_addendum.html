Status update, 20 November 2026

Addendum to: Three No-Go Results for String Origins of Modular Plateau Inflation (Zenodo record 24290738) · Danny Eldridge, independent researcher, Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, USA · dannyeldridge54@gmail.com

Status update, 20 November 2026. No errors have been found in the three results. Note that the bound Im S·Im T1 ≲ 731 in result (i) uses N_eff = 3, the tadpole count with an anti-D3 present. With N_eff = 4 it would be 975 and the conclusion, g_s ≳ 2.3, is unchanged in substance. Later work closed further routes. Shifted Epstein sums lose the hexagonal vacuum (D1380). In N=1 supergravity with a modular axio-dilaton, m3/2 cannot stay constant along Im τ → ∞ (D1543), and three more routes fail in the same limit (D1548). A different, sourced candidate for the plateau's shape has since appeared. The 10D species scale E(τ,3/2)^(−1/6) gives a y^(−2) plateau with its vacuum at the hexagonal point (D1523). It lies outside the three no-gos here because it runs along the string coupling, not the torus shape. Its potential, V ∝ |∂ ln Λ_sp|², is a proposal from the literature, not a derivation, and its tilt is fitted. That model is reported in "Inflation from the Running of the Species Scale" (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24378141). The overall status of the programme is in the SERAPIS v3.4 status update (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24396665): strict count 1 pass, 2 tension, 2 fail, after the quark row was added and open rows stopped counting (atlas deviation D3; the 12.8 GeV row stays open), with no test yet on data released after its prediction.
