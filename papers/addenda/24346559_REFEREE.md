## Addendum text
Status update, 20 November 2026. The toolkit results of this version are unchanged and no numerical errata were found. Three things changed around them. (1) The 670 GeV wino limit we quote (ATLAS, arXiv:2603.08315) is the observed limit of a 137 fb⁻¹ re-analysis; its expected limit is 770 GeV, the gap coming from a small local excess. A locked attempt to re-anchor our recast on that paper stopped as planned, because the paper does not publish a benchmark wino yield per signal region; CERN+1 therefore stays anchored on the 136 fb⁻¹ search (D1722, D1723). (2) The SERAPIS wino window used for the coverage numbers (0.67–1.85 TeV) has since been narrowed by gamma-ray line checks to 0.67–1.0/1.25 TeV (D1324, D1338). On that window the same HL-LHC reaches cover 53–92% (975 GeV) or 39–68% (896 GeV). (3) The window is now conditional on a reheating temperature below about 1.1×10⁹ GeV, because gravitino decays add non-thermal winos (D1687, D1720). The window was always post hoc, and the project's evidence atlas does not count it as evidence.
