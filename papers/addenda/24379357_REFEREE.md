## What changed since publication
- **TM2 excluded** by JUNO first data, sin²θ₁₂ = 0.3092 ± 0.0087 (arXiv:2511.14593): +3.6σ, +4.6σ with NuFIT. This is the first hard falsification of a Weave flavour prediction (D1652).
- **TM1 adopted post hoc** (after JUNO). Modular S₄ at the same two vacua gives TM1, sin²θ₁₂ = 0.3184 (+1.1σ) (D1667). Of the 6 images of i, 3 give TM1, 3 give a dead (½,½,0) pattern, and 0 give TM2 (D1668). With equal weight per image (D1674), P(TM1) = 0.69 × ½ = 0.345. δ_CP: only the 262°–286° branch survives NuFIT 6.0 (D1669), and T2K+NOvA's θ₂₃ picks ≈ 286°, about 10° from their joint peak (D1690). Level 4 over level 3 is a postulate (P-S4). The candidate reason D1697 was corrected in D1697a. In the magnetised level-8 variant, 3 of 12 images give TM1 (D1700).
- **Sec. IV (quarks):** the quark sector is hosted, not predicted. The inflaton endpoint ρ cannot be the quark modulus (D1727). Koide does not extend to quarks (D1728). Anomaly-free modular quark models fit but do not predict (D1729). A split slip-ladder improves the fit but fails the locked CKM tests (D1738, χ² 39; D1739, χ² 5.8). The example "τ_q ≈ 1/8 + i" is one model's fit (2305.08908), not a universal location (D1729 correction). The θ̄ ∈ {0, π} result is unaffected.
- **Sec. V** basins unchanged (69%). Charged leptons must stay at ρ, or on a rung ≳ 400: a rung-1 slip destroys Koide (D1737).

## Addendum text (ready to typeset)
**Status update, 20 November 2026.** This note adds to, and does not replace, the original text.
*Errata.* (1) The TM2 δ_CP values in Table I should read 235.2°/124.8° (sin²θ₂₃ = 0.561) and 286.2°/73.8° (0.470), 0.1–0.4° from the printed values; see also (2) below. (2) The dateline "(v2, draft)" should read "v2"; reference [P1] is doi:10.5281/zenodo.24378141.
*What changed.* JUNO's first measurement, sin²θ₁₂ = 0.3092 ± 0.0087, excludes exact TM2 (0.3408) at 3.6σ (4.6σ with NuFIT 6.0). Result (iii) and its δ_CP predictions are withdrawn. This is the first falsified flavour prediction of the framework. After JUNO, we found that modular S₄ at the same two vacua gives TM1: the neutrino point −½ + i/2 is an SL(2,ℤ) image of i. TM1 gives sin²θ₁₂ = 0.3184 (+1.1σ). Three of the six images of i give TM1, three give an excluded pattern, and none gives TM2. The surviving sum-rule branch gives δ_CP ≈ 262°–286°; with T2K+NOvA's θ₂₃ it gives about 286°. Because TM1 and level 4 were chosen after JUNO, this is a post-hoc rescue, not a confirmed prediction; it will be scored by DUNE and Hyper-K. In the quark sector, θ̄ ∈ {0, π} stands. Later locked tests show that the framework hosts the quark masses and mixing but does not predict them. The quark modulus is not the inflaton's endpoint, Koide does not extend to quarks, and no slip-ladder variant has yet passed the CKM test (D1727–D1739). "τ_q ≈ 1/8 + i" is one published model's fit.
*Current strict count* across the Weave: 1 pass, 2 tension, 1 fail, 1 open. No test has yet used data released after its prediction.
