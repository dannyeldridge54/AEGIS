## Errata (verified)
None.

Two notes, neither an erratum:
1. The JWST conversion to M ≈ −19.352 rescales SH0ES by an H₀ ratio that the paper does not give. With 73.04 the result would be −19.329, and the gap would be about 1.7σ instead of 1.4σ. The paper already labels the conversion approximate. It should state which H₀ it used.
2. "Passes laboratory tests by construction" and the nulls "no α drift, no fifth force" hold only if the field couples to dark matter alone. In the Twist supergravity embedding, ordinary matter is not shielded (D90, D96, D97), and a rolling Kähler modulus moves α at one loop (10.5281/zenodo.24281962). So the nulls are conditional on that coupling structure.
