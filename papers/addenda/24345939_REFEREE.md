## Errata (verified)
1. **v2 addendum §Corrections item 2 (cliff exponent).** The text says the cliff fixes "the magnitude, e^{−n} with n ≈ 124". The vacuum energy scales as Λ ∝ e^{−2n} (2n ≈ 248): see D383 and D413 (Λ = V(x_E) ∝ e^{−2n}/(1 + x_E/μ), n ≈ 124.1), and the v1.1 addendum item 3 in this same record. The wording e^{−n} was copied from D456, which has the same slip. Correct: "magnitude e^{−2n}, n ≈ 124.1". No number changes.
