## Addendum text
Status update, 20 November 2026. No errata were found in this version; all numbers were reproduced at the 18 November referee pass. Since publication the one-parameter Drell–Yan correction has passed an out-of-sample test: the exponent fitted to the wino tables predicts every 13.6 TeV higgsino cross section to 0.34% or better without refitting (CERN+1 v3, test LHC13). The scale and PDF uncertainties of the reference tables, which this paper excludes from its quoted errors, are now propagated into the disappearing-track limits in CERN+1 v3 (661.5 ± 10.0 GeV for the 136 fb⁻¹ search). The toolkit and its later results are described in CERN+1 v3 (doi:10.5281/zenodo.24346559). For the current state of the dark-matter model the toolkit was built to test, see the SERAPIS v3.4 status update (new version of doi:10.5281/zenodo.24289118) and Weave Control and the Evidence Atlas (this deposit series, 20 Nov 2026).
