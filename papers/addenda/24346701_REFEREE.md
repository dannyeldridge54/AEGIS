**Status update, 20 November 2026.** No errors have been found in this note since publication. The lattice counts, the duality proposition and the numerical champion table stand as written. Three later results bear on the final, speculative section. (1) A grid scan of the fundamental domain (Im τ ≤ 3) finds that E(τ,2) itself has no critical points other than τ = i and τ = ρ. It gives the same untilted inflation as the E(τ,2) form used in SERAPIS-E (D1523, D1552).
