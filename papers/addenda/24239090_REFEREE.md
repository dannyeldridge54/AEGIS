## Verdict
**Superseded in part.** As an audit trail and a credit to prior art, the report is still accurate. Its model-specific numbers (α = 1/3, N* = 52–55.6, r ≈ 1–2×10⁻³, w = −1) no longer describe the current model.

## Errata (verified)
1. **§2 "Vacuum" and §1 (square torus).** The report calls τ = i "a heavy supersymmetric Minkowski minimum". In the nilpotent-stabiliser embedding the report itself uses, the R8 referee pass (D636) found that τ = i is a symmetric critical point, not a SUSY-Minkowski vacuum. The fix was applied to SERAPIS v3. Correct wording: "a heavy Minkowski minimum at the symmetric point τ = i (not a supersymmetric vacuum in the nilpotent embedding)".
2. **§5 items 1–2 (experiments).** The report names "CMB-S4-class" experiments for the n_s and r forecasts. DOE and NSF ended support for CMB-S4 in July 2025 (D346; this was known before v2.2). The forecasts should be attributed to the Simons Observatory and LiteBIRD. The quoted σ(r) ≲ 4×10⁻⁴ (a delensed CMB-S4-class figure) has no funded experiment behind it.

## What changed since 14 Nov
- **Inflation model.**
  - SERAPIS v3 was renormalised to α_eff = 1/12, with Kairos at its own reheating N* = 46–49 (D636, D637).
  - The project's current realisation is the species-scale plateau with a fitted "Ushas" tilt (D1523; 10.5281/zenodo.24378141). Its values are n_s = 0.9707 (fitted), r = 5.4×10⁻⁴ (10D phase) or 1.07×10⁻³ (6D), and α_s ≈ −9.7×10⁻⁴.
  - The Kairos/Chronos split and its r ≈ 1–2×10⁻³ are therefore not current. The kill condition "r < 5×10⁻⁴ excludes both branches" is replaced by the v3.2 sheet's r < 2.4×10⁻⁴ (95% CL), or a detected r > 1.1×10⁻³.
- **Vacuum.** Two vacuum families are now carried. One is at ρ: in SERAPIS-E the hexagonal point is the vacuum, not a wall (D569, D609; 10.5281/zenodo.24345619). The other is at τ = i: the τ = i variant has a true zero-energy vacuum there (D1351).
- **Late universe.** The report's prediction 3 (w₀ = −1, w_a = 0) is no longer the project's position. The Weave now postulates a thawing "reverse thread" (10.5281/zenodo.24380387). On full Planck + DESI DR2 it beats ΛCDM meaningfully only on DES-Y5, and it trails w0wa (D1712, D1725). Its dark-matter coupling was not detected (D1725). Λ itself is still put in by hand.
