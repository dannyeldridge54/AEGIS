Sourced values (digitised LZ 2024 curve, 1.3×10⁻⁴⁷ cm² at 12.8 GeV): **3.5×10⁻⁹ (0.3 GeV), 3.9×10⁻⁸ (1 GeV), 9.7×10⁻⁷ (5 GeV)**. Source D1688. Caveat: secondary digitisation (C. O'Hare, DirectDetectionPlots), not an official LZ table; D1721 did not see D1688 and still reports NULL.
2. **Abstract and Table, ε range.** "10⁻¹¹ ≲ ε ≲ 10⁻⁷" / "ε ≲ 10⁻⁷" contradicts the paper's own edge at 5 GeV (8×10⁻⁷; sourced 9.7×10⁻⁷). Correct: ε ≲ 10⁻⁶ at the top of the band. Source: paper Eq. (2), D1688.
3. **Item 2, mass band.** "0.3–5 GeV" is too narrow: with m_X = 12.8 GeV the cassion may lie anywhere up to m_X, and 5–12.8 GeV is open for ε from ~5×10⁻¹² to ~10⁻³ (collider limit), xenon cut aside. Source D1685 (+ D1685a, DarkCast 1801.04847; referee D1686).
4. **Item 5.** "about 65 times below r < 0.036": 0.036/5.4×10⁻⁴ = **67**. Arithmetic.

## Addendum text (ready to typeset)
**Status update, 20 November 2026.** This note adds to, and does not replace, the original text.
*Errata.* (1) The dark-photon ceiling in item 2 is now sourced from a digitised LZ 2024 curve (1.3×10⁻⁴⁷ cm² at 12.8 GeV): ε_max = 3.5×10⁻⁹, 3.9×10⁻⁸ and 9.7×10⁻⁷ at 0.3, 1 and 5 GeV. The digitisation is secondary, not an official LZ table. (2) The abstract and table should read ε ≲ 10⁻⁶, not 10⁻⁷, at the top of the band. (3) The cassion mass band is not capped at 5 GeV: any mass below m_X = 12.8 GeV is allowed, and 5–12.8 GeV is unconstrained over a wide range of ε. (4) r = 5.4×10⁻⁴ lies 67 times, not 65 times, below r < 0.036.
*What changed.* Our full-Planck test of the Arthur-field coupling, run in a patched Boltzmann code, is complete (D1725). The coupling is **not detected**: the best-fit b changes sign between supernova sets and is consistent with zero, and the gain over the uncoupled thread is below our locked threshold. Item 3 is therefore no longer listed as a hint; in our strict count it is a failed test. The sign-switching effective Λ derived from the coupling is likewise not supported by data. The uncoupled thread is confirmed in an independent code (Δχ² = −2.6 to −8.1 against ΛCDM) and still trails w₀w_a. The ravon is no longer predicted: the natural cassion mass is of Stückelberg type (D1721). If family gauge bosons exist, they are expected at tens to hundreds of TeV (D1721). The TM1 pattern behind item 4 was adopted after JUNO excluded exact TM2. With T2K+NOvA's θ₂₃ it gives δ_CP ≈ 286° (D1690).
