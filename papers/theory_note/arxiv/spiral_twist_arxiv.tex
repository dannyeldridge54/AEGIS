\begin{abstract}
We test the Barbero--Immirzi (BI) parameter of loop quantum gravity, promoted to a dynamical pseudoscalar coupled to
torsion (the ``Twist''), as late-time dark energy, using Planck, ACT DR6, SPT-3G, DESI DR2 and three supernova
samples, with every test rule fixed and hashed before its data were seen. A minimal thawing Twist ties $\Lambda$CDM
on Pantheon+; its best preference (DES Y5, $\Delta\chi^2=-6.2$) vanishes when that sample is split at $z=0.1$ (the
whole gain is a low/high-redshift calibration offset, as for $w_0$--$w_a$), and weakens on Union3 ($-1.8$). We then
point out that the $\mathcal N=1$ supergravity embedding of the BI field (Gates, Ketov \& Yunes) is a dilaton--axion
pair on a hyperbolic field space of curvature $-2/3$ ($\alpha=1$), which fixes the axion's kinetic coupling to
$e^{\lambda\chi}$ with $\lambda=-2\sqrt{2/3}$, a structure known to produce apparent phantom crossing. A two-field solver,
validated against published attractors to $10^{-10}$ and implemented in AxiCLASS (cross-check $2\times10^{-5}$), shows
the fixed geometry is weak mixing ($\xi\simeq0.82$ versus $\gtrsim370$ needed for the strong-mixing DESI benefit). The
remaining route, the BI angle as a fraction $f$ of the dark matter, is dark matter coupled to the dilaton with strength
$0.816f$; it bends the $w_0$--$w_a$ reading toward DESI, but existing coupling bounds ($f\lesssim6$--$9\%$) allow only
$\Delta w_a\simeq-0.05$ to $-0.09$. We report solar-system, inflation ($n_s$ tension $2.4\sigma$) and early-energy
checks, a closure caveat in the coupled AxiCLASS build, and forward predictions hash-locked before DESI DR3, Euclid,
LiteBIRD and LISA data exist.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}
DESI DR2 BAO, combined with the CMB and supernovae, prefers an evolving dark-energy equation of state that appears to
cross $w=-1$ \cite{desi2025}. A canonical field cannot cross $w=-1$, but two-field systems with kinetic mixing can
\emph{appear} to cross in a $w_0$--$w_a$ analysis while obeying the null energy condition \cite{toomey2025,ramos2026}.
Loop quantum gravity contains a natural pseudoscalar: the Barbero--Immirzi parameter $\gamma$. Promoted to a field it
couples to torsion through the Holst or Nieh--Yan terms \cite{taveras2008,calcagni2009}. We ask two questions:
does the minimal BI field fit the late-time data, and what does its own supergravity geometry imply? Every test below
had its rule written, timestamped and hashed before computing; failures are reported at the same size as successes.

\section{The BI field's supergravity geometry}\label{sec:geo}
Gates, Ketov \& Yunes \cite{gky2009} show that a dynamical BI parameter in $\mathcal N=1$ supergravity is a chiral
dilaton--axion superfield $\Phi=\phi+ia$, with $\Omega=e^{\Phi+\bar\Phi}(\Phi+\bar\Phi)$ and $K=-3\ln(-\Omega/3)$,
$\phi<0$. The linear part of $K$ drops out of the metric:
\begin{equation}
K_{\Phi\bar\Phi}=\frac{3}{(\Phi+\bar\Phi)^2},\qquad
\mathcal L_{\rm kin}=-\tfrac12(\partial\chi)^2-\tfrac12e^{\lambda\chi}(\partial\tilde a)^2,\qquad
\boxed{\lambda=-2\sqrt{2/3}},
\end{equation}
with $|\phi|=e^{\sqrt{2/3}\chi}$, $\tilde a=\sqrt{3/2}\,a$: the hyperbolic plane of curvature $-2/3$, i.e.\ the
$\alpha=1$ case of $\lambda=-2\sqrt{2/(3\alpha)}$ \cite{qalpha2026}. Toomey et al.\ fit this coupling to Planck + DESI
and find $\lambda=-1.3^{+1.3}_{-1.5}$ \cite{toomey2025}; the fixed value lies inside, a consistency found after their fit.

\paragraph{Matter coupling.} If matter couples to the supergravity Jordan frame, $A^2=-3/\Omega$ and
$\alpha_{\rm DEF}=(2/\sqrt3)(|\phi|-\tfrac12)$: a general-relativity point at $\phi=-\tfrac12$ that is a
Damour--Nordtvedt attractor, but Cassini pins the dilaton within $|\Delta\chi|<0.0072\,\Mp$ today, paralleling the
Brax--Martin no-go theorem \cite{brax2007}. A rolling dilaton therefore needs matter blind to it, which is natural in
scale-invariant Einstein--Cartan gravity \cite{karananas2021,piani2022}.

\paragraph{Simplest potential.} A constant superpotential gives $V=\tfrac{81}{2}W^2(-\phi-1)e^{-6\phi}/\phi^2$, with a
turning point exactly at $\phi=-\tfrac12$ and its minimum at $\phi=-\tfrac23$, both at negative energy; an uplift is
required.

\section{The Spiral Twist: solver, validation, implications}
\paragraph{Model.} Einstein frame, matter blind to the dilaton:
$\mathcal L=-\tfrac12(\partial\chi)^2-\tfrac12e^{\lambda\chi}(\partial\theta)^2-V_0e^{-\kappa\chi}-m^2F^2(1-\cos\theta/F)$,
with $\lambda$ fixed as above and $F=(4/\pi)\sqrt{3/2}\,\Mp$ from the Nieh--Yan period at a Planck-length torsion scale.
\paragraph{Validation.} Exact $\Lambda$CDM and single-field limits; true $w\ge-1$ always; no ghost (kinetic matrix
positive, sound speeds 1). The late-time attractors of the published autonomous system of \cite{ramos2026}
($(\lambda_p,\xi)=(2,10),(2,100),(8,20)$) are reproduced to $\le5\times10^{-10}$. An attempt to reproduce the
benchmark of \cite{toomey2025} did not succeed (inputs not fully stated). In AxiCLASS, an exponential field plus dark
matter coupled as $m_c\propto e^{\beta\phi}$ agrees with the solver to $3\times10^{-8}$ ($\beta=0$) and $2\times10^{-5}$
($\beta=0.05$); with $\kappa=0.05$, $\beta=0$ the full pipeline reproduces the $\Lambda$CDM $\chi^2$ within 0.08.
\paragraph{Closure caveat.} For $\beta\ne0$ the coupled build closes on its actual $H(0)$, which differs from the input
$h$ by up to $0.6\%$; reported $H_0$ and $\sigma_8$ labels carry this offset, $\chi^2$ does not.
\paragraph{Weak mixing.} In the notation of \cite{ramos2026}, $\xi=-\lambda/2=0.82$, while their DESI benefit requires
$\log_{10}\xi>2.5$. A scan with $F$ fixed gives at best $w_0\simeq-0.84$, $w_a\simeq-0.24$, the thawing relation.
\paragraph{Matter-like angle.} An oscillating angle has $m_{\rm eff}=m\,e^{-\lambda\chi/2}$: dark matter coupled to the
dilaton with $d\ln m/d\chi=0.816$, i.e.\ $\beta_{\rm eff}\simeq0.816f$ for a fraction $f$ of the matter.

\section{Predictions locked before the data}
Hash-locked on 2 November 2026 (SHA-256 begins \texttt{b629f534}; file and numbers archived with

(excluded if $r<0.002$); gravitational-wave velocity birefringence $\sim10^{-43}$--$10^{-42}$\,GeV.

\section{Conclusions}
The minimal BI dark-energy field is consistent with the data but not preferred; its strongest hint is a supernova
calibration effect. The BI field's own supergravity geometry fixes a kinetic mixing of the type behind apparent phantom
crossing, but too weakly to matter unless part of the dark matter is the BI angle, which existing bounds keep small.
The locked predictions above will decide the remaining routes.
