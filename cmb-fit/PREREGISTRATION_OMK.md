# Pre-registration: the shape of space (curvature test)

Written 26 October 2026, 21:00 CT, before the fits below were run. Danny Lee Eldridge, with Claude.

**Question.** Does the data allow the curved, closed "Poincaré dodecahedral space" proposed by Luminet et al. (2003), which needs a total density Ω_tot ≈ 1.013, i.e. curvature Ω_k ≈ −0.013 (CLASS sign: negative = closed)? And what curvature does our own pipeline find?

**Fits** (Planck 2018 + DESI DR2 + Pantheon+, no SH0ES, same settings as all our fits):
1. Standard model with curvature free (one extra setting).
2. Standard model with Ω_k fixed at −0.013 (no extra setting: the value is set by the dodecahedral proposal).
3. The Locked Twist with curvature free (does the Twist change the answer?).

**What counts.**
- Dodecahedral curvature is **excluded** if fit 2 is worse than the flat standard model by Δχ² > 9 (3σ for one number).
- Curvature is **detected** only if fit 1 improves on flat by Δχ² > 9 and its best Ω_k is more than 3σ from zero; otherwise "space is flat to within the errors".
- Published reference values we expect to roughly reproduce: Planck 2015 topology limits (no compact topology smaller than the last-scattering sphere; cubic torus R_i > 0.97 χ_rec) and DESI DR2 + CMB Ω_k = 0.0023 ± 0.0011.
