# AEGIS Commercial License 1.0 (terms summary; full agreement signed per licensee)

## Who needs it
Every business or for-profit organisation that uses any AEGIS software, data product or paper content for any purpose, including internal research and development, evaluation, testing, AI training, consulting deliverables, or work done on its behalf by contractors or universities. Free use (individuals, scientists, students, schools, nonprofits, public research) is under the AEGIS Community License.

## Annual fee: a share of profits (placeholders set by Danny Eldridge)
Using AEGIS work in a business is an agreement to pay, every year, **the greater of** the tier minimum or the tier's percentage of the whole corporate group's annual pre-tax profit. The percentage rises with group size.

| Group annual revenue | Minimum per year | Share of group pre-tax profit |
|---|---|---|
| Under $100,000 (sole traders, micro-business) | $10 | none |
| $100,000 – $1M | $50 | 0.1% |
| $1M – $10M | $500 | 0.5% |
| $10M – $100M | $10,000 | 1% |
| $100M – $1B | $250,000 | 2% |
| Over $1B | $2,000,000 | 3% |

**Profit floor.** Large corporations can book profit down to zero through transfer pricing, related-party fees or tax havens. For groups over $10M revenue, the fee is therefore never less than 0.02% of group revenue, whatever profit is reported. "Pre-tax profit" means consolidated profit before tax across the whole group, as reported in audited accounts, or as the auditor reconstructs it if those are missing or understated.

## Terms
1. **Group size.** Revenue and headcount are counted across every entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with the licensee, plus anyone acting for them. Using shells, subsidiaries, contractors or "partners" to fall into a lower tier is a material breach.
2. **Signed statement.** The licensee certifies its group revenue and consolidated pre-tax profit each year, with its audited accounts. A false statement voids the license from its start date.
3. **Audit.** Once a year, on 30 days' notice, an independent auditor may verify use, size and profit, including related-party transfers that move profit out of the group's reported accounts. If the licensee underpaid by more than 5%, it pays the audit cost.
4. **Unlicensed use.** Fees for every year of unlicensed use at three times the list price, plus interest and the licensor's legal costs. The multiple is the parties' agreed estimate of loss, including the cost of detection and enforcement.
5. **Whistleblower reward.** Anyone who reports unlicensed business use with evidence that leads to payment receives 20% of the amount recovered. Report to dannyeldridge54@gmail.com; identities are kept confidential.
6. **Prohibited uses** (military, weapons, mass surveillance, human-rights violations) are never licensed at any price.
7. **No sublicensing, resale or rebranding** without written consent.
8. **Governing law:** Oklahoma, USA.
