Point & See

Hold your phone up to the night sky. The stars, planets and constellations are drawn right where they really are — move the phone and the sky moves with you.

Your phone asks for the camera and motion sensors. Nothing leaves your phone — no account, no tracking. Works best outside, away from bright lights. On iPhone, tap “Allow” for both.

The map shows the brightest 378 stars today. It will grow — and get sharper — as the AEGIS Gaia survey is processed and sent to your phone.
