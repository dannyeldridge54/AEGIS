Grades: A within 1σ, B 1–2σ, C 2–3σ, F beyond 3σ (worst end of each band). Post-data comparison; the scored test is on future data (locked forward predictions, AEGIS D567). Current limit r < 0.036.

Data: ESA Gaia DR3 (AEGIS sweep, 1.99 M stars with distances; 3,068 flagged) and SDSS DR17 spectroscopic catalog (sample of 180,000 of 1.33 M galaxies and 120,000 of 753,000 quasars; two galaxy query ranges were capped at 500,000 rows). Distances for galaxies use flat ΛCDM with Planck 2018 parameters. Local universe: 2MASS Redshift Survey and Cosmicflows-4; variable stars: AAVSO VSX (via VizieR, CfA mirror). Exoplanets: NASA Exoplanet Archive (6,470 planets). Fast-star cross-match: SIMBAD (Harvard mirror). Named galaxies: OpenNGC with Hubble-law distances (cz/H₀; unreliable below ~10 Mpc). Planner catalogs: HYG v4.1 stars and OpenNGC deep-sky objects (both CC BY-SA 4.0). Model: SERAPIS status preprint, doi 10.5281/zenodo.24238832. Still to add: Gaia quasars and the CMB map.
