# Comets, the galaxy and catastrophes: rules written 2026-10-26, before looking

Data: confirmed terrestrial impact structures (list compiled from the Earth Impact Database via Wikipedia's
"List of impact structures on Earth"), keeping craters with diameter >= 5 km and age 0-260 Myr with a stated age.
Hypothesis (mechanism first): the Sun crosses the galactic mid-plane every ~26-36 Myr; each crossing stirs the Oort
cloud and raises the comet impact rate.
Test 1 (rhythm): Rayleigh phase clustering of crater ages scanned over periods 20-45 Myr. Significance by
simulation: 10,000 fake crater sets drawn from a smooth version of the real age distribution (keeps the fact that
young craters survive better), taking the best period in each fake set (corrects for scanning).
Test 2 (galaxy link): if a period is found, it must lie in 26-36 Myr AND its peak phase must fall within +-5 Myr of
the present (the Sun crossed the plane a few Myr ago).
Pass: test 1 p < 0.01 and test 2 satisfied. Anything else is reported as no pattern.
Control (no mechanism): not run here; modern floods/fires against outer-planet positions are expected to show nothing.
