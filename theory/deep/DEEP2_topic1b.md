# DEEP pass 2, topic 1b — does an exact duality exist where Twist-M needs it? (21:19 CT 14 Nov)
Findings:
1. IIB orientifolds (Grimm, arXiv:0705.3253): only a SUBGROUP Γ_S of SL(2,Z) acting on the axio-dilaton survives; Kähler coordinates T_α do not transform homogeneously (they shift by
   K_αab G^a G^b terms); modular completions of K are conjectural. → No established T-duality on an IIB Kähler modulus.
2. Heterotic orbifolds (review arXiv:2411.06516): SL(2,Z)_T on the volume modulus T (R → 1/R) is exact in string perturbation theory; W has modular weight −3 (κ = 3 — matching Twist's
   K = −3 ln(T+T̄)); the fixed points T = 1 and T = ρ are ALWAYS extrema of V (as in Twist-M, V′(1) = 0, D493); in gaugino-condensation models T = 1 is a saddle and minima at T ≈ 1.2 break SUSY
   in AdS — so a dS minimum exactly at T = 1 is not the generic outcome there.
3. Magnetised/intersecting D-brane models (arXiv:1608.06129, 1804.06644, 1811.11384): modular symmetry acts on the COMPLEX-STRUCTURE modulus U of T² (modular flavour symmetry); in IIB
   toroidal orientifolds an isotropic untwisted U has exact SL(2,Z)_U and K = −3 ln(U+Ū) — the same form as the Twist's K (also used as U-duality in 2404.18995).
Reading: two candidate homes for Twist-M's exact duality —
 (a) heterotic orbifold volume modulus T (exact SL(2,Z), weight −3, fixed point automatically an extremum; capture FI term would be the heterotic anomalous-U(1) FI, which depends on the
     dilaton, not T — so case (ii)-type Twist-M);
 (b) IIB isotropic complex-structure modulus U (exact SL(2,Z)_U, K = −3 ln(U+Ū)); requires fluxes chosen to leave U for the Twist superpotential; D7 FI terms depend on Kähler moduli, not U.
Either way the duality is available; the FI-capture link (Twist-2's D436 picture) does not carry over naturally, which favours Twist-M case (ii) and makes the matter coupling β the key
question (D498). Credits: 0705.3253, 2411.06516, 1608.06129, 1804.06644, 1811.11384.
