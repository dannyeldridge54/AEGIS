# DEEP pass 2, topic 1 — where could Twist-M live? (orbifold embedding search, 21:16 CT 14 Nov)
Searched for IIB orientifolds of toroidal orbifolds with D7-branes and Kähler-moduli dualities.
- arXiv:2404.18995 (LPTHE): IIB orientifolds of T⁶/Z_N and T⁶/(Z_N×Z_M) with 3-form fluxes and magnetised D7-branes; dualities used are S-duality on the axio-dilaton and U-duality on
  untwisted complex-structure moduli; Kähler moduli left flat at tree level (no-scale); twisted (blow-up) moduli sit at the orbifold point without twisted flux; magnetised D7s give
  negative D3 charge; cited earlier work found a shallow dS minimum and inflection-point inflation from D7 quantum corrections.
- hep-th/0512287 ('Moduli stabilization in toroidal IIB orientifolds'), hep-th/0506090, hep-th/0609013/0609014: earlier toroidal-orientifold stabilisation programmes.
Reading for Twist-M: the natural home of an exact duality is an untwisted Kähler modulus of such an orbifold; magnetised D7-branes (needed for our FI term) are standard there; blow-up
moduli give small cycles. But the duality the literature actually uses is S/U-duality, not a T-duality on a Kähler modulus with fluxes and branes present; whether a Kähler-modulus
SL(2,Z) survives fluxes + magnetised D7s is not established in what we read. Open question stands (DEEP 11), now with a concrete class to look in.
