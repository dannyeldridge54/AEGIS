# DEEP 5 — capture and its fifth-force screening (21:07 CT 14 Nov)
Capture prior art was covered in DEEP 1 (1110.3333 D-term fixing; hep-th/0403001 ESP trapping). This tick checked the D476/D486 'kink screening' against the screening literature
(review arXiv:1011.5909: chameleon, symmetron, Vainshtein, k-mouflage; fine-tuning note 1807.11508). One search found no direct counterpart of a V-shaped (non-analytic) minimum.
Honest reading: after quantum rounding (D486) the field simply has a large mass (3.7e10 GeV) at the minimum, so the absence of fifth forces is the ordinary 'heavy modulus' result,
not a new screening mechanism; no novelty is claimed for it. The threshold picture of D476 is a convenient description of the same fact.
