# DEEP 11 — string embedding of Twist-M (21:16 CT 14 Nov)
Prior art: exact target-space (T-)duality, SL(2,Z) acting on Kähler moduli, holds in toroidal compactifications and orbifolds (review Giveon–Porrati–Rabinovici, hep-th/9401139;
modular-invariant gaugino condensation W ∝ η(T)^{-6} in Z_N orbifolds, Font–Ibáñez–Lüst–Quevedo; hep-th/9510022, hep-ph/9210219). In generic Calabi–Yau volume moduli it is NOT an exact symmetry.
TENSION found: Twist-M needs an exact duality on the Twist modulus (D490), which points to a toroidal-orbifold-type geometry, while the capture/FI picture needed a fibred CY with a small
divisor intersecting the big one (D462), plus an intermediate cycle τ ≈ 40 (D471/D502). Possible reconciliation (not checked): a IIB orientifold of a toroidal orbifold (untwisted moduli carry
SL(2,Z); twisted/blow-up moduli supply small cycles and D7-brane intersections) — e.g. T⁶/Z_N' with blow-ups. Alternatively Twist-M case (ii) (no FI, D498) drops the D462 requirement but then
needs β ≲ 3e-3 from the geometry. Verdict: open; the most important string-side question for Twist-M is whether a duality-preserving orbifold with these cycles exists.
