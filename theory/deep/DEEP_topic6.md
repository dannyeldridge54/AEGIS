# DEEP 6 — Λ from an exponential cliff (21:08 CT 14 Nov)
Prior art: Demirtas, Kim, McAllister, Moritz, Rios-Tascon, "Small cosmological constants in string theory" (arXiv:2107.09064) and "A cosmological constant that is too small"
(arXiv:2107.09065): explicit flux compactifications in which an exponentially small W₀ (from e^{−2πτ}-type terms, a 'perturbatively flat vacuum') gives an exponentially small
vacuum energy (they obtain |Λ| ~ 1e-144, too small — supersymmetric AdS). Older context: 1801.09173, hep-th/0408054, hep-th/0001157.
Comparison: the Twist's 'Λ from the cliff' (e^{−n(...)} with n ≈ 125 (Twist-2) or 234 (Twist-M)) is the same idea — Λ's 120-digit smallness from an exponential of a
modulus-dependent action — at the phenomenological level; the string papers show such exponents arise from fluxes/instantons and can even overshoot. CREDIT.
Open question sharpened: in a real compactification n would be ~2π × (cycle volume × charge); n ≈ 234 ⇒ cycle factor ≈ 37 — the same size as D471's intermediate cycle (τ ≈ 39–43).
Reading (coincidence check, not a claim): the axion-mass instanton cycle and the Λ cliff could be the same ~40-size cycle; worth a locked test later.
