## 1. What the literature offers (sources read today)

**The dark dimension** (Montero–Vafa–Valenzuela, arXiv:2205.12293; also 2306.16491).
- The swampland distance conjecture applied to de Sitter gives a light Kaluza–Klein tower with Λ ~ m⁴, written Λ^{1/4} = λm.
- Bounds then single out exactly ONE extra dimension, of micron size.
- The one-loop (Casimir) energy of that tower gives λ⁴ ≈ λ_Casimir = 3ζ(5)/(2π)⁶ ≈ 5 × 10⁻⁵, and the tower scale coincides with the neutrino mass scale.
- The species scale is ~10⁹–10¹⁰ GeV.

**"Lost energy that accumulates"** (running vacuum, Solà Peracaula et al.; arXiv:2512.20616).
- In these models the vacuum energy is ρ_vac = ρ₀ + νH² + …, built up by quantum effects as the universe expands.
- Against current data (DESI DR2 + CMB + supernovae), the flipped RVM gives ν = 0.029 (+0.010/−0.019) with Pantheon+, about 1.9σ from ΛCDM, and ν = 0.046 with DES-Y5, about 3.1σ.
- The w₀w_a benchmark sits at 2.3σ (Pantheon+) and 4σ (DES-Y5).

**Matter and dark energy exchanging energy** (interacting dark energy).
- DESI DR2 analyses (arXiv:2609.05660, 2609.03062, 2504.00985, 2504.00994) find hints of a coupling when all data are combined.
- Bayesian evidence still favours ΛCDM.
- The direction of transfer is model-dependent and not settled.

**Neutrinos in the dark dimension.** Right-handed neutrinos living in the extra dimension give naturally small Dirac masses (cited in the dark-dimension papers). This replaces the minimal Weinberg operator that failed in D1621. Modular A4 seesaw variants (arXiv:2509.22108, 2608.20508, 2409.10912) are the 4D alternatives; they have free coefficients.

## 2. How it maps onto the Weave ("the reverse thread")

The Weave's inflation runs DOWN the loom: the thread passes from ten dimensions towards four. The dark dimension is the first rung back UP: one dimension opening again at late times. Its Casimir energy is the one-loop energy of the tower, the "energy lost along the way" that accumulates in the vacuum. In weave words, inflation is the thread going in, dark energy is the thread coming back out, and the two are mirror images.

Two Weave results already point this way:
- In a genuine 6D supersymmetric phase the one-loop vacuum energy is Casimir-type, ∝ 1/r⁴ (D1592, SLED).
- The dark-dimension Casimir coefficient contains ζ(5), the next zeta value after the ζ(3) of the R⁴ codon that drives inflation (ζ(5) appears in the D⁴R⁴ coefficient E(τ,5/2)).

## 5. Flags (must be solved, not hidden)

1. **Inflation scale versus today's species scale.** Our inflation has H ≈ 5.8 × 10¹² GeV, about 10⁴ times above today's dark-dimension species scale. During inflation the dark dimension must therefore have been far smaller, with the radius growing afterwards. That needs a radion history. The dark-dimension literature argues for inflation within the 5D picture with low H, which would CONFLICT with our r ~ 5 × 10⁻⁴. This is the main tension to resolve.
2. **Fifth-force and torsion-balance bounds.** m_KK ≳ 6.6 meV is required; 26.7 meV passes.
3. The fermion-excess sign needed for positive Λ is assumed.
4. Running vacuum and interacting dark energy are alternatives with similar "accumulation" physics, and the data do not yet separate them from ΛCDM decisively.
