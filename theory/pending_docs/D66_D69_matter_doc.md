## Development 66 — n_s is locked by the number of e-folds (13 Nov 08:45)
Deforming the base Kähler coefficient (3 → 3α) barely moves n_s: α = 0.5 → 0.9647, α = 1 → 0.9653, α = 5 → 0.9671, while r runs from 0.0018 to 0.0132. n_s = 0.970 or 0.973 is not reachable for any α in [0.05, 50]. This agrees with the independent analysis of Starobinsky inflation (arXiv:2609.19296). Simons Observatory (σ ≈ 0.002) decides cleanly. Code: theory/inflation/kahler_correction_ns.py.

## Development 67 — a natural size for a small dark-matter coupling (estimate)
Tree level gives β = 0 (D64). Quantum (loop) corrections couple the dark-matter mass to the field at roughly β ~ n_eff·√(2/3)/(16π²): 0.005 (n_eff = 1), 0.010 (2), 0.021 (4), 0.041 (8). The fitted β ≈ 0.03 is within this range for n_eff ~ 6. **Order-of-magnitude only**: it shows a small β is not unnatural, not that the theory predicts 0.03. Crossroads: A. keep β = 0 as the prediction (taken — it is the tree-level result); B. treat β ~ 0.01–0.04 as a loop-level allowance, reported beside it. Code: field_tests/results/theory_items_D67_D69.txt.

## Development 68 — an open problem in the hidden SU(8)
The cosine that gives the kick needs a height of (0.75–1.7×10¹⁴ GeV)⁴ ≈ 10⁻¹⁸–10⁻¹⁷ M_P⁴. Gaugino condensation gives a height ∝ e^{−(π/4) Re T} times an O(1) prefactor, which would need Re T ≈ 50 — but the kick happens at the cliff, Re T ≈ 1. So either the hidden gauge kinetic function is f = kT with k ≈ 50 (a hidden sector much more weakly coupled than T alone suggests), or the prefactor is small (~10⁻¹⁷). **This is a hidden tuning that the D46 matching did not show.** Crossroads: A. f = kT, k ≈ 50 (one number, possibly from a string construction — to look up); B. a small prefactor from the hidden matter field's vacuum value; C. a different origin for the kick. None settled; listed as an open problem in the reviewer guide.

## Development 69 — neutrino masses
Matter-making needs the Weinberg operator, so neutrinos have Majorana masses. DESI DR2 + CMB already bound Σm_ν < ~0.064 eV (95%), close to the normal-ordering minimum 0.0589 eV. Prediction: **normal ordering, Σm_ν ≈ 0.059–0.064 eV, Majorana** (neutrinoless double-beta decay allowed but small, |m_ββ| ≲ 0.005 eV for normal ordering with a light lightest neutrino). Tested by JUNO (ordering), future cosmology (Σm_ν to ~0.02 eV) and next-generation 0νββ searches.
