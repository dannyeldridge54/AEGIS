# THE WEAVE — names (Danny 11:55 / 11:57, 19 Nov 2026)

Rule: the theory and its structure speak in **weave** words. The individual working parts carry names of sun, time, threshold and weaving figures **from cultures around the world**, one tradition per part, as an homage. SERAPIS (Greco-Egyptian) and JANUS (Roman) keep their places. Several of these figures belong to living traditions (Shinto, Hindu, Yoruba, Māori and others). They are used respectfully, as honours, and never as jokes or mascots.
Names avoided because they are already in use in the repo: Chronos (the Chronos Clock), Hyperion (the other track), Aion, Eos. The twin ladders are named for the pair Mawu–Lisa, never for "Lisa" alone, so they are not confused with the LISA gravitational-wave detector.
