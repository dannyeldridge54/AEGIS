    Audit features in this view

    The report keeps the Gaia source identifier, recorded catalog fields, derived values, scanner rule, source-file timestamps, and small-file SHA-256 provenance together. Candidate rows are explicitly bounded and ordered, can be filtered without changing the saved evidence, and can be exported through the original CSV and JSON links. Missing uncertainty fields stay missing rather than being estimated or replaced with zero.

    Catalog references: Gaia Collaboration et al. (2023), A&A 674, A1 (Gaia DR3); Bailer-Jones et al. (2021), AJ 161, 147 (geometric distances). See the report provenance for local input paths, modification times, and hashes. No measurements are fetched by this page.
