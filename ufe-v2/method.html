The AEGIS Method

How AEGIS keeps itself honest, in code. Every idea we've had is listed here with its fate: what passed, what failed, what couldn't be decided, and what we withdrew. Nothing is deleted.

Two tiers, one wall

Evidence

Tested against data, with a named cause and a rule written down and fingerprinted before the test.

Hypothesis

Ideas and computed consequences still to be decided. Anything new starts here.

The wall is enforced by the code (aegis/method.py), not by good intentions: nothing becomes evidence without a named mechanism, its data and a locked rule. The guardrails check the wall every few minutes.

Checking the wall…

The loop every idea goes through

  1. Observe the data or the question.
  2. A lens proposes a pattern; it's filed as a Hypothesis.
  3. Write it down first: the claim, the test and the pass/fail rule, fingerprinted (SHA-256).
  4. Test with the same data and code as everything else.
  5. Guardrails recompute every number from the saved results.
  6. Review by someone who didn't build the work, then Danny approves.
  7. Publish, losses in the same size print.
  8. Update the map (the Weave), keeping failures visible.
