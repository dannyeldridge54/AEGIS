# Sarah's Sky
A private, offline phone app (PWA) forked from the AEGIS Sky Map. Real computed sky underneath (stars, constellations, planets, Moon), with switchable overlays from world sky-traditions and personal spiritual tools.

Every overlay is labelled **cultural / spiritual tradition — not astronomy**. Nothing leaves the device: no accounts, no tracking. Astronomy is computed (astronomy-engine). Interpretive texts and tarot art are original, not copied.

Built by the loop's SA slot. Status: forked + Janus palette + overlay panel stub; overlays wired in later SA ticks (Western zodiac from real planet positions first). Not shared with Sarah until Danny approves.
