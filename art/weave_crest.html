The Arms of the Weave

Danny Eldridge’s achievement and maker’s mark, with a ring of world symbols around it. Mark hash 8e01c2a2c612.

Hart and arrow. The wounded seeker who keeps walking.
Heart, two hearts, three cords. Family bound to the edge of the field.
Red and blue stars. Danny and his twin: blue for Danny, red for Randy, who loved red.
Ashlar. The squared stone the work stands on.
Hood, hand and mallet. The maker at work, bound to the craft.
Crown. Two rings, the ouroboros and the hourglass: time folding back on itself, with one thread rising through three points.
Chiron and the hart. The wounded healer and the seeker.
Yggdrasil. The world tree behind the arms, shading from blue at the roots to magenta in the crown, its trunk woven with knot-dash; golden apples (Idunn's apples, the fruit that keeps the gods young) hang from the branch tips, and a ring of psilocybin mushrooms, blue-bruised at the stem, grows around the roots, its roots under the mottoes and its branches holding up the crest: the tree Danny sat under when he cried out, and the tree that joins the worlds.
The ring. Themis, Guanyin, Anansi, Pachamama, Saraswati, Airmed, the Celtic knot and the ankh: the traditions the Weave’s names honour.
