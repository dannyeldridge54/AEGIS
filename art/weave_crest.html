The Arms of the Weave

Danny Eldridge’s achievement and maker’s mark, with a ring of world symbols around it. Mark hash 8e01c2a2c612.

Hart and arrow. The wounded seeker who keeps walking.
Heart, two hearts, three cords. Danny, Sarah and Cassandra, bound to the edge of the field.
Red and blue stars. The two knots, ρ and i, where the Weave begins and ends.
Ashlar. The squared stone the work stands on.
Hood, hand and mallet. The maker at work, bound to the craft.
Crown. Two rings, the ouroboros and the hourglass: time folding back on itself, with one thread rising through three points.
Chiron and the hart. The wounded healer and the seeker.
The ring. Themis, Guanyin, Anansi, Pachamama, Saraswati, Airmed, the Celtic knot and the ankh: the traditions the Weave’s names honour.
